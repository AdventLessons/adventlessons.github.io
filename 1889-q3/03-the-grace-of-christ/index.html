Lesson 3 · July 20, 1889

The Grace of Christ

PDFShare
  1. 1.Is it necessary to have a living connection with Christ? John 15:4-6.
  2. 2.How was grace revealed in Christ's first advent? John 1:14.
  3. 3.By what are men saved? Eph. 2:8.
  4. 4.Unto how many has this grace appeared? Titus 2:11.
  5. 5.What does it teach men? Verses 12, 13.
  6. 6.How extensively is this grace bestowed? Ps. 84:11.
  7. 7.What condition of mind must men be in to profit by this grace? James 4:6.
  8. 8.What sustained the apostle Paul in his trials? 2 Cor. 12:9.
  9. 9.What will enable us to serve God acceptably? Heb. 12:28.
  10. 10.How will the grace of God affect the heart? Heb. 13:9.
  11. 11.How did it affect the churches of Macedonia? 2 Cor. 8:1, 2.
  12. 12.What did it do for them in affliction and poverty? Verses 3-5.
  13. 13.Wherein was the church at Corinth inferior to others? 2 Cor. 12:13.
  14. 14.In how many gifts did this church abound? 2 Cor. 8:7.
  15. 15.On what occasion was great grace poured out upon the apostles? Acts 4:32-34.
  16. 16.By what had they been redeemed? 1 Pet. 1:18, 19.
  17. 17.To what does the apostle appeal to show the nature of the grace of giving? 2 Cor. 8:9.
  18. 18.Can any be saved without the manifestation of the fruits of the grace of Christ? Rom. 8:9; 1 John 3:16-18.
  19. 19.From the testimony of this lesson, what will be the effect of grace upon those who receive it? Acts 20:35.
