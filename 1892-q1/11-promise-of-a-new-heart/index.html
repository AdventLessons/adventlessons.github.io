Promise of a New Heart.—Eze. 36:25-28

(Memory Verses, 25-27.)

Golden Text.—"A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you." .

  1. 1.What is said of the moral condition of Israel in the days of the prophet Ezekiel? . See also and elsewhere.
  2. 2.What among many admonitions did God give them? , last part.
  3. 3.How faithfully must the prophet warn them? .
  4. 4.What express duty did he set before them? .
  5. 5.If they would heed these injunctions, what blessings would the Lord bestow upon them? .
  6. 6.If they obeyed the Lord, how did he promise them they would regard their life, which they then loved, and which had brought them into captivity? .
  7. 7.How did the Lord say that Israel would heed these injunctions of the prophet? and why? .
  8. 8.What is the condition of the hearts of all men by nature? ; .
  9. 9.What, therefore, is necessary that they may be brought into union with God? .
  10. 10.Can man accomplish this change of himself? .
  11. 11.Who has promised to give the new heart? .
  12. 12.Through whom alone is this work wrought? ; ; .
  13. 13.By what agency does Christ change the heart and spirit? ; , first clause.
  14. 14.What is the purpose and result of the new heart? ; .
  15. 15.By virtue of what covenant is this change made? .
  16. 16.How many of those know the Lord who are in this covenant relation to the Lord? .
  17. 17.In addition to the new heart and new spirit, what is done for those who by faith embrace these promises? ; .
