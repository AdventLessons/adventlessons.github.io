Lesson 13 · June 27, 1925

The Review

PDFShare

Suggestions for Daily Family Study

  1. 1.Let different members of the family relate the story of healing in Mark 2:1-12. Study Questions 1, 2. Review the memory work belonging to Lesson 1.
  2. 2.Let different members tell the story of the healing by the pool of Bethesda. How did Jesus teach the true principles of Sabbath keeping? (See Lessons 2 and 3.) Review the memory work.
  3. 3.Review the Sermon on the Mount by use of the questions given in the junior review lesson. Review the memory work.
  4. 4.Younger members may relate the miracles recorded in Luke 7:1-17. Review the memory work.
  5. 5.Review the life of John the Baptist, each member contributing a part. Review the memory work.
  6. 6.Review the parable of the sower, all taking part. Review the memory work.
  7. 7.Review the parable of the tares, all taking part. Conduct the exercise in the junior lesson under the heading, "Who Said These Things? To whom Were They Said?"

Questions

  1. 1.On what occasion, and in what manner, did Jesus demonstrate that He had power to forgive sins? Lesson 1.
  2. 2.Relate the circumstances under which Levi was called to the apostleship. Lesson 1.
  3. 3.In what way did the healing of the cripple at the pool of Bethesda arouse the hostility of the Jews? Lesson 2.
  4. 4.On what two occasions did Jesus specially seek to teach the character of true Sabbath keeping? Lesson 3.
  5. 5.What chief purpose did Jesus have in giving the Sermon on the Mount? Lesson 4. Note 1.
  6. 6.Upon what nine principles of Christian living discussed in Lesson 5 did Jesus expand and enlarge in Matt. 5:17-48?
  7. 7.Name the things accompanying right living, concerning which Jesus gave special instruction in Matt. 6:1-8. Lesson 6.
  8. 8.How many illustrations did Jesus employ in Matt. 6:19-34, to show God's love and care for us? Lesson 7.
  9. 9.Name the separate points in Matthew 7, upon which Jesus gave special instruction on Christian living. Lesson 8.
  10. 10.In what positive manner did the centurion show his faith in Jesus' power to heal? Lesson 9.
  11. 11.By what miracle did Jesus reveal His deep sympathy for those who are bowed down with sorrow? Lesson 9.
  12. 12.What evidence of His Messiahship did Jesus give to John the Baptist while he was in prison? Lesson 10.
  13. 13.What important lessons are taught in the parable of the sower? Lesson 11.
  14. 14.What practical instruction is given in connection with the parable of the tares? Lesson 12.

Notes

"It would be well for us to spend a thoughtful hour each day in contemplation of the life of Christ."—"The Desire of Ages," p. 83.

"None but those who have fortified the mind with the truths of the Bible will stand through the last great conflict."—"The Great Controversy," p. 593.
