Lesson 9 · May 28, 1892

Nebuchadnezzar's Dream.—Dan. 2:31-49

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(Memory Verse, 44.)

Golden Text.—"All things are naked and opened unto the eyes of Him with whom we have to do." .

  1. 1.By what means did God trouble the mind of Nebuchadnezzar when he was established in his kingdom? .
  2. 2.What success did he have in learning by his wise men the dream and its interpretation? .
  3. 3.When the news came to Daniel, what did he do? .
  4. 4.Of whom did Daniel seek counsel? and what was the result? ; , last clause.
  5. 5.To whom did Daniel give all the glory? .
  6. 6.For what purpose was the dream given? .
  7. 7.Relate the dream. .
  8. 8.What did Daniel interpret the head of gold to mean? .

Notes

This is an unequivocal starting-point. Nebuchadnezzar, not as a single king, but as representing the Babylonian dynasty, was the "head of gold." This is shown by another "kingdom," not king, following. The word "king" is used as representing kingdom all through the book of Daniel.

  1. 9.What was represented by the breast and arms of silver? , first part; 5:30, 31; 8:20.
  2. 10.What is represented by the belly and thighs of brass? , last part; 8:21.
  3. 11.What did he say of the fourth kingdom? ; see 7:7.
  4. 12.Of what kingdom have we next a record in the word of God? . Cæsar was an emperor of Rome.
  5. 13.What prediction of this power did God give nearly a thousand years before Daniel's time? .
  6. 14.What was indicated by the mixture of iron and clay? , first part.

Notes

It is well to mark that the division of the empire is not indicated by the toes, as stated by some commentators, but by the mixture of iron and clay. As the feet and toes were part of clay and part of iron, so the kingdom was to be divided.

  1. 15.What would be the characteristics of the kingdom? .

Notes

Great strength and great weakness. Strong to resist any other power, as represented by the iron; so weak as never to become united, because of the intermingling of the clay. The iron well represents imperialism, that which seeks to dominate all, the strongest government among men, while clay represents the republican element, weak in cohesive power, tending always to division. These characteristics have been most prominent in European powers.

  1. 16.What effort would these divisions make to unite or amalgamate? .

Notes

This is generally understood to refer to intermarriages among royal families, in order to unite governments; but all efforts of man are futile. One line of God's word is mightier than all the powers of earth.
