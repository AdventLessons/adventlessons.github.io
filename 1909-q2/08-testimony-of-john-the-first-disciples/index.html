Lesson 8 · May 22, 1909

Testimony of John—The First Disciples

PDFShare

Lesson Scripture: John 1:19-51.

Parallel Scripture: John 5:33.

Lesson Helps: "Desire of Ages," chapter 14.

Place: Bethany, or Bethabara, east of the Jordan.

Persons: John the Baptist; priests and Levites of the Pharisees; Jesus, Andrew, Simon, Philip, Nathanael.

Questions

Testimony of John

  1. 1.When leading Pharisees in Jerusalem heard of John's preaching, what deputation did they send? For what purpose? John 1:19, 24.
  2. 2.What reply did John make? Verse 20.
  3. 3.What question did they next ask? What was John's answer? Verse 21. Note 1.
  4. 4.What third question did they ask? What did they finally demand? Verse 22.
  5. 5.Who did John say he was? Verse 23. Note 2.
  6. 6.What further question did the Pharisees ask? Verse 25.
  7. 7.To whom did John bear witness? Verses 26, 27.
  8. 8.Where did these things occur? Verse 28.
  9. 9.How did John bear witness concerning Jesus when he saw Him on the morrow? Verse 29.
  10. 10.In what words did he speak of our Lord's superiority? Verses 30, 31.
  11. 11.What further witness did he bear concerning Him? Verses 32-34.

The First Disciples

  1. 12.How were two of John's disciples directed to Jesus? Verses 35, 36.
  2. 13.What conversation took place between Jesus and these disciples? Verses 37-39.
  3. 14.Who was one of these two disciples? Verse 40.
  4. 15.Where did Andrew begin laboring for souls? With what result? What did Jesus say of Simon? Verses 41, 42.
  5. 16.What disciple did Jesus find the next day? Verse 43.
  6. 17.What did Philip then do? Whom did he say he had found? Verse 45.
  7. 18.What doubting question did Nathanael ask? What was Philip's reply? Verse 46.
  8. 19.What did Jesus say of Nathanael? Verses 47, 48.
  9. 20.What confession was Nathanael led to make? What blessed assurance did our Lord give him? Verses 49-51.

Notes

1. I am not Elijah; that is, the personal Elijah for whom some of the Jews were looking. He was Elijah only in the sense of coming "in the spirit and power" of Elijah. Luke 1:17. To this our Lord refers in Matt. 11:14. "That prophet" doubtless refers to a belief which the Jews held that the prophet of Deut. 18:15, 18 was not the Messiah, but His forerunner. This error was not, however, held by all. Others thought He was one of the old prophets raised from the dead.

2. John counts it the highest honor in simply being a "voice" for God. He is a type of the remnant church. Like John they should not seek earthly glory, but be happy in being simply a "voice" for Him in this generation.
