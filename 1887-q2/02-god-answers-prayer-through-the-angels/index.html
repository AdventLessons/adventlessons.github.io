Lesson 10 · April 9, 1887

God Answers Prayer through the Ministry of the Angels

PDFShare
  1. 1.How does Moses say the Lord answered the prayers of the Israelites when they cried unto him? Num. 20:16.
  2. 2.What did Hezekiah and Isaiah the prophet once do when in great distress? 2 Chron. 32:20.
  3. 3.How did the Lord answer them? Verse 21.
  4. 4.When Peter was about to be killed, what did the church do? Acts 12:5.
  5. 5.What is the promise concerning such prayer? Jas. 5:16.
  6. 6.What was the result in this case? Acts 12:7.
  7. 7.Who sent this angel? Verse 11.
  8. 8.When Paul and his company were in great peril (Acts 27:20), how did God comfort him? Verse 23.
  9. 9.How did Daniel seek God for light and help? Dan. 9:3.
  10. 10.Do many seek God like that now?
  11. 11.Who came to Daniel? Verse 21.
  12. 12.Why did he come? Verse 23, first part.
  13. 13.What did Daniel do on another occasion? Dan. 10:2, 3.
  14. 14.Did God hear his prayer? Verse 12.
  15. 15.Who was sent to answer him? Verse 12.
  16. 16.What do all these cases show as to the means by which God answers the prayers of his people?
  17. 17.Give as many more cases as you can upon this point.
  18. 18.Where is God's throne?
  19. 19.Where is Jesus, our high priest? Heb. 8:1.
  20. 20.What did John see before the throne in heaven? Rev. 8:3, first part.
  21. 21.What did the angel offer before God? Verses 3, 4.
  22. 22.Then who offers our prayers before God in heaven?
