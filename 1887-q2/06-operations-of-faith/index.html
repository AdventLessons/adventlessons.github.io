Lesson 3 · May 7, 1887

Operations of Faith

PDFShare
  1. 1.How is faith obtained? Rom. 10:17; Gal. 5:22.
  2. 2.What did the woman who had been diseased twelve years say within herself when she saw the Saviour? Matt. 9:21.
  3. 3.What effect did this touch have upon her? Mark 5:29.
  4. 4.In what closer contact to the Saviour were others brought at the same time? Luke 8:45.
  5. 5.What was the difference between the press of the multitude and the touch of the woman? Verse 48.
  6. 6.Why was the attention of the Saviour arrested by the woman's simply touching his garment? Mark 5:30.
  7. 7.How did the Saviour's announcement affect the woman? Luke 8:47.
  8. 8.What do these circumstances show?—That outward demonstrations in prayer or otherwise are not necessary to the exercise of faith.
  9. 9.In what manner is it probable that many of the people received a knowledge of the Saviour before they saw him?—Through the preaching of the disciples whom Christ sent out. Matt. 10:2, 23; Luke 10:1.
  10. 10.What was the basis of their faith in his Messiahship?—He fulfilled the prophecies that had been made concerning the Messiah. Matt. 8:16, 17.
  11. 11.What first induced the centurion to send to Jesus in behalf of his servant? Luke 7:3, first clause.
  12. 12.Why did he not go himself? Verse 7.
  13. 13.How does he further show that he realized his unworthiness?—By sending a second deputation. Verse 6.
  14. 14.What unusual faith did the centurion manifest? Verses 7, 8.
  15. 15.What was the Saviour's response? Verse 9.
  16. 16.What was the result of this faith on the centurion's servant? Matt. 8:13.
  17. 17.What did this exhibition of faith bring to the mind of our Saviour? Matt. 8:11, 12.
  18. 18.What expressions in this narrative indicate that this faith brought to the Saviour's mind the gathering in of the Gentiles?

Notes

"Wonderful, indeed, the faith embodied in the message which the centurion sent: I, a Roman officer, have a limited authority, but within its limits this authority is supreme. I can say unto one of my soldiers, Go, and he goeth; to another, Come, and he cometh; to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it. But thou, Jesus, art supreme over all. As my soldiers are under me, so under thee are all the powers and processes of nature. Thou canst say to this disease, Come, and it cometh; to that disease, Go, and it goeth; to thy servants Life and Death, Do this, and they do it. Say thou then but the word, and my servant shall be healed."—Hanna.
