Lesson 3 · June 25, 1887

Importance of Obedience. (Concluded.)

PDFShare
  1. 1.What special message did a prophet of God bear against the altar at Bethel? 1 Kings 13:2.
  2. 2.What most signal manifestation showed that God was with him? Verses 4, 5.
  3. 3.What charge did God give him before he started? Verse 9.
  4. 4.After God had restored the king's hand in answer to the prayer of the man of God, what did the king desire of him? Verse 7.
  5. 5.Did he resist this temptation? Verse 8.
  6. 6.What temptations did an old prophet set before him? Verses 13-15.
  7. 7.How did the man of God still persist in adhering to the command that had been given him? Verses 16, 17.
  8. 8.After he had refused, what testimony did the old prophet bear? Verse 18.
  9. 9.What was the result of this artifice? Verse 19.
  10. 10.What consequence followed this transgression? Verse 24.
  11. 11.What practical lesson can we draw from this?—That when God plainly reveals his will by his word, the slightest disobedience from that word will bring his disapprobation.
  12. 12.Because judgments do not immediately follow transgression, what are people emboldened to do? Eccl. 8:11.
  13. 13.Will their punishment be any less certain? Verse 13.
  14. 14.To whom does the Saviour liken those who hear his sayings and do them? Matt. 7:24, 25.
  15. 15.To whom does he liken those who hear and do them not? Matt. 7:26, 27.

Notes

We are not told who this man of God was, but he had an especial message from God that he should go directly to the altar, bear his testimony, and return by another way. Many are the speculations as to the reasons why this was required. Some have supposed that it was because he would come in contact with men who would make inquiries as to the message he had given, and thus finding those who were in sympathy with the king, his life would be endangered. But one thing is certain: had he not stopped under the oak tree to rest, the old prophet might not have overtaken him. Another important lesson taught by the narrative is that every person whom God uses in his service should have an individual experience, and when God's word reveals to him any duty or truth, he should be faithful in the discharge of the same, without compromising his integrity in any way whatever.
