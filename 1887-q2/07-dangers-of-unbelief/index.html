Lesson 4 · May 14, 1887

Dangers of Unbelief

PDFShare
  1. 1.What kind of men did Moses select to spy out the land of Canaan? Num. 13:2, 3.
  2. 2.What charge did Moses give them? Verses 17-20.
  3. 3.What report did these men bring back? Verses 27-29.
  4. 4.What evidence did they show of the fruitfulness of the land? Verse 23.
  5. 5.Is there any evidence that these men did not give a correct description of the land and of the people?—They greatly exaggerated every discouraging feature. Num. 13:32, 33; Deut. 1:28.
  6. 6.In what did their sin chiefly consist?—Probably in not believing God, who had promised to give them the land.
  7. 7.How was this unbelief more fully developed? Verses 30, 31.
  8. 8.What effect did this have upon the congregation? Num. 14:1-4.
  9. 9.What was the language of faith upon this occasion? Verses 8, 9.
  10. 10.What was the difference between the reasoning of the ten spies and the reasoning of Joshua?—One reasoning was based on what God had said he would do, and the other upon outward appearances.
  11. 11.What did God say to those who disbelieved his word? Verses 28, 29.
  12. 12.What further denunciation did God utter concerning them because of their unbelief? Verses 30-33.
  13. 13.How did God show his approval of Caleb's faith? Verse 24.
  14. 14.What became of the men who brought the evil report? Verse 37.
  15. 15.Why was their sin greater than the sin of the people?—Because of their position, which gave them influence with the people.
  16. 16.Why were these things written? 1 Cor. 10:11, 12.
  17. 17.Are we then in danger of committing the same sin? Heb. 4:11.
