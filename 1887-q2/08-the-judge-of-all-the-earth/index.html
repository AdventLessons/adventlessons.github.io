Lesson 5 · May 21, 1887

The Judge of All the Earth Will Do Right

PDFShare
  1. 1.What did Nebuchadnezzar require of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego? Dan. 3:15.
  2. 2.What expression in their response implies that they were not certain that God would deliver them from the fiery furnace? Verses 17, 18.
  3. 3.What shows that they possessed an uncompromising integrity? Dan. 3:18.
  4. 4.How did God show his approval of this confiding trust in him? Verses 24, 25, 27.
  5. 5.What important lesson of faith is taught by this narrative?—That God is pleased when we trust him without knowing just how he will work deliverance for us.
  6. 6.What message did the prophet Isaiah carry to Hezekiah when he was sick? 2 Kings 20:1.
  7. 7.How was the king affected by it? Verses 2, 3.
  8. 8.In answer to his prayer, what message did Isaiah return to him? Verses 5, 6.
  9. 9.What sign did God give to show that this would be fulfilled? Verses 9-11.
  10. 10.Notwithstanding God heard Hezekiah's prayer, and wrought a miracle as evidence that he should recover, what means did the prophet direct him to use? Verse 7.
  11. 11.What was meant by setting his house in order?
  12. 12.What serious mistake did Hezekiah make after his recovery? Isa. 39:1, 2.
  13. 13.What did the Lord say would happen in consequence of this? Verses 6, 7.
  14. 14.What son was born to him during the added fifteen years of his life? 2 Kings 20:21; 21:1.
  15. 15.What was the character of Manasseh's reign? 2 Kings 21:2-9.
  16. 16.What terrible judgments came upon Judah in consequence of these, and kindred sins?—The final overthrow of the kingdom, and a seventy years' captivity of the people. 2 Kings 21:11-16.
