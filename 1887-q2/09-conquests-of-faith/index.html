Lesson 6 · May 28, 1887

Conquests of Faith

PDFShare
  1. 1.What saved the harlot Rahab in Jericho? Heb. 11:31.
  2. 2.Did works have to do with her salvation and justification? Jas. 2:25.
  3. 3.What brought down the walls of Jericho? Heb. 11:30.
  4. 4.What works were manifested on this occasion? Josh. 6:13-16.
  5. 5.What worthy representatives of faith does Paul mention? Heb. 11:32.
  6. 6.After Gideon's army had been reduced from 32,000 to 300, were works necessary? Judges 7:20, 21.
  7. 7.What has faith oftentimes accomplished? Heb. 11:33, 34.
  8. 8.What has it led people to endure? Verses 35-37.
  9. 9.Did faith bring the fulfillment of the final promise? Verses 39, 40.
  10. 10.In view of the experiences of these worthies, what ought we to do? Heb. 12:1-3.
  11. 11.How may we show that God is pleased with our continued efforts to seek him and exercise faith in him? Luke 18:1-8.
  12. 12.Does faith always bring immediate answers to prayer? Luke 18:7.
  13. 13.How long did Daniel fast and pray on a certain occasion? Dan. 10:2.
  14. 14.When Gabriel appeared, what did he say to Daniel? Verse 12.
  15. 15.Who, besides Gabriel, was brought from heaven to earth by Daniel's prayers? Verse 13.
  16. 16.How long did the prayer of faith once prevent rain? Jas. 5:17.
  17. 17.How many times did Elijah pray, on one occasion, before there was any appearance of rain? 1 Kings 18:43, 44.
  18. 18.What had he previously told Ahab? Verse 41.
  19. 19.Will those who are looking for Christ to come, manifest this persistent faith? Luke 18:8.
  20. 20.What indicates that there will be but a small amount of this faith in the last days? Last clause.
  21. 21.Have you this faith?
