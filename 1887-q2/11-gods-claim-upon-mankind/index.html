Lesson 1 · June 11, 1887

God's Claim upon Mankind

PDFShare
  1. 1.Who is the father of the human family by right of creation? Mal. 2:10, first clause.
  2. 2.In what special way do we become Christ's? 1 Pet. 1:18, 19.
  3. 3.How should our bodies be presented to God? Rom. 12:1.
  4. 4.What special claim does the love of God have upon the human family? 1 Cor. 6:19, 20.
  5. 5.In what special sense does the church become the Lord's? Eph. 1:7.
  6. 6.Is it possible to conceive of a more sacred relation than is expressed in these texts?
  7. 7.In view of these things, what does God require of us? Luke 10:27, 28.
  8. 8.What does God set before his people? Deut. 11:26.
  9. 9.What are the conditions? Verses 27, 28.
  10. 10.What does the apostle say of those who despise the merits of Christ's blood? Heb. 10:29.
  11. 11.When God's people are brought to a test in the matter of obedience, what is his language to them? Josh. 24:15.
  12. 12.Prove from this text that God does not compel men to obey.
  13. 13.Why does the Lord require obedience? Jer. 7:23.
  14. 14.With what spirit must obedience be rendered? Isa. 1:19.
  15. 15.How should man obey the Lord? Rom. 6:17.
  16. 16.When a person possesses this willingness to obey, what will it lead him to do? 2 Cor. 8:3.
  17. 17.What should be the language of every heart? Jer. 42:6.
  18. 18.If God's people would obey his voice, what would be the result? Isa. 48:18.
  19. 19.What important lesson is taught by the above scriptures?
