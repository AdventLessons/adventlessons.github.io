Lesson 11 · April 16, 1887

At the End of the World They Gather the Saints and Destroy the Wicked

PDFShare
  1. 1.When Christ comes the second time, who will come with him? Matt. 16:27.
  2. 2.How many of the angels will come with the Saviour? Matt. 25:31.
  3. 3.What can you say of the glory which will be manifested on this occasion? Matt. 24:30, last part.
  4. 4.Whom will the angels gather at that time? Verse 31.
  5. 5.From whence will they gather them? Same verse.
  6. 6.By whom will the saints be carried to their reward? Matt. 24:31.
  7. 7.How, then, will the saints "be caught up" to meet the Lord? 1 Thess. 4:17.
  8. 8.Who rejoiced when these saints were converted? Luke 15:10.
  9. 9.Who watched over them all through life? Ps. 91:11.
  10. 10.Then will not these angels rejoice with Jesus and the saints when the work is finished and Satan is defeated? Rev. 18:20.
  11. 11.After the resurrection, whom will the saints be like? Luke 20:36.
  12. 12.What will these saints and angels then constitute? Eph. 3:15.
  13. 13.At the end of the world, who will gather out the wicked? Matt. 13:40, 41.
  14. 14.What will they do with them? Verse 42.
  15. 15.How was this same work represented to John? Rev. 14:17.
  16. 16.Who has power over fire? Verse 18, first part.
  17. 17.What did he command to be done? Verse 18.
  18. 18.What was the result? Verse 19.
  19. 19.Where are the wicked finally cast? Rev. 20:15.
  20. 20.What intelligent creatures will then be left alive?—Holy angels and redeemed saints.
  21. 21.How does John describe their condition then? Rev. 5:13.
