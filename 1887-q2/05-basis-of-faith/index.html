Lesson 2 · April 30, 1887

Basis of Faith

PDFShare
  1. 1.What are Paul's words about the impossibility of pleasing God without faith? Heb. 11:6, first clause.
  2. 2.What are the first two essential features of faith? Heb. 11:6, last clause.
  3. 3.What does the apostle say that faith is? Heb. 11:1, margin.
  4. 4.Is faith exercised before the blessing is received, or after? Mark 11:24.
  5. 5.How were the heavens and the earth made? Ps. 33:6-9.
  6. 6.How do we know that this is so?—Because God's word says it is so. Heb. 11:3, first clause.
  7. 7.By what language does the apostle show that faith is not based on what appears? Heb. 11:3, last clause.
  8. 8.What kind of reasonings does faith discard? 2 Cor. 10:5, margin.
  9. 9.What made Abel's offering more acceptable than Cain's?—Abel's offering showed faith in the blood of Christ, to be shed according to God's promise. Heb. 11:4.
  10. 10.What was the difference in the two offerings? Gen. 4:3, 4.
  11. 11.What was the basis of Noah's faith in the coming flood?—Faith in God's word. Gen. 6:13, 14.
  12. 12.How did Noah show that he believed what God had said? Heb. 11:7.
  13. 13.For how long a time was Noah required to exercise this faith? Gen. 6:3.
  14. 14.To what are the days of Noah likened? Matt. 24:37-39.
  15. 15.Were any saved in the ark except those who believed that a flood was coming?
  16. 16.What shall we then conclude respecting those who will be saved when Christ comes? Heb. 9:28.

Notes

To believe the promises of God with all the heart makes them real. This is illustrated by the forming of the worlds by the word of God. We know that they were thus formed because God says so, and the knowledge comes to us through God's word. We credit the testimony, and it becomes to us a reality. It is the same with things not seen, in proportion as we really believe the testimony of God. Happy is the man who can apply to himself the promises without questioning or doubt, because God says so.
