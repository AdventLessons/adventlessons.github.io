Lesson 2 · May 10, 1890

Justification

PDFShare
  1. 1.What is the meaning of the word "justification"? Ans. "A showing to be just, or conformable to law;" making or declaring righteous.
  2. 2.Who are the just before God? Rom. 2:13.
  3. 3.Then how shall we understand Rom. 3:20? Ans. Thus: No flesh can be justified by the deeds of the law, because all have violated it (Rom. 3:19), and it is impossible for any flesh to keep the law. Rom. 8:7, 8.
  4. 4.How, then, can we be made righteous? Rom. 3:25.
  5. 5.In what condition are men before they are justified by grace? Eph. 2:1.
  6. 6.How does God regard sin? Hab. 1:13.
  7. 7.Yet how does he regard men even while they are sinners? Eph. 2:4, 5; John 3:16.
  8. 8.By what is it, then, that we are justified? Titus 3:5-7.
  9. 9.Since there is no good thing in man by nature, what may be said of one who trusts in himself? Prov. 28:26.
  10. 10.For whose benefit did Christ once speak a parable? Luke 18:9.
  11. 11.What two men went into the temple to pray? Verse 10.
  12. 12.How did the Pharisee pray? Verses 11, 12.
  13. 13.What was the prayer of the publican? Verse 13.
  14. 14.What were the two features of his prayer? Ans. Confession of sin, and asking for mercy.
  15. 15.Which of the two went home justified? Verse 14.
  16. 16.If one asks mercy from God, what will he receive? Matt. 7:7-11.
  17. 17.What is mercy? Ans. "Disposition to overlook injuries, or to treat an offender better than he deserves."
  18. 18.How great is God's mercy? Ps. 36:5; 108:4; 103:11.
  19. 19.For whose sake does the Lord pardon sin? Isa. 43:25.
