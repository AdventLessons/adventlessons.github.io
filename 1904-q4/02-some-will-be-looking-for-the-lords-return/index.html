Lesson 2 · October 8, 1904

Some Will Be Looking for the Lord's Return

PDFShare

Questions

  1. 1.For what should God's people be looking? Titus 2:13.
  2. 2.To whom only will the appearing of Christ bring salvation? Heb. 9:28.
  3. 3.What message was sent announcing the deluge? Heb. 11:7; Gen. 6:3.
  4. 4.What warning was sent to Sodom and Gomorrha of their impending overthrow? Gen. 18:17-21; 19:1-3, 12-14.
  5. 5.What warning was sent to Nineveh? Jonah 1:1, 2; 3:4.
  6. 6.What announcement was made of Christ's first advent? Matt. 2:1, 2; Mark 1:1-8.
  7. 7.Will the second coming of Christ be looked for by all? 1 Thess. 5:1, 2.
  8. 8.What will some be saying? Verse 3.
  9. 9.What will be the condition of God's people? Verses 4, 5.
  10. 10.To what have they given heed? 2 Peter 1:16-21.
  11. 11.What message will be given announcing the coming of Christ? Joel 2:1; 2 Peter 3:3, 4. Note 1.
  12. 12.What signs point to this event? Luke 21:7, 25, 26.
  13. 13.What are those who see these signs to know? Verse 31.
  14. 14.What class of people will these warnings and signs develop? What will they exclaim when they see the Lord coming? Isa. 25:9.
  15. 15.What sad words will fall from the lips of the lost? Rev. 6:15-17.
  16. 16.What exhortation is given to those who see the day approaching? Luke 12:33-40.

Notes

The prophecy foretelling the forerunner of Christ was given seven centuries before the coming of Christ. Isa. 40:3. It was not fulfilled till the time came; then John appeared on the scene in fulfilment of the divine prediction. So also in the last days. When the Saviour is about to come the second time the Lord will raise up a people who will fulfil His word. The fact that some are said to scoff at the coming of the Lord is evidence that the subject will be agitated in the last days. Those who oppose it are called "scoffers."
