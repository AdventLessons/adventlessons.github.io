Lesson 13 · December 24, 1904

The Seal of God

PDFShare

Questions

  1. 1.What last-day work takes place in the earth? Rev. 7:1-3.
  2. 2.How many receive this seal? Verse 4.
  3. 3.What becomes of those who are sealed? Rev. 14:1-4. Note 1.
  4. 4.In connection with what is the seal of the living God found? Isa. 8:16.
  5. 5.What other words are used synonymous with seal? Rom. 4:11; Eze. 9:4.
  6. 6.For what is a seal used? What must it contain? Esther 3:12; 1 Kings 21:8. Note 2.
  7. 7.Which commandment of the law contains the seal of God? Ex. 20:8-11. Note 3.
  8. 8.Where is the Sabbath called a seal, or a sign? Ex. 31:13, 17; Eze. 20:12, 20.
  9. 9.Who alone receive the seal of the Lord? Eze. 9:4. What immediately follows? Verses 5, 6. Who are spared?
  10. 10.For what is the "remnant" church especially distinguished? Rev. 12:17; 14:12. Note 4.
  11. 11.When is salvation nigh at hand? Luke 21:28, 31.
  12. 12.At this time what exhortation is given concerning the Sabbath? Isa. 56:1, 2; 58:12-14.
  13. 13.What counterfeit seal will be urged upon God's people at this time? Rev. 13:16, 17. What warning is given? Chap. 14:9-12.
  14. 14.What should be our daily prayer? Ps. 119:33, 34.

Notes

1. The fact that those who are sealed are next seen on Mount Zion, redeemed from "among men," shows that the sealing work takes place just before the end.

2. A seal is that which gives authenticity to all legal documents, and must contain three specifications: (1) The name of the law-making power; (2) the authority for making the law; (3) the territory over which the law-making power has jurisdiction.

3. While other of the commandments contain the name of God, they do not define who He is. There are "gods many and lords many," the true God being defined by the fact that He created the heavens and the earth. Jer. 10:10-12. The fourth commandment contains the seal complete: (1) Name, God; (2) authority, Creator; (3) territory, heavens and earth.

4. The observance of the fourth commandment, or Sabbath, is the only thing in connection with the observance of the law which distinguishes a person at this time as a commandment keeper. This shows that the remnant church will be engaged in a Sabbath reform.
