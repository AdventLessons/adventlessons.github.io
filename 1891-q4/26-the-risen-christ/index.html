Lesson 26 · Dec. 26, 1891

The Risen Christ.—Mark 15:41-47; 16:1-20

PDFShare

Parallels: Matt. 27:57-66; 28; Luke 23:50-56; 24; John 19:38-42; 20; 21.

  1. 1.Who came for the body of Jesus before the Sabbath? Mark 15:43; John 19:38, 39.
  2. 2.What did they do with the body? Mark 15:46, 47; John 19:40, 41. See, also, Matt. 27:60.
  3. 3.What precautions were taken by the Jews? Matt. 27:62-66.
  4. 4.Who came first to the sepulcher? and for what purpose? Mark 16:1, 2.
  5. 5.What question did they ask themselves? Verse 3.
  6. 6.How did they find their question answered? Verse 4; Matt. 28:2-4.
  7. 7.Who did they find at the sepulcher? Mark 16:5; Luke 24:3, 4.
  8. 8.What did the angel say to the women? Luke 24:5-7; Mark 16:6, 7.
  9. 9.What did the women do? Mark 16:8; Matt. 28:8.
  10. 10.What occurred on the way? Matt. 28:9, 10.
  11. 11.What circumstance showed the blind wickedness of the Jews? Matt. 28:11-15.
  12. 12.How did the disciples receive the message? Mark 16:10, 11; Luke 24:10, 11.
  13. 13.To whom did Jesus next appear? Mark 16:12. See, also, Luke 24:13-33.
  14. 14.How did the eleven regard the testimony of these two? Mark 16:13.
  15. 15.Did they believe even after Jesus appeared to them? Luke 24:36-41; Mark 16:14.
  16. 16.What did Jesus say to them when he had convinced them that he had risen? John 20:21-23; Luke 24:44.
  17. 17.What did the Holy Spirit enable them to do? Luke 24:45-48.
  18. 18.When did Jesus next meet with his disciples? John 20:26-29.
  19. 19.On what occasion did Jesus meet with his disciples the third time? John 21.
  20. 20.Where did the next meeting recorded take place? Matt. 28:16, 17.
  21. 21.What instruction did Jesus give his disciples? Verses 19, 20; Mark 16:15.
  22. 22.What did he say would be the effect of their preaching? Mark 16:16. See, also, 2 Cor. 2:15, 16.
  23. 23.What assurance did he give them? Mark 16:17, 18; Matt. 28:20; Luke 24:49.
  24. 24.How able is he to fulfill his promises? Matt. 28:18; Rev. 1:18.
  25. 25.With what power did the disciples go forth after the ascension of Jesus? Mark 16:19, 20. See, also, Acts 4:7, 10; Eph. 1:19, 20.
