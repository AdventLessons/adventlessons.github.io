Lesson 7 · February 12, 1938

Spiritual Gifts (Concluded)

PDFShare

Daily Lesson Study: Place a check mark in the proper space.

Memory verse

Study Help: "Acts of the Apostles," pp. 35-56.

The Lesson

Pentecost and the Gift of Tongues

  1. 1.After giving the disciples their work, what did Jesus say as to where and when they should begin their labors? Luke 24:47-49.

Notes

The disciples were not to enter upon the fuller ministry to which they had been called until they had received the outpouring of the Holy Spirit. The coming of the Holy Ghost in the form of cloven tongues of fire on the day of Pentecost was the signal from heaven for them to go forth in obedience to Christ's command to "go, . . . teach all nations." Obedient to that summons they went forth, humble fishermen made bold, courageous, efficient, by the Spirit's presence in their lives.

  1. 2.What was the condition of their hearts that ultimately made the fulfillment of this promise possible? Acts 1:14; 2:1.

Notes

"The disciples prayed with intense earnestness for a fitness to meet men, and in their daily intercourse to speak words that would lead sinners to Christ. Putting away all differences, all desire for the supremacy, they came close together in Christian fellowship. They drew nearer and nearer to God, and as they did this, they realized what a privilege had been theirs in being permitted to associate so closely with Christ."—"Acts of the Apostles," p. 37.

  1. 3.At what time and under what conditions was the promised outpouring given? Acts 2:1-4.
  2. 4.Who spoke on the day of Pentecost? What spiritual gift was manifested? Acts 2:4-7, 14.
  3. 5.What made necessary the exercise of the gift of tongues on this occasion? Verses 8-11.

Notes

" 'There were dwelling at Jerusalem Jews, devout men, out of every nation under heaven.' During the dispersion, the Jews had been scattered to almost every part of the inhabited world, and in their exile they had learned to speak various languages. Many of these Jews were on this occasion in Jerusalem, attending the religious festivals then in progress. Every known tongue was represented by those assembled. This diversity of languages would have been a great hindrance to the proclamation of the gospel; God therefore in a miraculous manner supplied the deficiency of the apostles. The Holy Spirit did for them that which they could not have accomplished for themselves in a lifetime. They could now proclaim the truths of the gospel abroad, speaking with accuracy the languages of those for whom they were laboring. This miraculous gift was a strong evidence to the world that their commission bore the signet of Heaven. From this time forth the language of the disciples was pure, simple, and accurate, whether they spoke in their native tongue or in a foreign language."—"Acts of the Apostles," pp. 39, 40.

  1. 6.What is the rule given by the apostle Paul for the use of tongues in the church? 1 Cor. 14:26-28.

Notes

On the day of Pentecost the gift of tongues was exercised because it was necessary, and would not have been exercised if no one could have understood them. In the exercise of the true gift of tongues, every man heard in the tongue in which he was born.

  1. 7.For whose sake was the gift of tongues given? 1 Cor. 14:22.

Other Gifts in the Early Church

  1. 8.What gift of the Spirit was manifested in the apostolic church? Acts 15:32; 11:27, 28; 21:8-10.
  2. 9.What instruction concerning the gift of healing has been given the church? James 5:13-16. (Compare with the earlier exercise of this gift in Mark 6:13.)
  3. 10.What gift was exercised by Peter and John? Acts 3:1-11.
  4. 11.What further examples have we of the use of this gift? Acts 5:15, 16; 19:12.
  5. 12.What prophecy of Jesus was fulfilled by the apostles in the exercise of this gift of healing? John 14:12.
  6. 13.What gifts belonging to the church have been counterfeited by Satan? 2 Cor. 11:13-15; Rev. 13:14.

Notes

Satan has counterfeited the gifts of the Spirit in the past, and will continue increasingly to do so in the future. By this means he seeks to confuse minds, and lead men to reject the true gifts of God. Among the instances of counterfeit workings by Satan are those recorded in the following: Matt. 7:15; 1 John 4:1; Rev. 16:14.

  1. 14.How may the genuine gifts be distinguished from the counterfeit? Matt. 7:20; 1 John 4:1, 2; Isa. 8:20.

Notes

No Christian need be deceived by false prophets or spurious miracle workers. God has given infallible rules by which to judge professed servants of His. The fruitage of their lives; their loyalty to the commands of God as given in His word; the measure of their love for, and manifestation of, the Spirit of Jesus, are the factors which will either justify or condemn them.

Lesson Outline

I. Pentecost and the Gift of Tongues

  1. 1.The disciples were to await the outpouring of the Spirit. Luke 24:47-49.
  2. 2.Conversion and unity of heart were first essentials. Acts 1:14; 2:1.
  3. 3.When they were thus prepared, the Spirit appeared as cloven tongues. Acts 2:1-4.
  4. 4.To meet the special need the gift of tongues accompanied Pentecost. Acts 2:8-11.
  5. 5.Tongues were given to edify, and as a sign to unbelievers. 1 Cor. 14:22, 28.

II. Other Gifts in the Early Church

  1. 1.Prophecy was often manifested. Acts 15:32; 11:27, 28; 21:8-10.
  2. 2.Definite instruction is given the church today. James 5:13-16.
  3. 3.Peter and John were given the gift of healing. Acts 3:1-11.
  4. 4.Other apostles also were given this power. Acts 5:15, 16; 19:12.
  5. 5.Satan has counterfeited and will continue to counterfeit spiritual gifts. 2 Cor. 11:13-15; Rev. 13:14.
  6. 6.None need be deceived. The mark of the true. Matt. 7:20; Isa. 8:20.
