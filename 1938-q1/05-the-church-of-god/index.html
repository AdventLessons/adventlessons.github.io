Lesson 5 · January 29, 1938

The Church of God

PDFShare

Daily Lesson Study: Place a check mark in the proper space.

Memory Verse: "Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints, and of the household of God; and are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ Himself being the chief cornerstone." Eph. 2:19, 20.

Study Help: "Acts of the Apostles," pp. 9-16; "The Desire of Ages," pp. 105, 106.

The Lesson

The Church

  1. 1.What are some of the terms used in the Scriptures to represent the church? Ans. The body (Col. 1:18); family (Eph. 3:14, 15); pillar and ground of the truth (1 Tim. 3:15); holy temple (Eph. 2:21).
  2. 2.How many churches, or bodies, does God have? Rom. 12:5; 1 Cor. 12:13.
  3. 3.Who is the head of the church? Col. 1:18; Eph. 1:22.
  4. 4.What term does the martyr Stephen use in speaking of the Exodus movement? Acts 7:38.

Christ the True Foundation

  1. 5.Who is the chief foundation stone of the church? 1 Cor. 3:11; 1 Peter 2:5-9.

Notes

Christ is the chief foundation stone of the church of God. On Him and in Him the church is built,—apostles, prophets, and the assembly of the saints. No other foundation than Christ is possible to the true church, the Israel of God.

  1. 6.Upon what rock was the church in the wilderness founded? 1 Cor. 10:4; Deut. 32:4.

The Sign of God's True Church

  1. 7.What is the law of the church? Ans. The Decalogue. Ex. 19:5, 6; Ezek. 20:10, 11. (Compare Romans 3:31 and James 2:10-12.)

Notes

The Decalogue, the Ten Commandments, is the fundamental law of the church of God. It presents the chief ruler—God, the Creator of heaven and earth. It states our duty to Him, and it lays down the fundamental principles of the relationship between members of His family.

  1. 8.What is God's mark of identification between Himself and His people? Ex. 31:13-17; Ezek. 20:12.
  2. 9.What was the attitude of Christ to the law and the Sabbath? Matt. 5:17, 18; Mark 2:28.

The Condition of Membership

  1. 10.Through what service must one enter the church? 1 Cor. 12:13; John 3:5; Gal. 3:27.
  2. 11.What covenant does the Lord make with His people? Heb. 8:10.

Notes

"The same law that was engraved upon the tables of stone is written by the Holy Spirit upon the tables of the heart. Instead of going about to establish our own righteousness, we accept the righteousness of Christ. His blood atones for our sins. His obedience is accepted for us. Then the heart renewed by the Holy Spirit will bring forth 'the fruits of the Spirit.' Through the grace of Christ we shall live in obedience to the law of God written upon our hearts. Having the Spirit of Christ, we shall walk even as He walked. Through the prophet He declared of Himself, 'I delight to do Thy will, O My God; yea, Thy law is within My heart.' And when among men He said, 'The Father hath not left Me alone; for I do always those things that please Him.' "—"Patriarchs and Prophets," p. 372.

  1. 12.What provisions did God make for the organized working of the church on earth? 1 Cor. 12:28; Eph. 4:11-13.

The Remnant True to God

  1. 13.What promise is made concerning the remnant? Rom. 9:27, 28.
  2. 14.What are the characteristics of the "remnant" of the church? Rev. 14:12; 12:17.

Notes

A remnant is a part of and exactly like the whole. The remnant church will measure up to the law of God, which is the law of God's church, just as the saints have ever been obedient to His will. They will keep the commandments of God, and hold to the faith of Jesus. In true loyalty to the principles of the kingdom they will abide to the end.

  1. 15.Eventually what will be the home of the church? John 14:1-3; Rev. 21:1-3.

Lesson Outline

I. The Family of God

  1. 1.There is but one church of God. 1 Cor. 12:13.
  2. 2.Christ is its head. Col. 1:18; Eph. 1:22.

II. Christ the True Foundation

  1. 1.Christ is the chief foundation stone. 1 Cor. 3:11; 1 Peter 2:5-9.
  2. 2.The church in the wilderness was also founded on Christ. 1 Cor. 10:4; Deut. 32:4.

III. The Sign of God's True Church

  1. 1.The law of the church is the Decalogue. Ex. 19:5, 6; Ezek. 20:10, 11.
  2. 2.The Sabbath is its sign. Ex. 31:13-17; Ezek. 20:12.
  3. 3.Christ, apostles, and prophets so recognized it. Matt. 5:17, 18; Mark 2:28.

IV. The Condition of Membership

  1. 1.Baptism is the gate of the church. 1 Cor. 12:13.
  2. 2.The church covenant. Heb. 8:10.

V. The Remnant True to God

  1. 1.The remnant church will be faithful. Rev. 14:12; 12:17.
  2. 2.The holy city to be the future home of the church. John 14:1-3; Rev. 21:1-3.
