Lesson 13 · March 28, 1925

The Review

PDFShare

Memory Texts: Review the memory texts connected with each lesson.

Questions

  1. 1.State the circumstances surrounding the birth of John the Baptist, the prophetic nature of his message, and his manner of life. Lesson 1.
  2. 2.Relate the story of the birth of Jesus. What two names are given to the Son of God in the record? What do they each signify? Lesson 2.
  3. 3.How were the shepherds enabled to find the place where Jesus was born? Give a brief account of His dedication. Lesson 3.
  4. 4.Who were the wise men that visited the infant Saviour? What was the star that guided them? What evil purpose was in Herod's heart concerning Christ? Where did Jesus find refuge from his wrath? Lesson 4.
  5. 5.How and by whom was the way prepared for the ministry of our Lord? What was the burden of John's message? To the work of what prophet was John's ministry likened? Of what other preparatory message was his work a type? Lesson 5.
  6. 6.What nature had Christ when He was tempted of the devil in the wilderness? How was this made evident? Why did God permit this experience to come to His Son? By what means was He enabled to overcome these temptations? Lesson 6.
  7. 7.On what occasion did Jesus perform His first miracle? Tell the story briefly. Describe the condition in which He found the temple during the Passover, and the manner in which He cleansed it. Lesson 7.
  8. 8.What startling revelation did Jesus make to a certain Pharisee who came to Him with questions? What illustrations did Jesus use to show the nature of His own mission to earth? Lesson 8.
  9. 9.What brought about sharp religious and national controversy between the Jews and the Samaritans? Relate briefly the conversation between Jesus and the Samaritan woman at Jacob's well. What was the outcome of this talk? Lesson 9.
  10. 10.What request was made by a certain nobleman of Capernaum? What was the nature of his faith? What occurred in the synagogue at Nazareth one Sabbath after Jesus had given His message? Lesson 10.
  11. 11.On one occasion at Capernaum, over what supernatural powers did Jesus exercise His authority? What incident in the home of Simon demonstrated His power over disease? What was the effect of these two miracles on the people? Lesson 11.
  12. 12.What was the chief purpose of the miraculous draught of fishes? How did it affect Simon Peter? What did Jesus say He would make of Peter? Why was leprosy regarded as a type of sin? How did Jesus respond to the cry of every leper for healing? Lesson 12.
