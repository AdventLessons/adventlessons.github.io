Lesson Scripture: Acts 8:4 to 14:28.

Review memory verses for the quarter.

Chapter Outlines

Chapter 8: Dispersion of the believers from Jerusalem; work of Philip the evangelist.

  1. 1.How is Saul introduced into apostolic history?
  2. 2.Consider the relation of persecution to the spread of the gospel.
  3. 3.Sketch the work of Philip.

Notes

Chapter 9: Saul's conversion and preaching; Peter at Lydda; Peter at Joppa.

  1. 1.Relate briefly the facts of Saul's conversion and preaching till he returned to Tarsus.
  2. 2.What miracle did Peter perform at Lydda?
  3. 3.Tell briefly Peter's experience in Joppa.

Notes

Chapter 10: Visions of Cornelius and Peter; Peter visits Cornelius; Peter preaches at Cornelius's house.

  1. 1.Relate the visions of Cornelius and Peter.
  2. 2.Describe the meeting of Peter and Cornelius.
  3. 3.What was the result of Peter's preaching?

Notes

Chapter 11: Peter reports his experience to the church at Jerusalem; Barnabas and Saul at Antioch.

  1. 1.Give the substance of Peter's report of his work among the gentiles. What effect did this have upon the believers?
  2. 2.What took Barnabas and Saul to Antioch, then back to Judea?

Notes

Chapter 12: Persecution by Herod; Peter's deliverance from prison; death of Herod.

  1. 1.Why was Peter cast into prison? How was he delivered?
  2. 2.Give the circumstances and manner of Herod's death.

Notes

Chapter 13: Barnabas and Saul set apart to the ministry; their first missionary tour.

  1. 1.Where and how were Barnabas and Saul ordained?
  2. 2.Sketch the experiences of the apostles in Cyprus, and at Antioch of Pisidia.
  3. 3.Give the circumstances and declaration of Paul in turning to the gentiles.

Notes

Chapter 14: The apostles at Iconium and Lystra; establishing the believers; conclusion of the first missionary tour.

  1. 1.Give a brief account of the apostles' experiences at Iconium and Lystra.
  2. 2.How did they seek to establish the believers?
  3. 3.What report did they make to the church at Antioch in Syria?
