Title
The Mediation of Christ — Second Quarter, 1913
Thirteenth Sabbath Offering

THE THIRTEENTH SABBATH OFFERING

June 28 is the time for the next thirteenth Sabbath offering. The General Conference Committee has requested that this offering be used for sanitarium work in India. Remember that in India 300,000,000 souls, about one fifth of the world's population, are sitting in the darkness of heathenism. This call is a most urgent one, and our response should be liberal.

Special Offering, June 28, 1913, for sanitarium work in India. Remember India's 300,000,000 souls.
