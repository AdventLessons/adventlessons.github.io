Judging; Asking; Walking

Lesson Scripture: Matt. 7:1-14.

Questions

  1. 1.Why are we not to judge? Matt. 7:1.
  2. 2.On what basis shall we be judged? Verse 2.
  3. 3.What pointed question on judging is asked? Verse 3.
  4. 4.What inconsistent course do we often take toward a brother? Verse 4.
  5. 5.How are we counseled to correct this course? Verse 5, first part.
  6. 6.What can we then do? Verse 5, last part.
  7. 7.What are we further urged not to do? Verse 6. What reason is given?
  8. 8.What promise is made to those who ask? Verse 7. To those who seek? Who knock?
  9. 9.Who is said to receive? Verse 8. To find? To whom shall the door be opened?
  10. 10.What human experience is cited for illustration? Verses 9, 10. Note 1.
  11. 11.What conclusion is drawn from this parallel? Verse 11.
  12. 12.What golden rule is then laid down for us to follow? Verse 12.
  13. 13.Under what figure is the Christian walk presented? Verse 13.
  14. 14.Why are we counseled to enter in at the strait gate? Verse 13.
  15. 15.What is said of the number who go in at the wide gate? Verse 13, last part.
  16. 16.Where does the narrow way lead? Verse 14, first part.
  17. 17.What is said of the number who find the narrow way? Verse 14, last part. Note 2.

Notes

1. It is characteristic of Jesus' teaching to draw upon the things of common experience to illustrate spiritual truth. Here He chooses the tenderest of human relations — that of parent and child; and selects a touching incident in this relation — a hungry child asking for something to eat. Parents will and must refuse some things asked by children, but they do not refuse food to children when they are hungry. So our heavenly Father responds to the cry of him who hungers and thirsts after righteousness.

2. We perhaps oftenest, in our thought of this illustration of the broad way and the narrow way, apply the lesson to the doctrinal side of religious confession. Certainly it is important to follow true doctrine; and the many are prone to go the broad road of popularity, disregarding the claims of God's law and the message of truth for these last days. But our Saviour uses the illustration in this connection to teach those who are in the right way doctrinally to be thoroughly in earnest to walk the way of genuine Christian experience, not judging one another, but loving and serving, and seeking the Lord for the life of Christ to be revealed in them day by day. Thus only can we keep in the narrow way that leads to life eternal.
