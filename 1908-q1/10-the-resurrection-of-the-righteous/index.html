Lesson 10 · March 7, 1908

The Resurrection of the Righteous

PDFShare

Questions

  1. 1.What question was asked long ago concerning the dead? Job 14:14.
  2. 2.What answer does the Life-giver make to this question? John 11:25.
  3. 3.How had this same question been answered centuries before the first advent of our Lord? Isa. 26:19.
  4. 4.What is death called? John 11:11-13; Job 14:12.
  5. 5.When will the dead be waked out of sleep? Job 14:12-15.
  6. 6.What promise of a resurrection did the Lord make to His people through Ezekiel? Eze. 37:12.
  7. 7.Who will call for the dead to come forth? John 5:25.
  8. 8.When are the righteous to receive their recompense? Luke 14:14.
  9. 9.How many resurrections will there be? Acts 24:14, 15; Rev. 20:5.
  10. 10.In what graphic manner does the apostle describe the resurrection of God's people? 1 Cor. 15:51, 52.
  11. 11.In that glad day, what shouts of victory will be heard from immortal voices? Verses 54, 55.
  12. 12.When will the righteous be raised? 1 Thess. 4:15-17. Who will be caught up with them to meet the Lord?
  13. 13.What are we bidden to do with these words? Verse 18.
  14. 14.Who will gather the saints? Matt. 24:30, 31.
  15. 15.What power is the believer to know now? Phil. 3:10, 11; Rom. 8:11.
