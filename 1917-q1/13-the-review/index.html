Lesson 13 · March 31, 1917

The Review

Lesson Scripture: Acts 15:1 to 21:17.

Memory Verse: Review the memory verses for the quarter.

The questions here asked on each chapter are for the student's personal study. The teacher will naturally select the main points for class recitation.

Chapter 15

  1. 1.At what place were Paul and Silas at the beginning of this quarter's lessons?
  2. 2.What question was disturbing the church?
  3. 3.Who were present at this council?
  4. 4.How was the decision of the council sent to the churches?
  5. 5.Where did Paul go on leaving Antioch?
  6. 6.What contention arose?
  7. 7.Who accompanied Paul on his second missionary tour?

Chapter 16

  1. 1.What places are mentioned as being visited by the apostles?
  2. 2.How is the guiding presence of the Holy Spirit indicated?
  3. 3.What means did the enemy use to hinder the work of the apostles in Macedonia?
  4. 4.What happened to Paul and Silas?
  5. 5.Describe the deliverance which came to them.
  6. 6.From here, where did the apostles go?

Chapter 17

  1. 1.What Macedonian seaport was visited?
  2. 2.What was the result of the preaching of the Word?
  3. 3.What important city did Paul next visit?
  4. 4.In what way did Paul make the true God known to them?
  5. 5.What was the attitude of the philosophers toward the doctrine set forth by Paul?
  6. 6.Who is mentioned as being converted?

Chapter 18

  1. 1.Where did Paul go from Athens?
  2. 2.Whom did he find here with whom he abode?
  3. 3.How was Paul's testimony received at this place?
  4. 4.Before whom was he brought?
  5. 5.From what place did Paul begin his third missionary tour?
  6. 6.What man of note came to Ephesus about the time Paul was there?

Chapter 19

  1. 1.What disciples did Paul find at Ephesus?
  2. 2.Upon what point did he give them special instruction?
  3. 3.How long did Paul remain in Ephesus at this time?
  4. 4.What noted place did Paul now intend to visit?
  5. 5.What trouble was instigated by Demetrius?

Chapter 20

  1. 1.What region did Paul visit on leaving Corinth?
  2. 2.Describe the farewell meeting at Troas.
  3. 3.Relate the solemn warning given by Paul to the Ephesian elders.
  4. 4.Describe the parting scene.

Chapter 21:1-17

  1. 1.What places did Paul visit on his way from Miletus to Jerusalem?
  2. 2.How long did he remain at each place?
  3. 3.On his arrival at Jerusalem, where did Paul abide?
  4. 4.How was he received?
