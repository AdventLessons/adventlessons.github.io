Lesson 3 · October 19, 1907

The Sure Word of Prophecy—The Great Prophetic Image

PDFShare

Questions.

  1. 1.What great outline of the world's history was given to Nebuchadnezzar in a dream? Dan. 2:31-35.
  2. 2.After describing to the king the form and parts of this wonderful image, what does the prophet say he will do? Verse 36.
  3. 3.What was represented by the head of gold? Verses 37, 38.
  4. 4.What is said of the second kingdom? Verse 39.
  5. 5.What is predicted of the third kingdom and its extensive dominion? Verse 39, last part.
  6. 6.What is the interpretation given of the fourth and last of these kingdoms? Verse 40.
  7. 7.Of what were the feet composed? What did this symbolize? Verses 41-43.
  8. 8.What became of the image? Verses 34, 35.
  9. 9.What glorious kingdom is to take the place occupied by these earthly kingdoms? How strong will it become? How long shall it endure? Verse 44.
  10. 10.What promise did the Saviour once make to some of His disciples? Mark 9:1.
  11. 11.How did He illustrate His meaning, and show to them the nature and glory of His kingdom? Verse 2.
  12. 12.In the transfiguration, what is said of the glory? Verse 3.
  13. 13.Who from heaven conversed with Jesus? Verse 4.
  14. 14.What does the narrative say of a bright cloud and a voice speaking out of the cloud to the disciples? Matt. 17:5.
  15. 15.What does Peter, an eye-witness of this scene, say concerning the assurance thus given of the Lord's coming? 2 Peter 1:16-18.
  16. 16.What does Peter say is surer than eyesight? Verse 19. Note.

Notes

Our eyes may deceive us, and our ears may fail to correctly understand, but the prophetic word is infallible and will surely be fulfilled. It is more certain than the seeing of the eyes, or the hearing of the ears, for it can not fail. Prophecy shines into a dark place, throwing light upon the future.
