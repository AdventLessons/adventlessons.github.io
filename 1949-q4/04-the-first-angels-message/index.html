Lesson 4 · October 22, 1949

The First Angel's Message

PDFShare

Lesson Scripture: Rev. 10:1-11.

Memory Verse: "In the days of the voice of the seventh angel, when he shall begin to sound, the mystery of God should be finished, as He hath declared to His servants the prophets." Rev. 10:7.

Study Helps: "The Prophecies of Daniel and the Revelation," pages 519-529; "Beacon Lights of Prophecy," pages 261-267; "The Story of the Seer of Patmos," pages 180-190.

Aim: To understand more fully the secret of the "little book," now open, and the mystery of redemption which is to be finished under the sounding of the seventh trumpet.

The Lesson

A Parenthetical Prophecy

  1. 1.Between the sounding of the sixth and seventh trumpets, what did the seer of Patmos behold? Rev. 10:1, first part.

Notes

Since the period of the sixth trumpet closed in 1840, and the sounding of the seventh trumpet began in 1844, the message of Revelation 10 should find its fulfillment just prior to that event.

  1. 2.Describe the appearance of the celestial messenger. Rev. 10:1, last part.

An Open Book With a Universal Message

  1. 3.What was held in the hand of the angel? Rev. 10:2, first part.
  2. 4.What book of the Old Testament Scriptures was closed and sealed? Until what time? Dan. 12:4.

Notes

"There is no book spoken of as closed and sealed except the book of Daniel's prophecy, and there is no account of the opening of that book unless it be here in Revelation 10."—The Prophecies of Daniel and the Revelation, pages 519, 520.

  1. 5.Where did the angel place his feet? Rev. 10:2, last part.
  2. 6.In what forceful manner did he present his message? Rev. 10:3, first part.
  3. 7.What part of the vision was John restrained from writing? Rev. 10:3, 4.

Forecast of Momentous Events

  1. 8.In whose name did the angel testify? What vital announcement did he make? Rev. 10:5, 6.

Notes

The ending of the 2300-year prophecy in 1844 marks the ending of prophetic time. No time prophecies are to be found in the Scriptures that extend beyond that date.

  1. 9.Under the sounding of the seventh trumpet what mighty task is to be finished? Rev. 10:7.

Notes

The "mystery of God" is the gospel. Eph. 3:3-6; Col. 1:26, 27. As the seventh trumpet began to sound, our great High Priest commenced His final service in the heavenly sanctuary.

Eating the Little Book

  1. 10.What was the prophet instructed to do? Rev. 10:8.
  2. 11.What further charge was given by the angel? Rev. 10:9, Compare Jer. 15:16.
  3. 12.According to the prophetic outline what grievous effect would be produced by eating the little book? Rev. 10:10.
  4. 13.In the experience of the believers during the year 1844 how was the prediction fulfilled? Ans. "The disciples, weeping at the tomb over a crucified Saviour, seemed to drain the cup of bitterness, but a potion no less galling was drunk by the disciples in 1844. 'We thought that it was He that would save Israel,' was echoed eighteen hundred years later in the words, 'We looked for Him to save us, but He has not come.' "—The Story of the Seer of Patmos, page 189.

Notes

"Their mistake was in their misunderstanding of the event to take place at the end of this prophetic period, and not in their reckoning of the time."—The Prophecies of Daniel and the Revelation, page 528.

A World-Wide Commission

  1. 14.Following the bitter disappointment what further task was _assigned to God's people? Rev. 10:11.
