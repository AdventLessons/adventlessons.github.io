Lesson 12 · September 21, 1901

The Coming of Christ

PDFShare

Its Results to the Righteous and the Wicked

  1. 1.At what time are God's people delivered? 1 Thess. 4:16, 17.

Notes

Side texts: 1 Cor. 15:51, 52.

  1. 2.What is caused by the voice of God? Joel 3:16; Heb. 12:25-27. "Early Writings," page 33.

Notes

Side texts: Matt. 24:29; Isa. 2:19, 21.

  1. 3.In what words do God's people then express their faith? Ps. 46:1-3, 7-11.
  2. 4.What other event takes place at the same time? Dan. 12:1, 2; Rev. 1:7. Note 1.

Notes

Side texts: Luke 21:27-30; Matt. 26:64.

  1. 5.What follows the shaking of the powers of heaven? Matt. 24:30, 31.
  2. 6.How do the saints welcome their Saviour? Isa. 25:9.
  3. 7.What will be the result of His coming to them? 1 Thess. 4:16-18. Note 2.

Notes

Side texts: Phil. 3:20, 21; 1 John 3:2; 1 Cor. 15:51, 52; Matt. 13:43.

  1. 8.With what feelings will the Saviour and the saints view the results of redemption? Isa. 53:11; Ps. 17:15.

Notes

Side texts: Jude 24; Matt. 25:21; Isa. 65:14, 19; Ps. 91:16.

  1. 9.What will be the experience of the wicked at the coming of Christ? Rev. 6:14-17; Isa. 2:19-21.

Notes

Side texts: Zeph. 1:14-18; Jer. 25:34; Isa. 13:6.

  1. 10.What will be the result of His coming to them? Isa. 13:9.

Notes

Side texts: Jer. 25:33; Zeph. 1:2, 3.

  1. 11.What effect will the coming of the Lord have upon the earth? Jer. 4:23-27.

Notes

Side texts: Isa. 24:1, 3-6; Rev. 20:11.

  1. 12.What other great event takes place in connection with the coming of the Lord? Rev. 20:1-4. Note 3.
  2. 13.What in the type foreshadowed this? Lev. 16:20-22; "Great Controversy," p. 659.
  3. 14.Where are the righteous during the thousand years? Rev. 19:1; 20:4.

Notes

Read "Great Controversy," chapters 40, 41.

1. The voice of God at this time evidently causes the partial resurrection spoken of in these verses. The side texts given with the others show positively that those who took part in His crucifixion will see Him when He comes. Rev. 14:13 would indicate that those who had fallen asleep under the third angel's message would be raised at that time, for all are rewarded according to their works. "All those who have died in the faith of the third angel's message come forth from the tomb glorified, to hear God's covenant of peace with those who have kept His law."—Great Controversy, p. 637. See "Early Writings" ("Spiritual Gifts"), p. 145.

2. "At the voice of God they [the living saints] were glorified; now they are made immortal, and with the risen saints are caught up to meet their Lord in the air."—Great Controversy, p. 645.

3. Notice that in Rev. 20:4, 5, it says the saints "lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years. But the rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished." Then, at the beginning of the thousand years, the first resurrection takes place, which event we have found to occur at the second coming of Christ. 1 Thess. 4:16. Hence the binding of Satan takes place at the same time, and is caused by the saints being taken to heaven, and the destruction of the wicked. The earth reduced to a chaotic state may fitly be termed the bottomless pit, where Satan is cast or confined during the thousand years.
