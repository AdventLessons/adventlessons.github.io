Lesson 4 · January 26, 1907

The First Protest Against the Work

PDFShare

Questions

  1. 1.Who heard that the temple was being built? Ezra 4:1.
  2. 2.What request did they make? Verse 2.
  3. 3.Give Zerubbabel's answer. Verse 3.
  4. 4.What was the character of these people that wished to help build the house of the Lord? 2 Kings 17:33, 34. Note 1.
  5. 5.In what way did these Samaritans show their true character? Ezra 4:4, 5.
  6. 6.How long did they keep up this warfare of spite? Verse 5.
  7. 7.What did they finally do? Verse 6.
  8. 8.To whom did they next appeal? Verse 7. Note 2.
  9. 9.Who were the leading spirits in writing the letter? Verses 7, 8.
  10. 10.How many were represented in the letter? Verses 9, 10.
  11. 11.What did they write in regard to the Jews and Jerusalem? Verses 11-13.
  12. 12.What reason did they give for sending the letter? Verse 14 (margin).
  13. 13.What request did they make? Verse 15.
  14. 14.Give the first part of the king's letter. Verses 17-19.
  15. 15.What did Artaxerxes say about the former kings of Jerusalem? Verse 20.
  16. 16.Give the decree of the king? Verses 21, 22.
  17. 17.When the Samaritans received the letter, what did they do? Verse 23.
  18. 18.How long was the work stopped? Verse 24.

Notes

1. The Samaritans did not love nor fear the Lord; they wished to help build because they thought if Jerusalem was rebuilt it would be a wise policy for them to have a part in it. Such helpers hinder the Lord, and Zerubbabel showed great spiritual discernment in refusing them. 2 Kings 17:24-34 gives the origin of the Samaritans.

2. From the Bible record we have the names of Ahasuerus and Artaxerxes, kings reigning between the time of Cyrus and Darius the Persian. According to profane history Cambyses and Smerdis the Impostor reigned during this time, hence it has been thought probable that these names refer to the same kings.
