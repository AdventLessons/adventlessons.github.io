Lesson 4 · January 22, 1927

Peter Tells His Experience; the Gospel Reaches Antioch

PDFShare

Lesson Scripture: Acts 11.

Memory verse

Lesson Help: "The Acts of the Apostles," pp. 155-160.

Setting of the Lesson

A stir among the believers in Judea. Strange reports had reached their ears concerning the conduct of Peter. They had heard that Peter had actually been preaching the gospel to gentiles. The prejudice of the Jewish believers against the idea of gentile acceptance of the gospel led to the rehearsal of the experiences of Peter in detail, when he returned to Jerusalem.

The center of activity in spreading the gospel shifts in this lesson from Jerusalem to Antioch, capital of Syria. It was an important city commercially, "the common mart where East and West met," sixteen miles from the Mediterranean Sea, and three hundred north of Jerusalem.

Suggestions for Daily Family Study

  1. 1.Read the "Setting of the Lesson" and the Lesson Scripture. Drill on the memory verse each day.
  2. 2.Study Questions 1-4, and note. Children may read paragraphs 1-3 of the primary lesson.
  3. 3.Locate Jerusalem and Caesarea on the map. Study Questions 5-9, and notes. Teach to the children the salient points in the note following Question 9.
  4. 4.Study Questions 10-15, and note. Children may read paragraphs 4-7 of the primary lesson. Read pages 155 and 156 of the Lesson Help.
  5. 5.Study Questions 16-20, and note. Children may read paragraphs 8-10 of the primary lesson. Read pages 157-160 of the Lesson Help.
  6. 6.Find on the map the countries and cities mentioned in the lesson. Relate what occurred at each place. Children may answer questions in the primary lesson.
  7. 7.Review the lesson by asking questions from senior, youth's, junior, or primary lesson. Read notes in the "Youth's Instructor," if the paper is available. Discuss questions under "Personal Reflections."

The Lesson

  1. 1.What news reached the ears of the apostles and brethren in Judea? Acts 11:1.
  2. 2.When Peter arrived at Jerusalem, how was he received by the brethren? Verses 2, 3.

Notes

"When the brethren in Judea heard that Peter had preached to the gentiles, and had met with them, and eaten with them in their houses, they were surprised and offended by such strange movements on his part. They feared that such a course, which looked presumptuous to them, would tend to contradict his own teachings. As soon as Peter visited them, they met him with severe censure, saying, 'Thou wentest in to men uncircumcised, and didst eat with them.' "—"Spirit of Prophecy," vol. 3, pp. 332, 333.

  1. 3.How did Peter reply to their contention? Verse 4.
  2. 4.What was the substance of Peter's report to the brethren at Jerusalem? Verses 5-14.
  3. 5.What divine power did Peter tell them accompanied his sermon in the house of Cornelius? Verse 15.
  4. 6.Of what saying of Jesus did Peter say this experience at Cæsarea had reminded him? Verse 16.
  5. 7.What appeal did he then make to his brethren? Verse 17.
  6. 8.What great truth now, for the first time, dawned upon their minds? Verse 18.

Notes

"The disciples, upon hearing this account, were silenced, and convinced that Peter's course was in direct fulfillment of the plan of God, and that their old prejudices and exclusiveness were to be utterly destroyed by the gospel of Christ."—Id., pp. 333, 334.

  1. 9.In the first gospel commission, where were the apostles commanded to go? Where were they not to go? Matt. 10:5, 6.

Notes

In Daniel's prophecy (Dan. 9:24), 490 years of the great prophetic period were allotted to the Jews. This period, beginning in the autumn of B. C. 457, must terminate in the autumn of A. D. 34. The gospel call was therefore limited to the Jews until that date. Paul and Barnabas, speaking to the Jews, said (Acts 13:46): "It was necessary that the word of God should first have been spoken to you; but seeing ye put it from you, and judge yourselves unworthy of everlasting life, lo, we turn to the gentiles." In that very year, A. D. 34, was the great persecution in which Stephen, the first Christian martyr, was put to death.

  1. 10.Following the death of Stephen, what marked change took place in the gospel program? Acts 8:4, 5.
  2. 11.When the church was thus scattered abroad, to whom did they preach the gospel? Acts 11:19, 20.
  3. 12.What success attended their ministry of the Word? Verse 21.

Notes

"After the disciples had been driven from Jerusalem by persecution, the gospel message spread rapidly through the regions lying beyond the limits of Palestine; and many small companies of believers were formed in important centers. Some of the disciples 'traveled as far as Phenice, and Cyprus, and Antioch, preaching the Word.' Their labors were usually confined to the Hebrew and Greek Jews, large colonies of whom were at this time to be found in nearly all the cities of the world."—"The Acts of the Apostles," p. 155.

  1. 13.Whom did the church at Jerusalem send to build up the work in these newly opened centers of light? Verse 22.
  2. 14.How did Barnabas exhort the new converts? Verse 23.
  3. 15.What character does Inspiration ascribe to Barnabas? Verse 24.
  4. 16.Whom did Barnabas seek as a colaborer? Verse 25.

Notes

Barnabas now hastened to find Saul and brought him to Antioch, at which place their united labors in the gospel began. "The labors of Barnabas in Antioch were richly blessed, and many were added to the number of believers there. As the work developed, Barnabas felt the need of suitable help, in order to advance in the opening providences of God; and he went to Tarsus to seek for Paul, who, after his departure from Jerusalem some time before, had been laboring in 'the regions of Syria and Cilicia,' proclaiming 'the faith which once he destroyed.' Barnabas was successful in finding Paul, and in persuading him to return with him as a companion in ministry."—Id., p. 156.

  1. 17.At what place was the name Christian first bestowed upon the followers of Christ? Verse 26.
  2. 18.Who next came to Antioch from Jerusalem? Verse 27.
  3. 19.What noted event took place in fulfillment of their prophetic utterances? Verse 28.
  4. 20.How was the church benefited by this prophetic warning? Verses 29, 30.

Personal Reflections

Am I worthy of bearing that royal name of Christian?

Do I "send relief" to the needy "according to" my ability?

What effect would persecution for my faith have upon me?
