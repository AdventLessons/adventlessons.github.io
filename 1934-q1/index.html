Appropriations of Past Thirteenth Sabbath Overflow Offerings to Japan and Korea

Map

Many mission enterprises around the circle of the earth have benefited from the overflow offerings of past years on the Thirteenth Sabbaths. This first quarter of 1934, we are again working toward an overflow for Japan and Korea, the overflow to be applied to help maintain the work our previous gifts have established. We list herewith the concrete benefits those fields have previously received from Thirteenth Sabbath overflows:
