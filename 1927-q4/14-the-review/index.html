Lesson 14 · December 31, 1927

The Review

PDFShare

Suggestions for Daily Family Study

  1. 1.Study Questions 1-3. Children may study and answer questions on paragraphs 1 and 2 of the primary lesson. Review the memory verses each day.
  2. 2.Study Questions 4 and 5. Children may study and answer questions on paragraphs 3 and 4 of the primary lesson.
  3. 3.Study Questions 6-8. Children may study and answer questions on paragraphs 5 and 6 of the primary lesson.
  4. 4.Study Questions 9-12. Children may study and answer questions on paragraphs 7 and 8 of the primary lesson.
  5. 5.Study Questions 13 and 14. Children may study and answer questions on paragraphs 9 and 10 of the primary lesson.
  6. 6.Study Questions 15-17. Children may study and answer questions on paragraphs 11 and 12 of the primary lesson.
  7. 7.Study Questions 18 and 19. Children may study and answer questions on paragraph 13 of the primary lesson.

Questions

Lesson 1. Search the Scriptures

  1. 1.What are we admonished to do? Of whom do they testify? John 5:39.

Notes

Lesson 2. Prayer

  1. 2.What instruction is given God's people regarding prayer? 1 Thess. 5:17.
  2. 3.For what must every sinner pray? 1 John 1:9.

Notes

Lesson 3. Victory Through Faith

  1. 4.What experience shows that personal blessings are measured by our faith? Matt. 9:27-29.

Notes

Lesson 4. The Holy Spirit

  1. 5.Why was the Holy Spirit sent into the world? What is its work? John 16:7, 13; 14:26.

Notes

Lesson 5. The Fruit of the Spirit

  1. 6.What is the fruit of the Spirit? Gal. 5:22, 23.
  2. 7.What must be the continual experience of one who bears good fruit? John 15:4, 5.

Notes

Lesson 6. Trials

  1. 8.If we love God, what do all things do for us? Rom. 8:28.

Notes

Lesson 7. Patience; Power of the Tongue

  1. 9.What is the greatest victory one can gain? Prov. 16:32.
  2. 10.What is said of the most unruly member of the body? James 3:2, 5-8.

Notes

Lesson 8. Reverence in the House of God

  1. 11.What does the presence of God do for any place? Ex. 3:4, 5.
  2. 12.Where are we to feel especial awe before the presence of God? Ps. 89:7.

Notes

Lesson 9. God's Care for Us

  1. 13.How did Jesus illustrate the care of the heavenly Father for each one of us? Matt. 6:25-30.

Notes

Lesson 10. Obedience

  1. 14.How is our love for Jesus tested? John 14:23, 24.

Notes

Lesson 11. True Temperance

  1. 15.Whose temple are we? What is the rule of true temperance? 1 Cor. 6:19, 20; 10:31.

Notes

Lesson 12. Let the Light Shine

  1. 16.Who is the light of the world? John 8:12.
  2. 17.What commission was given to the disciples and to us? Matt. 28:19, 20.

Notes

Lesson 13. Christian Conduct

  1. 18.How should we esteem others? Phil. 2:3; Rom. 12:10.
  2. 19.How is the obligation of the various members of the family set forth? Eph. 5:22-28; 6:1, 2.
