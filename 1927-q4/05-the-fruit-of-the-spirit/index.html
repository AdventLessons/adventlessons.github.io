Lesson 5 · October 29, 1927

The Fruit of the Spirit

PDFShare

Memory Verse: "Ye shall know them by their fruits." Matt. 7:16.

Suggestions for Daily Family Study

  1. 1.Read Matthew 7:15-20 and Galatians 5:14-26. Children may take part in the reading or have the meaning explained to them. Drill on the memory verse each day.
  2. 2.Study Questions 1-4. Children may read paragraphs 1 and 2 of the primary lesson.
  3. 3.Study Questions 5-7, and memorize Galatians 5:22, 23. Children may read paragraphs 3 and 4 of the primary lesson.
  4. 4.Study Questions 8-12, and notes. Children may read paragraphs 5 and 6 of the primary lesson.
  5. 5.Study Question 13. Children may read paragraphs 7 and 8 of the primary lesson.
  6. 6.Study Questions 14 and 15, and notes. Children may answer questions in the primary lesson. Discuss: a. The change made in a person by the Holy Spirit. b. Can the Holy Spirit be bought? See Acts 8:18-24.
  7. 7.Review by asking questions from senior, youth's, junior, or primary lesson. Study notes in the "Youth's Instructor."

The Lesson

  1. 1.What did Jesus say concerning the kingdom of God? Luke 17:21, last part, margin.
  2. 2.By what spiritual experience is the kingdom of God established within the heart? Rom. 14:17.
  3. 3.Where does the Spirit of God dwell? 1 Cor. 3:16; 6:19.
  4. 4.After surrender to the Lord's direction, what must be actively exercised in order to receive the blessing of the Spirit? Gal. 3:14.
  5. 5.Who are the sons of God? Rom. 8:14, 16.
  6. 6.What illustration did Jesus use in distinguishing between the false and the true? Matt. 7:15-20.
  7. 7.What fruit is borne in the life of one in whom the Spirit dwells? Gal. 5:22, 23.
  8. 8.What is said of the fruit first mentioned? 1 Cor. 13:13; Col. 3:14.

Notes

The word here translated "love" is from the Greek word "agape." It is translated "love" eighty-five times in the New Testament. "Agape" is also translated "charity" twenty-nine times in the New Testament.

  1. 9.What emphasis does the apostle Paul place upon the necessity of possessing love? 1 Cor. 13:1-3.
  2. 10.How is love manifested? Verses 4-7.

Notes

"The one who stands nearest to Christ will be he who on earth has drank most deeply of the spirit of His self-sacrificing love,—love that 'vaunteth not itself, is not puffed up, . . . seeketh not her own, is not easily provoked, thinketh no evil,'—love that moves the disciple, as it moved our Lord, to give all, to live and labor and sacrifice, even unto death, for the saving of humanity."—"The Desire of Ages," p. 549.

  1. 11.How do we obtain this great love? Rom. 5:5.

Notes

The Holy Spirit is the representative of the Father and the Son. It is by the Holy Spirit that the Lord restores His character in mankind. "God is love" (1 John 4:7, 8), and through the Holy Spirit He imparts that same love to our character.

  1. 12.What does love do? Prov. 10:12; 1 Peter 4:8.
  2. 13.Study the following texts in connection with the fruit of the Spirit mentioned in Galatians 5:22, 23:

Notes

Joy, Phil. 4:4; Rom. 14:17.

Peace, John 14:27; Rom. 12:18.

Long-suffering, Eph. 4:1, 2.

Gentleness, 2 Tim. 2:24; Titus 3:2.

Goodness, Rom. 2:4; 12:20, 21.

Faith, Heb. 11:6.

Meekness, Ps. 25:9; 1 Peter 3:4, last part.

Temperance, 1 Cor. 9:25.

  1. 14.What is said of all these virtues? Gal. 5:23, last clause.

Notes

The law condemns sin; but these virtues are in harmony with the law. They are produced by the Spirit; and the law, which is spiritual, can not, therefore, condemn them.

  1. 15.What is said of the man who controls his own spirit? Prov. 16:32.

Notes

"To subdue self, and bring the passions under the control of the will, is the greatest conquest that men and women can achieve."—"Testimonies," Vol. 3, p. 183.
