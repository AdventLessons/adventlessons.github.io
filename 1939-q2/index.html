Thirteenth Sabbath Offering
June 24, 1939
Southern African Division

Map

The Thirteenth Sabbath Offering, June 24

is devoted to the Southern African Division. The special projects of greatest need in this field are hospitals. Let us enter wholeheartedly into giving a large offering that there may be a liberal overflow. A special Thirteenth Sabbath envelope has been prepared, which your school can supply you, and in which you can save your offerings during the quarter, that your gift may be a generous one on June 24.

We hope the Thirteenth Sabbath Offering will reach $90,000. When our world Sabbath schools give $60,000 as a Thirteenth Sabbath Offering, $2,000 of this amount is applied as an "overflow," as well as 20 per cent of the excess above $60,000. Thus if our Sabbath schools give $90,000, there will be an overflow of $8,000 for the work in this needy Southern African Division. We can do it if we will!
