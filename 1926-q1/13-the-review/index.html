Lesson 13 · March 27, 1926

The Review

PDFShare

Suggestions for Daily Family Study

  1. 1.Study Questions 1, 2. Let each member of the family tell the details of the great supper. Children may tell the stories of the lost sheep and the prodigal son. In each day's study, review the memory verses.
  2. 2.Study Questions 3, 4. Let the children tell the story of the resurrection of Lazarus.
  3. 3.Study Question 5. Children relate the stories, older members of the family emphasize the practical truths.
  4. 4.Study Question 6. Children relate the story of the rich young ruler. The practical application should place the right estimate upon riches.
  5. 5.Study Questions 7, 8. Children should learn to define true greatness according to the Bible standard.
  6. 6.Study Questions 9, 10. Children may relate the three stories.
  7. 7.Study Questions 11, 12. Children describe the triumphal entry and the temple scene.

Questions

  1. 1.In the parable of the great supper, what excuses were offered by those bidden? How was the feast supplied with guests? What lessons may we learn from this parable? Lesson 1. Luke 14:12-24.
  2. 2.Relate the parable by which Jesus makes known the worth of a single soul. Of what is the parable of the prodigal son an illustration? Lesson 2. Luke 15:1-32.
  3. 3.What lesson may we learn from the parable of the unjust steward? Lesson 3. Luke 16:1-15.
  4. 4.By what great miracle did Jesus prove the truthfulness of His claim that He was the Son of God? What did this miracle cause the chief priests and rulers to do? Lesson 4. John 11:1-54.
  5. 5.What lesson is taught from the experience of the importunate widow? Contrast the prayer of the publican with that of the proud and boastful Pharisee. Lesson 5. Luke 18:1-17.
  6. 6.What test did Jesus give to a certain rich young ruler? What did this incident lead Jesus to say about riches? What did He say about future reward? Lesson 6. Matt. 19:16-30.
  7. 7.How did Jesus make plain the true principle which is the basis of the reward the Lord gives to His servants? Lesson 7. Matt. 20:1-16.
  8. 8.How did Jesus seek to prepare His disciples for the events awaiting Him? What mistaken notion led James and John to seek high honors? What is true greatness? Lesson 8. Matt. 20:17-28.
  9. 9.What wayside incident led to the healing of a blind man? What is the practical lesson taught by the story of Zaccheus? Lesson 9. Luke 18:35 to 19:10.
  10. 10.What great honor was shown to Jesus at a feast given by Simon, at Bethany? What caused criticism of the act? Lesson 10. John 12:1-11.
  11. 11.How was prophecy fulfilled in the triumphal entry of Jesus into Jerusalem? What scene was repeated in the temple? Lesson 11. Matt. 21:1-16.
  12. 12.What lesson is taught by the cursing of the fig tree? How were the Pharisees led to condemn themselves after hearing the parable of the two sons? Lesson 12. Matt. 21:17-32.

Notes

"Christ calls upon His people to believe and practice His word. Those who receive and assimilate this word, making it a part of every action, of every attribute of character, will grow strong in the strength of God."—"Testimonies," vol. 8, p. 300.

"The words of the living God are the highest of all education."—"Testimonies," vol. 8, p. 308.
