Thirteenth Sabbath Offering, Detached Missions of the General Conference, December 25

Picture

For the fourth quarter of this year, the detached mission fields of the General Conference are the Thirteenth Sabbath "overflow" offering objective. The principal detached mission lands are Egypt, Arabia, Syria, Iraq, Iran, Ethiopia, and the African west coast mission fields, including such countries as Sierra Leone, Liberia, Nigeria, Cameroons, and French Equatorial Africa.

The need is urgent in all these lands. Sabbath school members can do much in supplying these needs by a generous Christmas "overflow" offering. A tremendous task is yet to be accomplished in these fields. Training school work should be strengthened; a school for girls is desired in Nigeria, and new mission stations should be opened in many places.

May God move upon the hearts of all to give as never before. You can help to make it possible to enter some of these lands where work has not yet been started.
