Lesson 13 · March 29, 1924

Review

PDFShare

Daily Study Suggestions

  1. 1.Name the lesson titles in order, as a summary of the lessons for the quarter. Study Questions 1-3. Review the memory texts for Lessons 1 and 2.
  2. 2.Review Questions 1-3. Study Questions 4-7. Review all memory texts in Lessons 1-4.
  3. 3.Review Questions 1-7. Study Questions 8-12. Review memory texts in Lessons 1-7.
  4. 4.Review Questions 1-12. Study Questions 13-16. Review memory texts in Lessons 8-10.
  5. 5.Review Questions 13-16. Study Questions 17-20. Review memory texts in Lessons 11, 12.
  6. 6.Review the first half of the lessons, giving Bible answers from memory, if possible. Review half of the memory texts.
  7. 7.Review the last half of the lessons, giving answers from memory, if possible. Review the last half of the memory texts.

Questions

  1. 1.Since the fall of man, what has been the world's condition and need? Rom. 8:22, 23.
  2. 2.How did the Son of God completely fulfill His mission as a Saviour? Rom. 5:10.
  3. 3.In both the old and the new dispensation, what is the only message that saves? Luke 24:47; Acts 4:12.
  4. 4.How did the Lord Jesus reveal Himself to Abraham? How was He revealed through Abraham? Gal. 3:8; Gen. 12:7, 8.
  5. 5.What was God's great purpose in placing ancient Israel in their strategic geographical location? Zech. 8:22, 23.
  6. 6.Mention missionary opportunities noted in Old Testament history. 1 Kings 10:1, 6-9; 2 Kings 5:1-3, 8, 15.
  7. 7.Near the close of Judah's kingly line, what signal missionary opportunity and failure is recorded? 2 Kings 20:12, 13.
  8. 8.In what guise did God's missionaries travel to Babylon? What witness did they bear in Babylon? How did they testify at the courts of Media and Persia? Dan. 1:2-4, 15; 2:27, 28; 3; 4.
  9. 9.With Israel's tragic failure, what new foundation for world evangelization was laid? Matt. 10:1, 5, 8; Luke 10:1-3.
  10. 10.What divine equipment for service was given the early apostles? Acts 2:1-4.
  11. 11.Outline the steps in Paul's early life, conversion, and call to mission service. Acts 9:1-20.
  12. 12.Summarize the trials, burdens, and accomplishments of the apostle's career. 2 Cor. 11:24-28.
  13. 13.Mention divine providences that preserved "the church in the wilderness" from extinction.
  14. 14.What prolonged warfare was sustained by the "two witnesses"? Rev. 11:3-10.
  15. 15.Cite the Scriptural foundation for the world-wide mission movement of to-day. Matt. 28:18-20.
  16. 16.What prophecies indicate God's program for the finishing of the work? Rev. 14:6-12; Matt. 24:14.
  17. 17.What are the spiritual and financial duties of the church towards Christ's ambassadors? 2 Cor. 8:1-5.
  18. 18.What is the wellspring of all true sacrifice? Matt. 22:37, 38.
  19. 19.Enumerate the present life compensations of loyal service. Mark 10:29, 30; 1 Thess. 2:19, 20.
  20. 20.To the missionaries and those who support them, what future joys are in waiting? Dan. 12:3.

Notes

Make the Thirteenth Sabbath Dollar Day in your school by giving as many dollars as you have church members.
