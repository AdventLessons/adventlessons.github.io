Lesson 13 · December 26, 1925

The Review

PDFShare

Suggestions for Daily Family Study

  1. 1.Study Questions 1, 2. In each day's study, review the memory verses. Let each member of the family help to supply the details of the story told in Matt. 18:23-35.
  2. 2.Study Questions 3, 4. Let the children tell the story of the ten lepers. Luke 17:12-19.
  3. 3.Study Question 5. Let each member of the family help to supply the details of the lesson story.
  4. 4.Study Questions 6, 7. Let the children tell what the Good Shepherd does for His lambs, and also relate the parable of the good Samaritan.
  5. 5.Study Questions 8, 9. Children may relate the story of the selfish rich man.
  6. 6.Study Questions 10, 11. Children may tell the story of the fig tree, and that of the healing of the sick woman.
  7. 7.Study Question 12. Children may tell of Jesus' love and sorrow for the people in Jerusalem.

Questions

  1. 1.Relate briefly the story of the tribute money, and one lesson you get from it. How was humility exalted by Christ before His disciples? Lesson 1.
  2. 2.What did Jesus teach to be the full measure of forgiveness to others? How was this illustrated? Lesson 2.
  3. 3.Name the different excuses offered by those invited to follow Jesus. What should be a cause for greater rejoicing than the possession of great power? Lesson 3.
  4. 4.What were the ten lepers doing when healing came to them? What did one do when he saw that he was healed? Who was he? Lesson 4.
  5. 5.Tell briefly the story of the healing of the blind man. How did this miracle affect the Pharisees? Lesson 5.
  6. 6.Who is the Good Shepherd? What does He give for His sheep? How intimate is His knowledge of the flock? Lesson 6.
  7. 7.For what purpose did a lawyer question Jesus? How did Jesus bring an answer to his question from the lawyer's own lips? Relate the parable of the good Samaritan. Lesson 7.
  8. 8.Why did Jesus reprove Martha, while visiting in her home? On what occasion and in what manner, did Jesus reprove hypocrisy in the Pharisees? Lesson 8.
  9. 9.Relate briefly the parable given to warn against covetousness. Lesson 9.
  10. 10.Relate the parable given to admonish us to be always ready for Jesus' coming. How are families sometimes affected by the preaching of the gospel? Lesson 10.
  11. 11.What lesson is taught by the parable of the fig tree? After healing the woman on the Sabbath day, how did Jesus meet the objections of the synagogue ruler? Lesson 11.
  12. 12.How does Jesus show that outward forms or profession do not insure one a place in the kingdom of heaven? What were some of the causes that brought from Jesus deep expressions of sorrow and anguish? Lesson 12.

Bible texts in this lesson
