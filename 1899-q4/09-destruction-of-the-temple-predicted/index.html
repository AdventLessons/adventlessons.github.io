Lesson 9 · December 2, 1899

Destruction of the Temple Predicted

PDFShare

Mount of Olives, A. D. 31.

(Matt. 24:1-20; Mark 13:1-18; Luke 21:5-24.)

  1. 1.Upon departing from the temple to what did the disciples call the Saviour's attention? Mark 13:1. Side texts: Luke 21:5.
  2. 2.What prediction did Jesus make in reply to these observations? Verse 2.
  3. 3.To what place did they go? Which of the disciples questioned Jesus concerning His prediction? Mark 13:3. Side texts: Luke 21:7.
  4. 4.What was involved in the questions asked? Matt. 24:3. See note 1.
  5. 5.Of how much importance did Jesus consider these questions? Verse 4. Side texts: Eph. 5:6; Col. 2:8; 2 Thess. 2:3.
  6. 6.Why was this caution necessary? Verse 5. See note 2.
  7. 7.What further caution did He give them relative to His second coming? Verse 6.
  8. 8.What events did He mention as marking the beginning of sorrows? Verses 7, 8. Side texts: 2 Chron. 15:6; Luke 21:11.
  9. 9.What would be the experience of Christians during that period? Verses 9, 10.
  10. 10.What did Jesus say of false prophets? Verse 11. Side texts: Matt. 7:15-20.
  11. 11.What was to be the effect upon God's people of all this iniquity? Verse 12.
  12. 12.What precious promise is given to the faithful? Verse 13.
  13. 13.Before the end of the age, what must surely take place? Why is it necessary? Verse 14. Side texts: Acts 1:8.
  14. 14.Having traced events in a general way to the end, with what does Jesus begin to specify the events to reach to His coming? Verse 15. Side texts: Mark 13:14; Luke 21:20; Dan. 9:27.
  15. 15.What were Christians to do when this sign appeared? Verse 16.
  16. 16.How much haste were they to manifest in their movements when the time was indicated? Verses 17, 18.
  17. 17.Between the time of the Saviour's prediction and the occurrence of the events predicted, over what two points were the disciples constantly to pray? Verse 20.

Notes

Reading.

"Desire of Ages," chapter 69.

1. Three questions were asked, involving the destruction of the temple at Jerusalem, which the disciples believed would stand until the end of all things, the sign of Christ's second coming, and the end of the age. Christ took up first in His answer the great event around which all others cluster—His second coming to earth—and treated that in a general way. He then spoke of the destruction of the temple as the beginning of a series of events which are to terminate in His coming.

2. That is, men would proclaim themselves the Anointed, and by certain signs and wonders, lead many to accept their statements for truth. See Matt. 24:24. This has ever been a favorite method employed by Satan to mislead honest souls.
