Lesson 11 · March 11, 1893

The Effects of the Spirit

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

1. Questions on Gal. 5:16-25

  1. 1.What admonition is first given?
  2. 2.What follows as a consequence?
  3. 3.How are the desires of the flesh and Spirit contrasted?
  4. 4.What is the result of this opposition?
  5. 5.What is true of those led by the Spirit?
  6. 6.Mention the works of the flesh.
  7. 7.What happens to those who do such things?
  8. 8.What is the fruit of the Spirit?
  9. 9.What has been done by those who are Christ's?
  10. 10.What is the closing exhortation?

2. Questions on Rom. 8:9-17

  1. 1.What is true if the Spirit of God dwells in one?
  2. 2.What if he has not the Spirit?
  3. 3.If Christ is in us, what is dead? Why?
  4. 4.What is life? Why?
  5. 5.On what condition will our mortal bodies be quickened?
  6. 6.How will this be done?
  7. 7.What conclusion is then drawn?
  8. 8.What consequence follows living after the flesh?
  9. 9.On what condition shall we live?
  10. 10.Who are sons of God?
  11. 11.Have we received the spirit of bondage?
  12. 12.What Spirit have we received?
  13. 13.To what does this Spirit bear witness?
  14. 14.What consequences follow from our adoption?

Notes

  1. 1."While the work of the Spirit is silent and imperceptible, its effects are manifest. If the heart has been renewed by the Spirit of God, the life will bear witness to the fact." "Our lives will reveal whether the grace of God is dwelling in us." "And wherever the life of God is in the hearts of men, it will flow out to others in love and blessing." Love is of God (, Revised Version), and comes to us by the Spirit (). "The excellence and value of pure love consist in its efficiency to do good, and to do nothing else but good" (; , Revised Version). Christ desires us to have joy (), and has given us peace (), which is really giving us himself (), and both these gifts are in the Holy Spirit (). Those who are renewed after the image of God () will exhibit long-suffering (), will be gentle (), and yet will be great ().
  2. 2.God anointed Jesus with the Holy Spirit (), so he went about doing good. Our present need is the same anointing (). Where the Spirit of God is, there is unity () and liberty (). We are to walk worthy of our calling (), which means to walk according to the Spirit (). The Spirit being one of power (), and life (), and Christ being a life-giving spirit (, Revised Version; ), we are strong in him (), and not in our own might (). The gospel is to be preached with the Holy Spirit (; ), not in man's wisdom (); and thus the minister of God is approved (). "It is the absence of the Holy Spirit and of the grace of God that makes the gospel ministry so powerless to convict and convert." Such are some of the ways in which the Holy Spirit will manifest itself in those who receive it.
