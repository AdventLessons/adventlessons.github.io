Lesson 4 · October 27, 1923

Teachers

PDFShare

Lesson Help: "Education," pp. 73-83.

Daily Study Suggestions

  1. 1.Read the Lesson Help. If this is not at hand, trace the word "teacher" through the Scriptures by aid of a concordance or marginal references.
  2. 2.Study Questions 1-5. Notice what is taught in Notes 1 and 2, concerning teachers in Christ's day. What point is especially emphasized in Note 3?
  3. 3.What points are developed in the first five questions? Study Questions 6-9. Memorize Matt. 28:19, 20. In connection with the texts in Timothy, it will be interesting to read Acts 16:1, 2, and "Acts of the Apostles," pp. 203-205.
  4. 4.State the principal points in Questions 1-9. Study Questions 10-13. Review some of the experiences of the apostles as they taught the people. (Acts 5.) Review some of the experiences of the apostle Paul. (2 Cor. 6:3-10.) Repeat the memory text.
  5. 5.State the principal points in Questions 1-13. Study Questions 14-18. Repeat the memory text.
  6. 6.Review the first half of the lesson, giving answers, if possible, without referring to the texts. Arrange points in logical order. Memorize 2 Tim. 3:16.
  7. 7.Review the last half of the lesson, giving answers, if possible, without referring to the texts. Arrange points in logical order. Repeat the memory texts.

Questions

  1. 1.What gift to the church does Paul name following the gift of pastors? Eph. 4:11.
  2. 2.What formed a part of the personal ministry of Jesus? Matt. 4:23. Note 1.
  3. 3.What form of address did Nicodemus use in speaking to Jesus? John 3:2. Note 2.
  4. 4.What distinguishing characteristic was there about the teachings of Jesus? Matt. 7:28, 29. Note 3.
  5. 5.How extensively was this work of teaching carried on by Jesus? Matt. 9:35; Luke 23:5.
  6. 6.What commission did Jesus give to His followers just before His ascension? Matt. 28:19, 20.
  7. 7.Who was to be their instructor as they should go forth to fulfill this commission? John 14:26.
  8. 8.What kind of men were chosen to carry forward this line of work? 2 Tim. 2:2.
  9. 9.What previous instruction to Timothy regarding elders is equally applicable to teachers? 1 Tim. 3:2-4.
  10. 10.What is said of the teaching of the apostles after the departure of Jesus? Acts 5:42.
  11. 11.How earnestly did the apostle Paul strive to carry out the great commission? Col. 1:28.
  12. 12.How did he regard the work committed to him? 1 Tim. 2:7.
  13. 13.How were his teachings associated with those of Jesus? Acts 11:25, 26.
  14. 14.How can the church be best served by the gift of teaching? Deut. 6:4-9.
  15. 15.What should constitute the textbook of every Christian teacher? 2 Tim. 3:16.
  16. 16.To what extent were the followers of Christ to teach the gospel? Matt. 28:19.
  17. 17.What is said of those who do and teach the commandments of God? Matt. 5:19.
  18. 18.To what are those teachers compared who turn many to righteousness? Dan. 12:3, margin.

Notes

1. "Went about . . . teaching." It was customary in Eastern countries for teachers to travel about in their work of teaching, in company with their disciples, or students. Christ followed this custom largely in His work of teaching.

2. "Rabbi" was a term of respect for teachers. Jesus was called "teacher" oftener, perhaps, than by any other title. The word translated "master" in the Bible means teacher, and is generally so rendered in the Revised Version. The office of teacher was an honorable one in Christ's day, and had been for centuries before. Many of the most illustrious leaders in Israel were teachers. The prophetic office was essentially a teaching office, and Solomon was as much teacher and preacher as king. So Christ came into an atmosphere of inherited respect for the calling of teacher, and He gladly availed Himself of it.

"In the Teacher sent from God, heaven gave to men its best and greatest. He who had stood in the councils of the Most High, who had dwelt in the innermost sanctuary of the Eternal, was the One chosen to reveal in person to humanity the knowledge of God."—"Education," p. 73.

3. "What He taught, He lived. 'I have given you an example,' He said to His disciples; 'that ye should do as I have done.' I have kept My Father's commandments.' Thus in His life, Christ's words had perfect illustration and support. And more than this; what He taught, He was. His words were the expression, not only of His own life-experience; but of His own character. Not only did He teach the truth, but He was the truth. It was this that gave His teaching power."—Id., pp. 78, 79.

"Those who will put on the whole armor of God, and devote some time every day to meditation and prayer, and to the study of the Scriptures, will be connected with Heaven, and will have a saving, transforming influence upon those around them."—"Testimonies," vol. 5, p. 112.
