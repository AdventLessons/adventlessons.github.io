Lesson 7 · May 16, 1891

The Light of the World

  1. 1.What can you say as to Christ's equality with the Father?
  2. 2.Of how much honor is he worthy? .
  3. 3.What did the mind that was in him lead him to do?
  4. 4.Yet even when he came into the world humbling himself as a servant, what was he still worthy to receive? .
  5. 5.How are we to exhibit the same mind that was in Christ?
  6. 6.What has been done to Christ in consequence of his humiliation? ; .
  7. 7.If we have the same mind of humility that was in Christ, what will be done to us? ; .
  8. 8.What may be said of the greatest sufferings that we may endure? ; .
  9. 9.In view of all that Christ has done, what further exhortation is given? .
  10. 10.Who is it, after all, that does the work? .
  11. 11.To whom are we indebted for the first impulse to do right? .
  12. 12.And how is the work to be perfected? ; .
  13. 13.How should all things be done? ; compare .
  14. 14.If we obey this injunction, how shall we be? .
  15. 15.In what relation shall we stand in the world?—
  16. 16.What is the office of the church of God in the world? .
  17. 17.What is held forth by those who are the light of the world? .
  18. 18.Whence does all the light for the world come? .
