Lesson 11 · December 15, 1917

Proper Sabbath Observance, and Sabbath Reform in the Last Days

Questions

  1. 1.What promise was made to Israel, conditioned on proper Sabbath observance? Jer. 17:24, 25.
  2. 2.What judgment was predicted to overtake them for continued violation of the Sabbath? Verse 27.
  3. 3.What command had been given Israel concerning Sabbath observance? What had they been forbidden to do? Ex. 20:8-11.
  4. 4.What indicates that the Sabbath was not designed to be a burdensome thing, a yoke of bondage? Isa. 58:13.
  5. 5.While they were on their way to the promised land, what definite instruction was given to Israel concerning the Sabbath and its observance? Ex. 16:23-30.
  6. 6.During the wilderness experience of Israel, what point of Sabbath observance was especially emphasized? Ans.—Which day of the week was the Sabbath. Note 1.
  7. 7.When the children of Israel returned from captivity in Babylon, what was one of the chief features of Nehemiah's reformation? Neh. 13:15-22.
  8. 8.When our Lord was on earth, what kind of Sabbath reformation did He teach? Matt. 12:1-8.
  9. 9.Since the time of Christ, how has man treated God's holy day? Note 2.
  10. 10.How were the people of God anciently led to desecrate God's holy day? Ezek. 22:25-28. Note 3.
  11. 11.How does the prophet Daniel characterize the power that should think to change the times and laws of God? Dan. 7:25.
  12. 12.What does the Revelator say of the same power? Rev. 13:2-8.
  13. 13.To save a remnant of humanity from the influence of this last-day apostasy, what message is God now sending to the world? Rev. 14:6-11.
  14. 14.What will be the result of this message? Rev. 14:12; 15:2, 3.

Notes

1. Every week of the forty years, the children of Israel witnessed a threefold miracle, which definitely pointed out which day of the week was meant by the fourth commandment: first, a double portion of manna was given the day before the Sabbath; secondly, it was entirely withheld on the Sabbath; thirdly, that which was kept overnight for Sabbath use was found to be perfectly fresh and sweet, but if kept at any other time, it "bred worms, and stank." Ex. 16:20, 24.

2. Since the apostolic period, the professed church of Christ, like its prototype of old, has substituted the traditions of men for the commandments of God. It has degraded Jehovah's sacred day to a common working-day, and elevated in its place the "wild solar holiday of all pagan times."

3. While this passage applies to the apostasy in ancient Israel, it graphically describes the doings of that dominant ecclesiasticism which ruled Christendom for centuries; and it also pictures what will again take place when the same spirit of apostasy actuates the religious leaders of modern Christendom. This prophecy is rapidly passing into history before our eyes.
