Lesson 2 · October 10, 1953

The Lord's Supper and Life

PDFShare

Memory verse

Study Help: "The Desire of Ages," pages 524-536.

Aim: To show that although the observance of the Lord's Supper commemorates Christ's death, it also points forward to the second advent and life beyond the grave.

Daily Study Assignment

  1. 1.Sabbath afternoon: general survey of the lesson, based on outline.
  2. 2.Key Words; Introduction. Questions 1, 2.
  3. 3.Questions 3, 4; memorize John 11:25, 26.
  4. 4.Questions 5-8.
  5. 5.Questions 9-12.
  6. 6.Read "The Desire of Ages," pp. 524-536.
  7. 7.Review entire lesson; think about Suggestions for Meditation.

Lesson Outline

I. Introduction

II. Christ the Giver of Life

  1. 1.The origin of life. John 5:26; 1 John 5:12.
  2. 2.The claims of Jesus. John 11:25.
  3. 3.The proof of His claims. John 11:43, 44.

III. Emblems of the Life-Giver

  1. 1.The blood of the lamb saved life. Ex. 12:7, 13.
  2. 2.Jesus was the Lamb of God. John 1:29.
  3. 3.Believers triumph by the blood of the Lamb. Rev. 12:11.

IV. The Lord's Supper and the Resurrection

  1. 1.The celebration foreshadowed the second advent. 1 Cor. 11:26.
  2. 2.The second advent requires a resurrection. 1 Thess. 4:14, 16.
  3. 3.Deliverance from death is by "the blood of the everlasting covenant." Heb. 13:20.

Key Words

  1. 1."Covenant." The Greek word suntheke, covenant, compact, or treaty, between individuals or states, which is very common in profane Greek, is not found in the New Testament inasmuch as it connotes a compact between equals. The word used under the inspiration of the Holy Spirit to denote the covenant between God and man is diatheke, which differs from the preceding word in that its essential reference is a relationship established on one side only, or between unequals. This relationship is established by God between Himself and His people, consisting of both ordinances and the pledge of promises.
  2. 2."Resurrection" is an English translation of a Latin word that means "to resurge" or "to rise again." This gives added significance to the vision of Stephen when he saw "Jesus standing on the right hand of God." Acts 7:55.
  3. 3."Testament" means "contract, a will, or a covenant." Christ's blood was the seal of the new covenant, or new contract.

The Lesson

Introduction

In Christ only is there life. That life is symbolized by His blood, which in turn is represented by the wine. We show forth the Lord's death by celebrating the Lord's Supper. His life was made effectual for us because of His death; therefore the Lord's Supper represents His life which culminated in His death for us.

Life in Christ

  1. 1.What does Christ possess within Himself? John 5:26.
  2. 2.In seeking to comfort the sorrowing Martha, what did Jesus declare Himself to be? John 11:23-25.

Notes

"With human and divine pity Jesus looked into her sorrowful, careworn face. Martha had no inclination to recount the past; all was expressed by the pathetic words, 'Lord, if Thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.' But looking into that face of love, she added, 'I know, that even now, whatsoever Thou wilt ask of God, God will give it Thee.'

"Jesus encouraged her faith, saying, 'Thy brother shall rise again.' His answer was not intended to inspire hope of an immediate change. He carried Martha's thoughts beyond the present restoration of her brother, and fixed them upon the resurrection of the just. This He did that she might see in the resurrection of Lazarus a pledge of the resurrection of all the righteous dead, and an assurance that it would be accomplished by the Saviour's power."—The Desire of Ages, pages 529, 530.

  1. 3.By what miracle did Jesus prove that He was the resurrection and the life? John 11:43, 44.

Notes

"Still seeking to give a true direction to her faith, Jesus declared, 'I am the resurrection, and the life.' In Christ is life, original; unborrowed, underived. 'He that hath the Son hath life.' 1 John 5:12. The divinity of Christ is the believer's assurance of eternal life. 'He that believeth in Me,' said Jesus, 'though he were dead, yet shall he live: and whosoever liveth and believeth in Me shall never die. Believest thou this?' Christ here looks forward to the time of His second coming. Then the righteous dead shall be raised incorruptible, and the living righteous shall be translated to heaven without seeing death. The miracle which Christ was about to perform, in raising Lazarus from the dead, would represent the resurrection of all the righteous dead. By His word and His works He declared Himself the Author of the resurrection. He who Himself was soon to die upon the cross stood with the keys of death, a conqueror of the grave, and asserted His right and power to give eternal life."—The Desire of Ages, page 530.

  1. 4.How, then, may we be assured of eternal life? 1 John 5:12.

The Life of Christ Symbolized

  1. 5.How was the blood of the first Passover lamb used? Ex. 12:7, 13.
  2. 6.What did John declare Jesus to be? John 1:29.

Notes

"In Jesus, as shown in types, as shadowed in symbols, as manifested in the revelation of the prophets, as unveiled in the lessons given to His disciples, and in the miracles wrought for the sons of men, they have beheld 'the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world,' and through Him they are brought within the fold of His grace."—The Desire of Ages, page 477.

  1. 7.What statement of John indicates the importance of Christ's shed blood in individual victory over sin? Rev. 12:11.

Picture: The Lord's Supper Points Forward to Christ's Second Advent.

  1. 8.What is the blood declared to be? Gen. 9:4.

The Lord's Supper a Promise of Life to Come

  1. 9.How long was the observance of the Lord's Supper to show Jesus' death? 1 Cor. 11:26.

Notes

The expression "till He come" indicates that He will come. The emblems of the Lord's Supper are meaningless unless Jesus lived after His death and is still living. Although these emblems typify His death, they are also bound up inseparably with His life.

  1. 10.What does the apostle Paul call the blood of Jesus? Heb. 13:20, 21.

Notes

In many Bibles the marginal reading for "covenant" is "testament."

  1. 11.When Jesus gave the cup to the disciples, what did He say it symbolized? Mark 14:24.
  2. 12.What solemn pledge did Jesus make regarding the fruit of the vine? Mark 14:25.

Suggestions for Meditation

  1. 1.Jesus' death and life is our salvation. "For if when we were enemies, we were reconciled to God by the death of His Son, much more, being reconciled, we shall be saved by His life." Rom. 5:10.
  2. 2.As we partake of the emblems of the Lord's Supper, do we long for the life to come when we personally shall share the Saviour's promise? See Rev. 22:20.
