Lesson 9 · November 28, 1953

"My Flesh Is Meat"

PDFShare

Memory verse

Study Helps: "Education," pages 123-127; "Patriarchs and Prophets," page 599.

Aim: To more fully understand the meaning of Jesus' words, "My flesh is meat."

Daily Study Assignment

  1. 1.Sabbath afternoon: general survey of the lesson, based on outline.
  2. 2.Key Words; Introduction. Questions 1-3.
  3. 3.Questions 4, 5; memorize John 6:63.
  4. 4.Questions 6-8.
  5. 5.Questions 9-11.
  6. 6.Questions 12, 13; read "Education," pp. 123-127; "Patriarchs and Prophets," p. 599.
  7. 7.Review entire lesson; think about Suggestions for Meditation.

Lesson Outline

I. Introduction

II. Spiritual Food

  1. 1.Christ's flesh is the symbol of man's necessary spiritual food. John 6:53-56.
  2. 2.Christ's words are quickening food. John 6:63.
  3. 3.God's will was food to Jesus. John 4:32-34.
  4. 4.Jesus is the Bread of Life. John 6:48.

III. Partaking of Spiritual Food

  1. 1.Significance of partaking of the Communion. 1 Cor. 10:16, 17.
  2. 2.David's and Jeremiah's estimates of the word. Ps. 119:103; Jer. 15:16.
  3. 3.Spiritual regeneration. John 3:5-7.

IV. The Objective of Partaking of the Lord's Supper

  1. 1.To remind us of the death of Christ. 1 Cor. 11:26.
  2. 2.To remind us that Christ died for our sins. 1 Cor. 15:3.
  3. 3.To help us to follow Jesus' example in dying to sin. Rom. 6:10; 1 Peter 2:21.
  4. 4.To make us worthy of our Lord. 1 Cor. 11:27-29.

Key Words

  1. 1.Communion has the same meaning as the word common—"to have in common," or "the act of sharing." The Greek word used in the New Testament which is translated "fellowship" has the same meaning—"sharing in common." The communion of His body and blood means sharing the emblems with discernment and fellowship.
  2. 2.The Word. Christ is the living Word, and that Word was made flesh. (John 1:1-3, 14.) The Bible is the written word and is powerful. (Heb. 4:12.) By the word and by the Spirit comes the new birth. (1 Peter 1:23; John 3:5.) The written words are spirit and life. (John 6:63.)

The Lesson

Introduction

When we begin to discern the body and blood of Christ, the first part of the service (the ordinance of humility) will prepare us for the Lord's table, and partaking of the bread and wine will result in our spiritual revival. Then the ordinances will no longer be a mere form to us. We will then be glad to observe them, and will not pierce the heart of Jesus by neglecting them.

"These ordinances are regarded too much as a form, and not as a sacred thing to call to mind the Lord Jesus. . . . It is in these, His own appointments, that He meets with and energizes His people by His personal presence."—Ellen G. White, in Review and Herald, June 22, 1897.

"How the heart of Christ is pierced by the forgetfulness, the unwillingness and neglect, to do the things that God has enjoined upon us!"—Ellen G. White, in Review and Herald, June 22, 1897.

Spiritual Food

  1. 1.What must men eat and drink to live? John 6:53-56.
  2. 2.By what are Christ's body and blood symbolized? Matt. 26:26, 27.
  3. 3.What final explanation did Jesus give regarding His flesh as food? John 6:63.

Notes

"It is the Spirit who quickens the soul to a new, immortal, and heavenly existence, and that Spirit acts through the words of sacred truth which I speak to you. They are spirit, and they are life.'"—Cunningham Geikie, The Life and Words of Christ, page 718.

  1. 4.What was the meat, or food, of Jesus? John 4:32-34.

Notes

"God stands toward His people in the relation of a father, and He has a father's claim to our faithful service. Consider the life of Christ. Standing at the head of humanity, serving His Father, He is an example of what every son should and may be. The obedience that Christ rendered God requires from human beings today."—Christ's Object Lessons, page 282.

Picture: Do Unwillingness, Forgetfulness, or Neglect Keep Us Away?

  1. 5.What did Jesus declare Himself to be? John 6:48, 50, 51. Compare John 1:14.

Partaking of Spiritual Food

  1. 6.What does the partaking of the Communion signify? 1 Cor. 10:16, 17.
  2. 7.How did David and Jeremiah express their pleasure in partaking of the word of God? Ps. 119:103; Jer. 15:16.
  3. 8.What spiritual regeneration is essential? What place does the word of God have in this experience? John 3:5-7; 1 Peter 1:23.

Notes

"The word of God is the seed. Every seed has in itself a germinating principle. In it the life of the plant is enfolded. So there is life in God's word. Christ says, 'The words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.' 'He that heareth My word, and believeth on Him that sent Me, hath everlasting life.' In every command and in every promise of the word of God is the power, the very life of God, by which the command may be fulfilled and the promise realized. He who by faith receives the word is receiving the very life and character of God."—Christ's Object Lessons, page 38.

Reason for Partaking of Spiritual Food

  1. 9.How do we show forth the Lord's death? 1 Cor. 11:26.
  2. 10.In showing His death, what are we to remember that He died for? 1 Cor. 15:3.
  3. 11.Unto what did Jesus die? What example did He leave us? Rom. 6:10; 1 Peter 2:21.

Notes

In dying to sin, Jesus died to self-seeking, selfishness, and to the unforgiving spirit, and by His conduct He gave us an example. He "came not to be ministered unto, but to minister."

  1. 12.In order not to partake of the Lord's Supper unworthily, what are we admonished to do? 1 Cor. 11:27-29.
  2. 13.What twofold purpose does the Lord's Supper serve? 1 Cor. 11:26.

Suggestions for Meditation

  1. 1.The fact that we feel unworthy does not make us eat and drink at the Lord's table unworthily. The unworthiness consists in the fact that we do not see in the body and blood of Jesus a way out of sin.
  2. 2.If Jesus died for our sins, and has given us an example that He also died to sin and selfishness, then we should show that His death has been effectual for us by the fact that we also have died to sin and selfishness.
