2026 · Quarter 4

God's Word Through the Ages

How has God made His will known to His people? This quarter explores how God spoke through His prophets, guided the writing of Scripture and preserved His Word through the centuries. We will learn how to recognise His voice, test what we hear against the Bible and grow in faith through careful study. Above all, we will see how Scripture leads us to Jesus Christ.
