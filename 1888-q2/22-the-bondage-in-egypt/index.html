Lesson 22 · June 2, 1888

The Bondage in Egypt

PDFShare
  1. 1.When Joseph had made himself known to his brethren, what did he tell them to do? Gen. 45:9-11.
  2. 2.What liberal offer did Pharaoh make? Verses 17-20.
  3. 3.What timely caution did Joseph give his brethren? Verse 24.
  4. 4.How did Jacob feel when his sons returned with this story? Verses 25, 26.
  5. 5.What finally induced him to consent to go? Verses 27, 28.
  6. 6.When he started, what encouragement did the Lord give him? Gen. 46:1-4.
  7. 7.How many were there who went down into Egypt? Acts 7:14.
  8. 8.How long did they live there in peace? Compare Gen. 41:46; 45:11; 50:26.
  9. 9.How did the children of Israel prosper in Egypt? Ex. 1:7.
  10. 10.What took place some time after Joseph's death? Verse 8.
  11. 11.What did this king say to his people? Verses 9, 10.
  12. 12.What did they do to the Israelites? Verse 11.
  13. 13.How did this affect them? Verse 12.
  14. 14.What did the children of Israel have to suffer at the hands of the Egyptians? Verses 13, 14.
  15. 15.Of what prophecy was this a fulfillment? Gen. 15:13.
  16. 16.What cruel order did the king of Egypt make? Ex. 1:22.
  17. 17.Relate the story of one child who was saved from the king's decree. Ex. 2:1-10.
  18. 18.What led the parents of Moses to do as they did? Heb. 11:23.
