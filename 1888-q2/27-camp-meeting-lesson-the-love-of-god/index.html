Camp-meeting lesson · 1888

The Love of God

This lesson PDF Whole booklet PDF · 27 lessonsShare on WhatsApp
  1. 1.In what does the Lord delight? .
  2. 2.How is his mercy continually manifested? .
  3. 3.Is his mercy limited to any special class? ; .
  4. 4.How has mankind repaid him for his love in creating them and giving them all things to enjoy? ; .
  5. 5.While all men were in this condition, how did God still further show his love to them? ; .
  6. 6.Is there any difference between the love of the Father and that of the Son? .
  7. 7.Into what relationship to God does his love bring us, if we accept it? .
  8. 8.What is given us to show that we are sons? .
  9. 9.What does the Holy Spirit beget in the hearts of those who receive it? .
  10. 10.If the love of God is shed abroad in our hearts, what effect will it have? .
  11. 11.How much must we love one another? ; .
  12. 12.What exhortation to us is based upon Christ's love for us? .
  13. 13.If we have the love of God, will we love only those who love us? ; .
  14. 14.Did God love us because of any good that we had done? ; .
  15. 15.What alone should prompt all our acts? .
  16. 16.In what other way is God's love manifested to his people? ; .
  17. 17.What may we confidently expect from the fact that God so loved us as to give Christ to die for us? .
  18. 18.What is God's love for his children able to do when enemies rise up against them? .
  19. 19.If we appreciate this loving-kindness what will we do? .
  20. 20.How enduring is God's love to us? .
  21. 21.Can anything turn aside the love of God for those who trust him? .
  22. 22.Unto whom will the saints of God ascribe universal praise both here and in eternity? , last part.

Notes

> "There's a wideness in God's mercy, > Like the wideness of the sea; > There's a kindness in his justice, > That is more than liberty. > > "There is welcome for the sinner, > And more graces for the good. > There is mercy with the Saviour; > There is healing in his blood. > > "For the love of God is broader > Than the measure of man's mind; > And the heart of the Eternal > Is most wonderfully kind. > > "If our love were but more simple, > We should take him at his word; > And our lives would be all sunshine > In the sweetness of our Lord."
