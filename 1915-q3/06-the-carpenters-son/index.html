Lesson 6 · August 7, 1915

The Carpenter's Son

Lesson Scripture: Matt. 13:47-58.

Questions

  1. 1.To what is the kingdom of heaven further likened? Matt. 13:47. Note 1.
  2. 2.What was done with the net when it was full? Verse 48, first part.
  3. 3.What did the fishermen then do? Verse 48, last part.
  4. 4.What time does the drawing of the net to the shore typify? Verse 49, first part.
  5. 5.What does the sorting of the fish represent? Verse 49, last part.
  6. 6.What was done with the bad fish? Verse 48.
  7. 7.What will be done with the wicked? Verse 50.
  8. 8.Did the disciples understand these parables? Verse 51.
  9. 9.What saying of Jesus was here fulfilled? Verse 11.
  10. 10.By what comparison did Jesus illustrate the work of the Christian disciple? Verse 52.
  11. 11.What did Jesus then do? Verse 53.
  12. 12.When He came into His own country, what did He do? Verse 54, first part.
  13. 13.What was the effect of His teaching? Verse 54, second part.
  14. 14.What question did the people ask? Verse 54, last part.
  15. 15.How did they indicate their unbelief in His divine origin? Verse 55.
  16. 16.What question of their own could they not solve? Verse 56.
  17. 17.What was the result of their reasonings? Verse 57, first part. Note 2.
  18. 18.What pointed truth did Jesus utter to them? Verse 57, last part.
  19. 19.What was the outcome of Jesus' teaching in His own country? Verse 58.

Notes

1. How true it is that the gospel net, when it is cast into the sea of humanity, gathers of every kind! The church has always had its foes within as well as its foes without. Among the twelve disciples were the bad as well as the good. Peter and Paul were troubled with false brethren, while supported in their labors by loyal ones. So will it doubtless be to the end.

2. This effort of Jesus to teach in His own country recalls the first recorded instance of His standing up in the synagogue at Nazareth, when He read from the book of Isaiah with such effect that the eyes of all were fastened on Him. Luke 4:16 and onward. At that time, the people began to talk of His family connection; and as He sought to explain the meaning of what He had read, they were filled with wrath, and rose up and thrust Him out of the city. So here His longing to save His own countrymen was disappointed by their foolish reasoning and their unbelief.
