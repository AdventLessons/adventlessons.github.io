Lesson 12 · September 18, 1915

Spiritual Discernment

Lesson Scripture: Matt. 16:1-12.

Questions

  1. 1.What two classes now came to Jesus? In what spirit? Matt. 16:1, first part. Note 1.
  2. 2.What request did they make of Jesus? Verse 1, last part.
  3. 3.What common saying did their request for a sign suggest to Jesus? Verse 2.
  4. 4.What was a common saying in the morning about weather? Verse 3, first part.
  5. 5.What comparison did Jesus then draw? Verse 3, last part.
  6. 6.How did He characterize these seekers after a sign? Verse 4, first part.
  7. 7.What did He say about giving them a sign? Verse 4, middle part. Note 2.
  8. 8.What did Jesus then do? Verse 4, last part.
  9. 9.What did the disciples forget as they went with Him? Verse 5.
  10. 10.What did Jesus say to them? Verse 6.
  11. 11.How did they reason about His saying? Verse 7.
  12. 12.How did Jesus reprove them? Verse 8.
  13. 13.What pointed question did He ask them? Verse 9.
  14. 14.Of what other miracle did Jesus remind them? Verse 10.
  15. 15.What further question did He ask them? Verse 11.
  16. 16.How did they understand Jesus' saying in verse 6? Verse 12.

Notes

1. Though the Pharisees and the Sadducees were opposed to each other in many of their beliefs, and were often at enmity in their dealings, yet they frequently united in their efforts to overthrow the teachings of Jesus and weaken His influence. Here they sought to "tempt" or test Him, that they might entangle Him in His talk.

2. The sign that the Jews most needed had already been given them — the sign of the prophet Jonah, with the lesson of Jonah's message of repentance to Nineveh. This was also the message of John the Baptist. If the Pharisees and the Sadducees could have the experience of repentance, this would clear up their spiritual perceptions; but without repentance and belief on the Lord Jesus Christ, it would be useless to give them other signs.
