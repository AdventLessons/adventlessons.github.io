Lesson Scripture: Matt. 12:22 to 16:12.

Outline and Questions

(See Note)

Chapter 12 — He That Is Not with Me Is Against Me

  1. 1.How did the Pharisees charge Jesus with being against Himself?
  2. 2.What principles did He enunciate in response?
  3. 3.What sign did Jesus give the Pharisees when they asked for one?

Chapter 13 — Teaching in Parables

  1. 1.Give a list of the parables recorded in this chapter.
  2. 2.Give at least one important lesson taught in each.
  3. 3.Cite the experience of Jesus in His own country after the giving of these parables.

Chapter 14 — Death of John; The Loaves and the Fishes; Walking on the Sea

  1. 1.Give two important lessons from the narrative of John's death.
  2. 2.What principles of Christian living are illustrated in the miracle of the loaves and the fishes?
  3. 3.What practical help have you gained from the record of the walking on the sea, since we studied that lesson?

Chapter 15 — Heart Service; Prevailing Faith; Feeding the Hungry

  1. 1.Point out the lesson that Jesus developed from the criticism of His disciples for not observing the ceremonial washing of hands.
  2. 2.What circumstances led up to Jesus' calling the faith of the Canaanitish woman great?
  3. 3.What added help do you get from the study of this second miracle of loaves and fishes?

Chapter 16 — Spiritual Discernment

  1. 1.How did Jesus deal with the lack of spiritual discernment by the Pharisees and the Sadducees?
  2. 2.How did He deal with the lack of it by His disciples?

Notes

Seek earnestly in this week's study to fix in mind the general content of each chapter studied during the quarter. The review outline and questions have been arranged to help you in this. It is of great value to the Christian and gospel worker to know not only the lessons of the Scripture, but also where to find them readily.
