Lesson 4 · April 26, 1919

Regeneration, or the New Birth

PDFShare

Daily Study Outline

  1. 1.Read "Desire of Ages," chapter 17.
  2. 2.Importance of the New Birth.
  3. 3.Nature of the New Birth.
  4. 4.Baptism and Regeneration.
  5. 5.Means Used in Connection with Regeneration.
  6. 6.Review questions 1-7.
  7. 7.Review questions 8-15.

Questions

Importance of the New Birth

  1. 1.Upon what does one's entrance into the kingdom depend? John 3:3.
  2. 2.Do conditions of race or birthplace exempt one from this experience? Gal. 6:15.
  3. 3.What is the fundamental reason why every one is in need of this experience? Rom. 8:7, 8. Note 1.

Nature of the New Birth

  1. 4.Is this change wrought by mere human reformation? John 1:13. Note 2.
  2. 5.By whom is this new nature given? Eph. 2:1, 10. Note 3.
  3. 6.Of whose nature does the regenerated soul partake? 2 Peter 1:4.
  4. 7.How is this new nature formed? Eph. 4:24.

Baptism and Regeneration

  1. 8.How is the new birth experience given? John 3:5.
  2. 9.Even after his baptism by water, to what was Simon the sorcerer a stranger? Acts 8:13, 21.
  3. 10.What experience came before baptism to the company gathered at the home of Cornelius? Acts 10:44-48.

Means Used in Connection with Regeneration

  1. 11.What experience follows genuine conversion? John 1:12, 13. Note 4.
  2. 12.How is the word of God operative in this great transaction? James 1:18; 1 Peter 1:23.
  3. 13.What divine operation accompanies the Word in regeneration? Acts 16:14.
  4. 14.What should man do as the Word comes to him? 1 Peter 1:22, 23.
  5. 15.Into what personal relation to God does the soul enter in regeneration? Gal. 3:26, 27. Note 5.

Notes

1. "There are thousands to-day who need to learn the same truth that was taught to Nicodemus by the uplifted serpent. They depend on their obedience to the law of God to commend them to His favor. When they are bidden to look to Jesus, and believe that He saves them solely through His grace, they exclaim, 'How can these things be?'"—"Desire of Ages," page 175.

2. "No human invention can find a remedy for the sinning soul. . . . The fountain of the heart must be purified before the streams can become pure. He who is trying to reach heaven by his own works in keeping the law, is attempting an impossibility. There is no safety for one who has merely a legal religion, a form of godliness. The Christian's life is not a modification or improvement of the old, but a transformation of nature. There is a death to self and sin, and a new life altogether. This change can be brought about only by the effectual working of the Holy Spirit."—Id., page 172.

3. "Not all the wisdom and skill of man can produce life in the smallest object in nature. It is only through the life which God Himself has imparted, that either plant or animal can live. So it is only through the life from God that spiritual life is begotten in the hearts of men. Unless a man is 'born from above,' he cannot become a partaker of the life which Christ came to give."—"Steps to Christ," page 71.

4. "More than this, Christ changes the heart. He abides in your heart by faith. You are to maintain this connection with Christ by faith and the continual surrender of your will to Him; and so long as you do this, He will work in you to will and to do according to His good pleasure. So you may say, 'The life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave Himself for me.'"—Id., pages 67, 68.

5. "Here is where thousands fail: they do not believe that Jesus pardons them personally, individually. They do not take God at His word. It is the privilege of all who comply with the conditions to know for themselves that pardon is freely extended for every sin. Put away the suspicion that God's promises are not meant for you. They are for every repentant transgressor. Strength and grace have been provided through Christ to be brought by ministering angels to every believing soul."—Id., page 57.
