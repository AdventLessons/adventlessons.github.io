Lesson 10 · March 10, 1900

Scourged and Condemned

PDFShare
Side lights "Desire of Ages," chapters 76, 77; "Spirit of Prophecy," vol. 3, chapter 8.

Jerusalem, A. D. 31.

(Matt. 27:11-26; Mark 15:2-15; Luke 23:2-25; John 18:29 to 19:1.)

  1. 1.When Jesus was brought before Pilate, what accusations were made against Him? Luke 23:2.
  2. 2.What question did Pilate ask Jesus? What did He reply? Verse 3. Side texts: John 18:29-38.
  3. 3.What did Pilate then say to the chief priests and the people? Verse 4.
  4. 4.How did this decision affect the people? What further charge did they bring? Verse 5.
  5. 5.When Pilate learned that Jesus belonged to Herod's jurisdiction, what did he do? Verses 6, 7. Side texts: Luke 3:1.
  6. 6.How did Herod feel when he saw Jesus? Verse 8. Side texts: Matt. 14:1, 2.
  7. 7.When questioned by Herod, what did Jesus do? Verse 9.
  8. 8.What were the priests and their attendants doing at the same time? Verse 10.
  9. 9.How did Herod treat Jesus? Verse 11. Side texts: Acts 4:27.
  10. 10.Upon Jesus being returned to Pilate, what did Pilate say to the people? Verses 13-16.
  11. 11.To what custom did he refer when he said he would release Jesus? Matt. 27:15. Side texts: Mark 15:6; John 18:39.
  12. 12.What question did he then ask the people? Verses 16-18. Side texts: John 6:15.
  13. 13.As Pilate went back into the judgment hall, what message was sent to him? Verse 19.
  14. 14.For whose release from custody did the people call? Why? Verses 20, 21. Side texts: Mark 15:11.
  15. 15.Who was Barabbas? Mark 15:7.
  16. 16.What further question did Pilate ask? What answer was returned? Matt. 27:22.
  17. 17.When Pilate pleaded in behalf of Jesus, what was the result? Verse 23. Side texts: John 19:8-12.
  18. 18.What did Pilate then do? Verse 24. Side texts: John 19:13.
  19. 19.How did the people reply? Verse 25.
  20. 20.In view of this, what did Pilate consent to do? Verse 26. Side texts: Luke 23:25.

Reading

"Desire of Ages," chapters 76, 77; "Spirit of Prophecy," vol. 3, chapter 8.
