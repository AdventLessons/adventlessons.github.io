Lesson 13 · December 26, 1903

Gathering Out of His Kingdom All Things That Offend

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

Memory Text.—"Then shall the righteous shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father." .

Questions

  1. 1.What parable of our Saviour illustrates the great controversy between Christ and Satan, concerning the ownership and rulership of this world? .
  2. 2.Who is represented by the man who sowed good seed in this field? Whose field was it? .
  3. 3.When did he sow his field with good seed? John ⟦1:3, 4?⟧.
  4. 4.What is the field in which the good seed was sown? What is represented by the good seed? What by the tares? .
  5. 5.Who is the enemy who sowed the tares? When will the harvest be? Who will be the reapers? Matt. ⟦13:39?⟧
  6. 6.How will it be in the end of this world? .
  7. 7.On what mission will the Son of Man then send His angels? What will then be gathered out of His kingdom? Out of whose kingdom? .
  8. 8.After this gathering, where will the righteous appear? Then whose kingdom has it been all the time? .
  9. 9.What will then have been brought back to its rightful owner? .
  10. 10.Whose throne will then be established upon this earth? .
  11. 11.Whose character will the subjects of the kingdom then bear? .
  12. 12.How long will they then reign in the kingdom of their Father? Rev. ⟦21:5?⟧
  13. 13.What promise will then be fulfilled to the faithful? .
