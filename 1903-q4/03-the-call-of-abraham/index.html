Lesson 3 · October 17, 1903

The Call of Abraham

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

Memory Text.—"For the Lord is our defense; and the Holy One of Israel is our King." .

Questions

  1. 1.After the flood when the whole world had again departed from God, and had gone into idolatry and sin, why could not the Lord again destroy the world by a flood? . (Repeat .)
  2. 2.What provision was then made for the fulfilment of the promise that the Seed of the woman should bruise the serpent's head? .
  3. 3.From whom, and to what place, was Abraham called to go? .
  4. 4.How did he respond to this call? .
  5. 5.How was the promise made to Adam, that the Seed of the woman should bruise the serpent's head, repeated to Abraham, and enlarged upon? .
  6. 6.What was comprised in this covenant made with Abraham? .
  7. 7.What was "His oath unto Isaac?" .
  8. 8.How was this covenant "confirmed to Jacob for a law"? . (Repeat .)
  9. 9.How was it "confirmed to Israel for an everlasting covenant"? .
  10. 10.Repeat .
  11. 11.Who were associated with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, in the promise?
  12. 12.Who is the Seed referred to in all these promises? .
  13. 13.Through whom alone was Abraham to look for the promised Seed? .
  14. 14.Tell the wonderful story of the trial of Abraham's faith. .
  15. 15.Repeat .
