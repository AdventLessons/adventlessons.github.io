Lesson 5 · October 31, 1903

The Seventy Years' Captivity in Babylon

PDFShare

Memory verse

Questions

  1. 1.After coming out of Egypt, how long was it before the children of Israel entered the land of Canaan? Acts 13:17-19.
  2. 2.How long did they continue to faithfully serve the Lord? Judges 2:7.
  3. 3.What did they do after Joshua's death? Judges 2:11-13.
  4. 4.What was the result of the protecting hand of the Lord being withdrawn from them? Judges 2:14, 15.
  5. 5.What means of deliverance did the Lord, in mercy, raise up for them? Judges 2:7.
  6. 6.How long were they ruled by judges? Acts 13:20.
  7. 7.At the close of Samuel's life, what did the people demand? 1 Sam. 8:4, 5.
  8. 8.In their demand for a king, whom were they rejecting as king? 1 Sam. 8:7.
  9. 9.In His compassion, how did the Lord seek to bring them back to Him and to the high calling to which He had called them? 2 Chron. 36:15.
  10. 10.How long did they continue to depart from the Lord and refuse to walk in His ways? 2 Chron. 36:16.
  11. 11.When "there was no remedy"—no other remedy—what did the Lord permit to come upon them? Jer. 25:8, 9, 11.
  12. 12.What did the Lord promise to do for them at the close of the seventy years? Jer. 29:10.
