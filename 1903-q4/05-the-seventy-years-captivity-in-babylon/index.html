Lesson 5 · October 31, 1903

The Seventy Years' Captivity in Babylon

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

Memory Text.—"Nevertheless He regarded their affliction, when he heard their cry; . . . He made them also to be pitied of all those that carried them captives." .

Questions

  1. 1.After coming out of Egypt, how long was it before the children of Israel entered the land of Canaan? .
  2. 2.How long did they continue to faithfully serve the Lord? .
  3. 3.What did they do after Joshua's death? .
  4. 4.What was the result of the protecting hand of the Lord being withdrawn from them? .
  5. 5.What means of deliverance did the Lord, in mercy, raise up for them? .
  6. 6.How long were they ruled by judges? .
  7. 7.At the close of Samuel's life, what did the people demand? .
  8. 8.In their demand for a king, whom were they rejecting as king? .
  9. 9.In His compassion, how did the Lord seek to bring them back to Him and to the high calling to which He had called them? .
  10. 10.How long did they continue to depart from the Lord and refuse to walk in His ways? .
  11. 11.When "there was no remedy"—no other remedy—what did the Lord permit to come upon them? .
  12. 12.What did the Lord promise to do for them at the close of the seventy years? .
