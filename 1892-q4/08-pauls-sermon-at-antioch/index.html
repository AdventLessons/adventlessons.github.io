Lesson 8 · Nov. 19, 1892

Paul's Sermon at Antioch.—Acts 13:26-43

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(Memory Verse, 38.)

Golden Text.—"To you is the word of this salvation sent." .

  1. 1.In response to the invitation to speak in the synagogue at Antioch, what familiar history did Paul rehearse? .
  2. 2.What did he connect with the mention of David? .
  3. 3.What encouraging word did he speak to those present? .
  4. 4.How had the dwellers in Jerusalem condemned themselves? .
  5. 5.What story did Paul next rehearse? .
  6. 6.What glad tidings did Paul now declare? .
  7. 7.In what words is the resurrection of Christ spoken of in one place in Isaiah? .
  8. 8.Where are these words found? .
  9. 9.In what other way did Paul show from the Scriptures the resurrection of Christ? .
  10. 10.What glorious gift is assured by the resurrection of Christ? .
  11. 11.To what does the apostle Peter say that we have been begotten by the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead? .
  12. 12.But in what condition only can we have a hope of that inheritance? .
  13. 13.Then what must we know of the resurrection of Christ, in order to have this hope? ; .
  14. 14.How can we know the power of his resurrection? ; .
  15. 15.With what exhortation did Paul close his discourse? .
  16. 16.What request followed? .
  17. 17.What did Paul and Barnabas do after the meeting? .
