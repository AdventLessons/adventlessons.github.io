Lesson 2 · October 8, 1892

Dorcas Raised to Life.—Acts 9:32-43

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(Memory Verses, 33, 34.)

Golden Text.—"This woman was full of good works and almsdeeds which she did." .

  1. 1.Where was Peter when we last heard of him? .
  2. 2.To what place did he finally come? .
  3. 3.What afflicted person did he find there? .
  4. 4.What did Peter say to him? and what was the result? .
  5. 5.What was the effect of this miracle? .
  6. 6.What pious woman lived at Joppa? .
  7. 7.Upon her death, what did the disciples do? .
  8. 8.What did Peter find on his arrival at Joppa? .
  9. 9.What did he do? , first part.
  10. 10.What was the result? .
  11. 11.How were many affected toward the word? .
  12. 12.To what miracle of Jesus' was this one similar? .
  13. 13.What signs did Jesus say should go with believers? .
  14. 14.How great works did he say should be done by those who believe on him? .
  15. 15.Is this promise limited to believers in any particular age?

Notes

Jesus said, "These signs shall follow them that believe," naming several. And again, "Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works than these shall he do; because I go unto my Father." What greater works can be done it is impossible to say; but we have the word of Christ that they shall be done. Those who ask, "Why don't we see some miracles, if they are to be in the church till the end of time?" do not realize that in every age the gifts of the Spirit have been manifested in the church, although often to a limited extent. But if it is asked why miracles are not so common now as in the first century, we can only reply by pointing to the conditions. When the same devotion, unselfishness, faith, and zeal are seen among believers, then the same power may be expected to be seen in the church.
