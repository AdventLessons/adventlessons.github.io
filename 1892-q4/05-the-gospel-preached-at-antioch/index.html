Lesson 5 · October 29, 1892

The Gospel Preached at Antioch.—Acts 11:19-30

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(Memory Verses, 21-24.)

Golden Text.—"A great number believed, and turned unto the Lord." .

  1. 1.What took place immediately upon the death of Stephen? .
  2. 2.To whom did they preach the word? .
  3. 3.What different course did the disciples from Cyprus and Cyrene pursue? .
  4. 4.What was the result? .
  5. 5.When the news of this came to the church in Jerusalem, what did they do? .
  6. 6.What did Barnabas do? .
  7. 7.What was the result? .
  8. 8.What is further said of his work and unselfishness? .
  9. 9.How long did they two continue to preach in Antioch? .
  10. 10.For what is Antioch noted in the history of the gospel?—, last part.
  11. 11.While Saul and Barnabas were there, what was prophetically declared to the church? .
  12. 12.What did the Christians at Antioch determine to do? .
  13. 13.How was this resolution carried out? .
  14. 14.What exhortation should the church always have in mind? .
  15. 15.What was the apostle Paul's practice? .
