Lesson 4 · April 22, 1893

Looking for Christ

PDFShare
  1. 1.Review questions:—

Notes

(a) What three points in the work of redemption have we considered?

(b) How and when will the work of redemption be complete?

(c) Give three texts that speak of the restored dominion.

  1. 2.When did Peter say the restoration of all things would take place? Acts 3:20, 21.
  2. 3.Who does he say has spoken of this time? Verse 21, last clause.
  3. 4.What promise was made to Abraham? Gen. 17:7, 8.
  4. 5.Who are heirs of this promise made to Abraham? Heb. 11:9.
  5. 6.Did they receive the promise while they were living? Verse 13.
  6. 7.For what did they look? Verses 10, 16.
  7. 8.To what time did Job look for final deliverance? Job 19:25-27.
  8. 9.What was revealed to Balaam when he was seeking to prophesy against Israel? Num. 24:17, 19.
  9. 10.What does the Psalmist say about the coming of the Lord? Ps. 50:3-6; 97:3-6; 37:10, 11, 29.
  10. 11.What warning is given by Zephaniah? Zeph. 2:3.
  11. 12.Why were they commanded to seek the Lord? Verses 1, 2.
  12. 13.To what point in the plan of redemption did all the prophets look, and why? See note 1.
  13. 14.What did Jesus say his people should do after they saw certain signs in the heavens? Luke 21:28.
  14. 15.What does Paul say of the coming of the Lord? Heb. 9:28.
  15. 16.Why should the Bible writers say so much about the coming of the Lord, when he would not come for so many hundred years after they wrote? See note 2.
  16. 17.What admonition did Jesus leave for his people? Mark 13:35-37.
  17. 18.What effect should this theme have on all believers? 1 Thess. 4:18.
  18. 19.How can we comfort one another? Heb. 10:25.

Notes

1. Peter said that the time of restoration of all things was spoken of by all the prophets since the world began. By carefully studying the prophetic scriptures, it will be noticed that the Lord very frequently reminds his people of a judgment day when the Lord would cut off the wicked and give the faithful an inheritance in the new earth.

2. The Scriptures everywhere teach that God hath appointed a judgment day (Acts 17:31), when every man shall give an account of all that he has done (Eccl. 12:14; 2 Cor. 5:10). They also teach that all the dead are to be raised from their graves (John 5:28, 29), to receive their rewards, and that these great events are connected with the second coming of the Lord (2 Tim. 4:1; Rev. 22:12).
