Lesson 22 · June 1, 1889

The Rebellion of Korah

PDFShare
  1. 1.What instance have we already had of envy because of the exalted position of Moses?
  2. 2.How did God regard that manifestation?
  3. 3.How was rebellion again fomented? Num. 16:1-3.
  4. 4.Against whom were they really rebelling? Verses 9-11.
  5. 5.When Moses sent for the conspirators, what did they reply? Verses 12-14.
  6. 6.What false accusation did they bring against Moses?—Ib.
  7. 7.How did Moses repel their charge? Verse 15.
  8. 8.What did the Lord tell the people to do? Verses 23, 24.
  9. 9.Why was this order given? Verse 26.
  10. 10.What did Moses say the Lord would do to test the truth of the charges that had been brought against him? Verses 28-30.
  11. 11.What was done to Korah, Dathan, and Abiram? Verses 31-33.
  12. 12.What was done to their fellow-conspirators? Verse 35.
  13. 13.What was done to keep this rebellion and its result in mind, and as a warning to others? Verses 37-40.
  14. 14.Notwithstanding the fate of the rebellious princes, what wicked charge did the people still bring against Moses and Aaron? Verse 41.
  15. 15.What did the Lord say to this? Verses 44, 45.
  16. 16.How quickly was the judgment of God visited upon them? Verses 46, 47.
  17. 17.How many perished in this plague? Verse 49.
  18. 18.How can you account for such daring rebellion as was manifested by the people?

Notes

Such presumptuous rebellion as was manifested by the people on this occasion, can be accounted for only by saying that it was caused by the blindness of unbelief. Their hearts had been hardened by the deceitfulness of sin, until they were ready to charge God to his face with injustice. Such Heaven-daring rebellion seems almost incredible, yet it is often equaled in our day. The Israelites had the same human nature that men have nowadays, and were not sinners above all that have ever lived.
