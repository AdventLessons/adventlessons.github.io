Lesson 4 · January 30, 1886

The Cleansing of the Sanctuary

PDFShare

[Note to the Student.—Do not consider the lesson learned until you can give at least the substance of every text, with the correct reference for each. The references in black letters indicate those texts that should be committed to memory. A little diligent application each day will enable you to learn them, although this need not be considered a test of scholarship.]

  1. 1.Describe the daily round of service in the sanctuary. Ex. 29:38, 41.
  2. 2.What other services were performed from time to time? Lev., chapters 4 and 23.
  3. 3.When did the yearly service take place? Lev. 23:27.
  4. 4.What was that day called? Verse 28.
  5. 5.What was the object of this day of atonement? Lev. 16:30.
  6. 6.With what was the atonement made? Lev. 17:11.
  7. 7.What besides the people was cleansed? Lev. 16:33.
  8. 8.How were the people required to observe this day? Lev. 23:27-31. (See note.)
  9. 9.What offering was selected with which to make the atonement? Lev. 16:7, 8.
  10. 10.What is the penalty for breaking the law of God? Eze. 18:20.
  11. 11.Which goat was slain? Lev. 16:9.
  12. 12.Why did the goat have to suffer this penalty?—Since the sins of the people rested upon him, he had to receive the penalty of God's broken law.
  13. 13.What did the priest do with the blood of the Lord's goat? Verse 15.
  14. 14.What does the blood represent? Lev. 17:11.
  15. 15.Why was the blood of these sin-offerings sprinkled upon the mercy-seat?—To show that the law deposited beneath it demanded the life of those who had broken that law.
  16. 16.What was the mercy-seat?
  17. 17.What was beneath it? Lev. 16:13.
  18. 18.What solemn work did the priest perform when he returned from the most holy place into the first apartment called in this chapter the "tabernacle of the congregation"? Lev. 16:18, 19.
  19. 19.How was this work accomplished?
  20. 20.For what did the priest first make an atonement on this tenth day of the seventh month? Lev. 16:11-14.
  21. 21.What was the next step in the important services of the day? Verses 15, 16.
  22. 22.What work of cleansing was performed in the first apartment? Verses 18, 19.
  23. 23.After the high priest had thus made an end of reconciling, or purifying, both holy places and the altar of incense, what ceremony did he perform at the door of the tabernacle? Verses 20, 21.
  24. 24.What name was given to this goat that was kept alive? Verse 10, (also margin of verse 8).
  25. 25.What was finally done with this goat? Verses 21, 22.
  26. 26.What was thus in a figure fully accomplished?—The cleansing of the sanctuary by removing the sins of the people from it.
  27. 27.Did this service actually take away sin? Heb. 10:3, 4.

Notes

Afflicted.—For the expression in Lev. 23:29, "shall be afflicted," the Septuagint has … (tapeinothesetai). Greenfield defines the verb as follows: "To humble, bring low... to humble one's self, exhibit humility of mind and deportment." Matt. 18:4; 23:12.
