Lesson 8 · February 27, 1886

The Great Prophetic Period

PDFShare
  1. 1.In the vision of Daniel 8, what symbols were presented to the prophet?
  2. 2.What interest did Daniel manifest in this vision? Verse 15.
  3. 3.Who was sent to make him understand the meaning of the vision? Verse 16.
  4. 4.What verses record the angel's explanation of the first symbol?
  5. 5.What verses record his explanation of the second symbol? The third?
  6. 6.Why did he not explain the application of the symbolic time spoken of in the fourteenth verse? Verse 27, first clause.
  7. 7.When the prophet had recovered from his sickness, how did he regard the vision? Verse 27, last part.
  8. 8.Is there any intimation that he did not understand the part that had been explained to him?
  9. 9.How long was it before Gabriel returned to complete the work of making Daniel understand the vision?—About 15 years. (See margin.)
  10. 10.How was Daniel employed when the angel thus returned to him? Dan. 9:20, 21.
  11. 11.What did Gabriel say he had now come to do? Verse 22.
  12. 12.What did he tell Daniel to consider? Verse 23, last part.
  13. 13.What vision must this have been?
  14. 14.Why was he to consider the vision?—That he might "understand the matter" that the angel was about to explain. Verse 23.
  15. 15.What part of the former vision had been left unexplained? Dan. 8:14.
  16. 16.In explanation of this point, what does Gabriel first introduce? Dan. 9:24.
  17. 17.How much literal time is denoted by the seventy weeks?—All agree that a prophetic day stands for a literal year. See also Eze. 4:5, 6; and Num. 14:34.
  18. 18.What important things were to be accomplished during the period thus allotted to the Jews? Dan. 9:24.
  19. 19.What do we understand by the finishing of the transgression? (See note.)
  20. 20.What is meant by making an end of sin, and by making reconciliation for iniquity?
  21. 21.What is the everlasting righteousness that was to be brought in?
  22. 22.In what sense were the vision and the prophecy to be sealed up?
  23. 23.What do we understand by the anointing of the most Holy?

Notes

To finish the transgression.—The Jews were to fill up the measure of their iniquity by rejecting and crucifying the Messiah; they would then no longer be his peculiar people, or host. Read Matt. 21:38-43; 23:32-38; 27:25.

Making an end of sin.—This probably means to make an end of sin-offerings, which were to be abolished by the death of Christ. See Dan. 9:27 (cause the sacrifice and oblation to cease); read also Heb. 9:10. The sacrifice of Christ, in making an end of sin-offering, would also make reconciliation for iniquity, since it would satisfy the demands of the broken law, for all who would seek pardon through it.

Everlasting righteousness.—This must mean the righteousness of Christ,—that righteousness by which he was enabled to make an atonement for sin, and which, through faith, may be imputed to the penitent believer.

To seal up the vision.—To seal sometimes means to fulfill; to establish. See Webster. During the seventy weeks a part of the vision was exactly fulfilled, and this establishes and makes sure the fulfillment of the whole.

To anoint the most Holy.—This is supposed to refer to the anointing of the heavenly sanctuary, preparatory to opening the services there.

Summary.—The angel assured Daniel that 490 years of the 2300 should be given to the Jews; and that during that period they would fill up the measure of their iniquity; Christ would put an end to sin-offering by offering himself, thus making reconciliation for iniquity, and bringing in everlasting righteousness; the events of this period would so strikingly fulfill the first part of the prophecy as to make its complete fulfillment a certainty, and the way would be prepared for Christ, the crucified, to enter upon his ministry in the heavenly sanctuary,—the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man.
