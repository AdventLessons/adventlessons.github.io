Lesson 3 · January 23, 1886

Services of the Earthly Sanctuary

PDFShare
  1. 1.Give a brief description of the sanctuary built by Moses.
  2. 2.Name the furniture of the holy place, and give its arrangement.
  3. 3.Describe the furniture of the most holy place.
  4. 4.Where was the altar of burnt-offering located? Ex. 40:6.
  5. 5.What was its use? Ex. 40:29.
  6. 6.What stood between this altar and the sanctuary? Ex. 30:18.
  7. 7.By what general name were the people of God designated at this time? Hosea 11:1.
  8. 8.What was the origin of this title? Gen. 32:28.
  9. 9.Into how many tribes was Israel divided? Ex. 1:1-5; Gen. 49:28.
  10. 10.Who were to minister in the sanctuary? Heb. 8:4, 5.
  11. 11.What tribe was selected to do the service of the tabernacle? Num. 3:5-7.
  12. 12.What particular family of this tribe was chosen to fill the sacred office of the priesthood? Num. 3:10.
  13. 13.Describe the regular daily offering and service of the sanctuary. Ex. 29:38-41; 30:7, 8.
  14. 14.How did the offerings on the Sabbath differ from those of other days? Num. 28:9, 10.
  15. 15.When an individual sinned, what was he required to do? Lev. 4:27, 28.
  16. 16.What disposition was made of the blood? Lev. 4:30.
  17. 17.Where did this altar stand?
  18. 18.When a priest sinned, what was required of him? Lev. 4:3, 4.
  19. 19.What was done with the blood of his offering? Lev. 4:5-7.
  20. 20.State the chief difference between these two offerings.
  21. 21.When a priest or the whole congregation sinned, how were their sins transferred to the sanctuary? Lev. 4:6, 17.
  22. 22.When one of the common people sinned, was the blood of his offering taken into the sanctuary? Lev. 4:30.
  23. 23.Then how were his sins transferred to the sanctuary? Lev. 10:17, 18.
  24. 24.Were the priests to partake of all the sin-offerings? Lev. 6:30.
  25. 25.Where were all these services performed? Ex. 29:42; Lev. 4:17, 18; Heb. 9:6.
  26. 26.What change was made in the continual round of service which we have described? Heb. 9:7.
