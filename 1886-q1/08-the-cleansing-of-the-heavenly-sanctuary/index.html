Lesson 7 · February 20, 1886

The Cleansing of the Heavenly Sanctuary

PDFShare

[Note to the Student.—Do not consider the lesson learned until you can give at least the substance of every text, with the correct reference for each. The references in black letters indicate those texts that should be committed to memory. A little diligent application each day will enable you to learn them, although this need not be considered a test of scholarship.]

  1. 1.What were the closing acts of the earthly priest in the yearly round of service?
  2. 2.Of what did this cleansing consist?
  3. 3.What were the people required to do on the day of atonement?
  4. 4.Was an offering made on that day besides the atonement offering? Num. 29:7-11.
  5. 5.Will the "true tabernacle," or heavenly sanctuary, be cleansed? Heb. 9:23.
  6. 6.From what was the earthly sanctuary cleansed? Lev. 16:30, 33.
  7. 7.How did the earthly sanctuary stand related to the heavenly? Heb. 9:9.
  8. 8.Then from what will the heavenly sanctuary be cleansed? Heb. 9:26.
  9. 9.How did the sins of Israel get into the sanctuary? Lev. 4:13-18; 10:17, 18.
  10. 10.How do our sins get into the heavenly sanctuary?—Christ, our great sin-offering, takes them upon himself, and bears them into the sanctuary.
  11. 11.In what manner do they find a lodgment there? Jer. 2:22.
  12. 12.Are sins recorded in heaven? Rev. 20:12.
  13. 13.How, in figure, did ancient Israel receive forgiveness of their sins? Num. 15:24-26.
  14. 14.How do we receive forgiveness of our sins? Col. 1:14.
  15. 15.Anciently, when sins were forgiven, were they removed from the sanctuary?
  16. 16.When was this done? Lev. 16:29, 30, 34.
  17. 17.What was this removing of sins?—Cleansing the sanctuary. Lev. 16:30, 33.
  18. 18.How does our Saviour show that our sins are not blotted out when they are forgiven? Matt. 18:23-35.
  19. 19.Since the heavenly sanctuary is to be cleansed from sin, what must be done with the sins recorded there? Acts 3:19.
  20. 20.The removal of sins from the earthly sanctuary by the high priest, corresponded to what act of our High Priest in the heavenly sanctuary?
  21. 21.To what, then, is the blotting out of sins equivalent? Heb. 9:23.
  22. 22.When will the cleansing of the heavenly sanctuary begin? Dan. 8:14.
