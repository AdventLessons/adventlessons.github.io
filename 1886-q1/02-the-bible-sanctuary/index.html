Lesson 1 · January 9, 1886

The Bible Sanctuary

PDFShare

[Note to the Student.—Do not consider the lesson learned until you can give at least the substance of every text, with the correct reference for each. The references in black letters indicate those texts that should be committed to memory. A little diligent application each day will enable you to learn them, although this need not be considered a test of scholarship.]

  1. 1.What command did the Lord give Moses in Exodus 25:8?
  2. 2.Did Moses have it in his heart to do such a work as this? Ex. 15:2.
  3. 3.Why did the Lord desire a sanctuary built?
  4. 4.What is the meaning of the term sanctuary? (See note.)
  5. 5.What method of instruction did the Lord use in teaching Moses how to make the sanctuary? Ex. 25:9, 40.
  6. 6.Name the materials used in its construction. Verses 3-7.
  7. 7.How were these materials to be furnished? Ver. 2.
  8. 8.From whom were these offerings to be accepted?
  9. 9.How did the people respond to the call? Ex. 36:5-7.
  10. 10.How does God regard such a spirit in this dispensation? 2 Cor. 9:7.
  11. 11.Who did the Lord say should perform the work of making this structure? Ex. 35:10.
  12. 12.Whom did he especially call by name? Ex. 31:1-6.
  13. 13.What other qualification did these men possess besides being skillful workmen? Ex. 35:34.
  14. 14.What were the dimensions of this building? Ex. 26:15-23; 1 Kings 6:20. (See note.)
  15. 15.Describe the roof or coverings of the sanctuary. Ex. 26:1-14.
  16. 16.How were the walls of this structure adorned? Ex. 26:1; 36:8.
  17. 17.What work corresponding to this was seen in the temple built by Solomon? 1 Kings 6:29.
  18. 18.How many apartments had it? Heb. 9:2-4.
  19. 19.What were these two apartments called? Ex. 26:33.
  20. 20.What divided them?
  21. 21.How does the apostle Paul speak of this vail? Heb. 9:3.
  22. 22.What purpose did the first veil serve? Ex. 26:36. (See note.)

Notes

Sanctuary.—A sacred place; a consecrated spot; a holy inviolable site.—Webster.

Dimensions.—It will be noticed there were six boards across the end of the sanctuary, standing upright. On the lower end of each board were two tenons, and these were inserted in sockets of silver. These boards were one and a half cubits wide, making nine cubits for the six boards; then there were two more boards spoken of "for the corners of the tabernacle in the two sides." It is evident that these corner boards were so arranged and of such a width as to make the width of the sanctuary ten cubits. The temple erected by Solomon, though it was on a much larger scale, was built from the same pattern that Moses saw; and we know that the length of the temple was just three times as great as its width, and that the Most Holy place was a perfect cube. See 1 Kings 6, and 1 Chron. 28:11-20.

"According to the pattern." (Ex. 25:9, 40.) We have in these words a beautiful illustration as well as a divine warrant, for object teaching. The earnest teacher will catch the idea.

Hanging.—The same Greek word used for veil in Hebrews 9:3, is also used in the Septuagint in Exodus 26:36 where it is translated "hanging." This Greek word is defined by Robinson as follows: "A covering, veil which hangs down. In Septuagint, a vail, curtain, of the tabernacle and temple, of which there were two; viz., one at the entrance of the outer sanctuary (Ex. 26:36; 40:5); and the other before the holy of holies, separating it from the outer sanctuary. Ex. 26:31; 27:21; 40:3."
