Lesson 6 · February 13, 1886

The Heavenly Sanctuary

PDFShare

[Note to the Student.—Do not consider the lesson learned until you can give at least the substance of every text, with the correct reference for each. The references in black letters indicate those texts that should be committed to memory. A little diligent application each day will enable you to learn them, although this need not be considered a test of scholarship.]

  1. 1.Were sins actually removed by the ceremonies and offerings connected with the earthly sanctuary? Heb. 10:3, 4.
  2. 2.What does Paul say of the service of the earthly priests? Heb. 8:5.
  3. 3.What does he call the earthly sanctuary? Heb. 9:8, 9.
  4. 4.Of what was it a figure, or pattern? Heb. 9:23.
  5. 5.Then is there a real sanctuary in heaven? Heb. 8:1, 2.
  6. 6.Does the heavenly sanctuary have two holy places? Heb. 9:24.
  7. 7.Who is the high priest of this sanctuary? Heb. 4:14.
  8. 8.Did he minister there while the first or earthly tabernacle was standing? Heb. 9:8. (See note.)
  9. 9.What position did our High Priest take when he left this earth? Heb. 10:12; 8:1.
  10. 10.What office does he there fill? Heb. 8:2.
  11. 11.In what part of the earthly sanctuary were the altar of incense and the golden candlestick placed?
  12. 12.Where was the throne of God seen by John? Rev. 4:5; 8:3.
  13. 13.Then in which apartment did our Lord begin his priestly work?
  14. 14.What did the second apartment of the earthly sanctuary contain?
  15. 15.What did John behold in the heavenly temple? Rev. 11:19.

Notes

Holiest of all.—The word rendered "holiest of all," is in the Greek in the plural number, and should be rendered "holy places," referring, of course, to the true holy places, or tabernacle, in heaven. This text, therefore, proves that there was no ministry by Christ in any part of the heavenly sanctuary, until the close of the typical work in the sanctuary on the earth.
