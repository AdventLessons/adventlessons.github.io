Lesson 18 · January 2, 1886

The Kingdom of Grace

PDFShare

[Note to the Student.—Do not consider the lesson learned until you can give at least the substance of every text, with the correct reference for each. The references in black letters indicate those texts that should be committed to memory. A little diligent application each day will enable you to learn them, although this need not be considered a test of scholarship.]

  1. 1.For what are we to look, after the fires of the last day have swept away all traces of the curse? 2 Pet. 3:13.
  2. 2.Where is this promise found? Isaiah 65:17; 66:22.
  3. 3.What does John say about it? Rev. 21:1.
  4. 4.Who will accompany the saints when they return to the earth? Jude 14.
  5. 5.What descends from heaven with them? Rev. 21:2.
  6. 6.What will be the condition of those who are thus brought back to earth by the King of glory? Rev. 5:10; 21:3, 4.
  7. 7.Who will be assembled in the New Jerusalem? Heb. 12:22-24.
  8. 8.How long will the saints remain in this happy state? Dan. 7:14, 18, 27.
  9. 9.Will the Sabbath be recognized in the new earth? Isaiah 66:23.
  10. 10.What throne will be there? Rev. 22:3.
  11. 11.In what two ways is the throne of God sometimes spoken of in the Scriptures? Heb. 4:16; Matt. 25:31.
  12. 12.What does a throne represent?—The kingdom, and the government of the kingdom.
  13. 13.Why are different names applied to the throne of heaven?—Because the relation between mankind and the kingdom of heaven is different in this life from what it will be in the life to come.
  14. 14.In what relation are we now?—We belong to a race that is in rebellion against the government of heaven.
  15. 15.How must we be saved, if saved at all, from the destruction that awaits the wicked?—By the grace of God, who has given his Son to die for us.
  16. 16.What do we understand by grace, as spoken of here?—Unmerited favor.
  17. 17.Why may the heavenly throne be fitly called the throne of grace?—Because it is the throne from which grace proceeds.
  18. 18.What do we understand by the kingdom of grace?—The entire plan for the salvation of men by grace, through faith in Jesus Christ.
  19. 19.What fitness is there in applying the term "kingdom of grace," or "kingdom of heaven," to the work of saving men?—The King of heaven and all his subjects are engaged in that work.
  20. 20.What relation exists between the kingdom of grace and the kingdom of glory?—The former prepares subjects for the latter.
  21. 21.What conditions does the King of heaven make for us who have been in rebellion against his government?—We are to renounce our allegiance to Satan, and by faith and obedience prove our loyalty to our heavenly King until he shall come to take us to the kingdom of glory.
  22. 22.Will the kingdom of grace exist after the kingdom of glory is established?
  23. 23.Which of these kingdoms did we learn about in lesson 17?
  24. 24.As found in the New Testament, how is the term "kingdom of God," or "kingdom of heaven," to be understood?—Sometimes it means the kingdom of grace, while in other passages it means the kingdom of glory.
  25. 25.When was the kingdom of grace established?—When salvation by grace, through faith in a promised Saviour, was first offered to sinful man.
