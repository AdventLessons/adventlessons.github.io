Lesson 2 · January 16, 1886

The Apartments of the Earthly Sanctuary

PDFShare

[Note to the Student.—Do not consider the lesson learned until you can give at least the substance of every text, with the correct reference for each. The references in black letters indicate those texts that should be committed to memory. A little diligent application each day will enable you to learn them, although this need not be considered a test of scholarship.]

  1. 1.After the general instruction in regard to building the sanctuary, what seems to have been the first article of its furniture that Moses was instructed to make? Ex. 25:10.
  2. 2.Where was this ark placed? Ex. 26:34.
  3. 3.Describe the mercy seat, and give its position. Ex. 25:17-21.
  4. 4.What was the testimony? Ex. 31:18.
  5. 5.What was written upon these tables? Ex. 34:28.
  6. 6.Where was this testimony placed? Ex. 25:21.
  7. 7.… placed? Ex. 30:6.
  8. 8.For what was this altar used? Ex. 30:7, 8.
  9. 9.What were the people doing without, while the priest was burning incense within, the sanctuary? Luke 1:10.
  10. 10.What beautiful allusion does David make to this service? Ps. 141:2.
  11. 11.What other articles of furniture did the first apartment or holy place, contain? Ex. 25:23, 31.
  12. 12.For what was the table used? Ex. 25:30.
  13. 13.How did Moses know how to make all these things? Ex. 25:40.
  14. 14.Of what was this sanctuary a pattern, or figure? Heb. 9:24.
  15. 15.Why was this structure erected? Ex. 25:8.
  16. 16.How was God's presence manifested in this sanctuary? Lev. 16:2.
  17. 17.What place in the sanctuary did the Lord especially choose where he would thus manifest himself? Ex. 25:22.
  18. 18.Did he ever meet with them in the first apartment? Ex. 29:42, 43.
