Lesson 12 · March 19, 1898

Meeting with the Elders of Ephesus

(.)

  1. 1.Give an account of the night meeting at Troas.
  2. 2.How did it happen that a meeting was held at that time?
  3. 3.State the course of the journey from that point onward.
  4. 4.From what place did Paul send for the elders of the church at Ephesus to come to him?
  5. 5.When they had come to him, of what did he remind them? .
  6. 6.What could he say of his service to the Lord? .
  7. 7.How faithful had he been in the ministry? Where had he taught, and whom? .
  8. 8.What had been the burden of his testimony to them? .
  9. 9.What did he say of his present condition and his journey? Of what was he ignorant? .
  10. 10.Of what thing was he nevertheless sure? What assurance had he of this? .
  11. 11.How did this prospect affect him? How did he hold his life? What was the sole object of his life? .
  12. 12.What did he say as to their meeting again? .
  13. 13.From what did he say he was clear? .
  14. 14.How was this? .
  15. 15.In view of all this, what exhortation did the apostle give? What other title did he give to the church? What relation did he say the elders sustained to it? Who had placed them in that position? What were they to do? How did he indicate the value of the church? .
  16. 16.What made this exhortation so necessary? .
  17. 17.What gave special point to the exhortation, "Take heed unto yourselves"? .
  18. 18.What admonition does the apostle therefore repeat? What did he call upon the elders to remember? .
  19. 19.In conclusion, to what did he commend them? What is the Word able to do? .
  20. 20.What did Paul say of his own unselfishness? .
  21. 21.How disinterestedly had he labored? .
  22. 22.What had he thereby showed them? What words of the Lord Jesus are to be remembered? .
  23. 23.When Paul had thus spoken, what did he do? .
  24. 24.How were they all affected? .
  25. 25.What indicates the great affection the brethren had for Paul, and their appreciation of his labors? What last act of kindness did they do? .

Additional Questions and Suggestions

  1. 1.Nothing more sublime and touching can be found than this account and the words of the apostle as he talked, with the certainty of persecution before him. The instruction is most valuable, and so concise that not a single word can be passed lightly over. Special thought and study should be given to it, so that every sentence may be fixed in the mind exactly as the apostle spoke it.
  2. 2.Compare with the Revised Version, and note how the word "temptations" is used in its original signification of "trials."
