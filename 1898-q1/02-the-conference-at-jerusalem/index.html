Lesson 2 · January 8, 1898

The Conference at Jerusalem

PDFShare

(Acts 14:23 to 15:12.)

  1. 1.Name in their order the places in which Paul and Barnabas labored after they were sent out together, up to the point where the last lesson left them.
  2. 2.What did they do for every congregation of believers? In whose care did they leave them? Acts 14:23.
  3. 3.Describe the course which the apostles took back to Antioch. Verses 24-26.
  4. 4.What testimony is given to the faithfulness of their labors? Verse 26.
  5. 5.When they arrived in Antioch, what did they do? Verse 27.
  6. 6.Where did they continue to labor? Verse 28.
  7. 7.Who came to Antioch while Paul and Barnabas were there? From what place did they come? What did they begin to teach the brethren? Chapter 15:1.
  8. 8.How did Paul and Barnabas regard their teaching? What did the brethren finally decide? Verse 2.
  9. 9.What is said of the journey to Jerusalem? What did the apostles do on the way? With what result? Verse 3.
  10. 10.How were they received at Jerusalem? What did they do there? Verse 4.
  11. 11.Who offered objections? What did they say was necessary? Verse 5.
  12. 12.What did the apostles and elders therefore do? Verse 6.
  13. 13.What was the nature of the meeting at the first? Who afterwards gave important testimony? To what did Peter first refer? For what had God a long time before chosen Peter? Verse 7.
  14. 14.What did God do as the Gentiles believed? What had He given them? Verse 8.
  15. 15.How had God treated the Gentiles as compared with the Jews? (What does God know which caused Him to treat them thus? Verse 8.) What did He do for their hearts? How did He purify them? Verse 9.
  16. 16.What question did Peter therefore put to the assembly? In what way could they tempt God? Verse 10.
  17. 17.What did Peter say that he believed would be accomplished by the grace of God? Verse 11.
  18. 18.To whom did the assembly then give attention? What did Barnabas and Paul declare? Verse 12.

Additional Questions and Suggestions

  1. 1."Certain men came down from Judea." Were these brethren representatives of the believers in Judea? See verse 24, especially in the Revised Version.
  2. 2.What kind of men were they? Compare Gal. 2:4.
  3. 3.What did these false brethren say was necessary? For what purpose did they claim that circumcision was necessary? Were they preaching salvation by faith or by works? What were they in reality teaching and doing? Gal. 1:6, 7; 2:4.
  4. 4."Paul and Barnabas had no small dissension and disputation with them." Why was this? Gal. 2:5.
  5. 5.The brethren "determined that Paul and Barnabas, and certain other of them, should go up to Jerusalem." Was it in consequence of this decision of the brethren that Paul went up to Jerusalem at this time? See Gal. 2:2.
  6. 6.Who was one of the brethren who accompanied Paul? Gal. 2:1.
  7. 7.As they went they declared "the conversion of the Gentiles; and they caused great joy unto all the brethren." Note that instead of dwelling on the new opposition that had arisen, they proclaimed "good tidings of great joy."
  8. 8."God, which knoweth the hearts, . . . put no difference between us and them, purifying their hearts by faith." What can we learn from this as to the nature of the controversy? What all-important question was involved?
