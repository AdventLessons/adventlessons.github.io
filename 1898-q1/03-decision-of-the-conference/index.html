Lesson 3 · January 15, 1898

Decision of the Conference

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(.)

  1. 1.Relate in outline the circumstances of Paul's first missionary journey.
  2. 2.Tell what took place after his return.
  3. 3.What was the occasion of his visit to Jerusalem?
  4. 4.What was the real point at issue?
  5. 5.Who was the first speaker of importance at the meeting?
  6. 6.Relate the substance of his remarks.
  7. 7.Who next spoke and to what end?
  8. 8.After they had finished, who summed up the matter? .
  9. 9.To whose speech did he refer? What did he say that God had done? .
  10. 10.With what did he say this was in harmony? .
  11. 11.From what prophet did he then quote?
  12. 12.Repeat the portion quoted. .
  13. 13.What did God say by that prophet that He would do? What would the Lord build up? .
  14. 14.Since the preaching of the gospel to the Gentiles was in the line of this prophecy, of what is that work a building up?
  15. 15.Why would the Lord thus build up the tabernacle of David? .
  16. 16.How long had God had this purpose in mind? .
  17. 17.In view of all this what did James conclude? .
  18. 18.What did he say they would write to them? .
  19. 19.Why was it not necessary for them to specify all the details of the law? .
  20. 20.What did the whole church, with the apostles and elders, then decide to do? .
  21. 21.What did they send by these brethren? .
  22. 22.Give the substance of the letter. .
  23. 23.What did the letter say of those who had been teaching circumcision as necessary to salvation? What had been the result of their teaching? .
  24. 24.What did the brethren say of the men by whom the letters were sent? .
  25. 25.What authority did they give for the decision rendered? .
  26. 26.After they were dismissed, where did the messengers first go, and what did they do? .
  27. 27.How did the reading of the epistle affect the church? .
  28. 28.What did Judas and Silas do? What special gift did they have? .
  29. 29.Having delivered their message what did these brethren do? .
  30. 30.Who remained behind? .
  31. 31.What did Paul and Barnabas do? What was their continued occupation? .

Additional Questions and Suggestions

  1. 1.What did the brethren assembled say that the self-appointed teachers had done to the brethren which were of the Gentiles? .
  2. 2.What did Paul afterward write of the same ones? .
  3. 3.What were they really doing in speaking these troublesome words?
  4. 4.And what was the natural effect upon the souls of the hearers? .
  5. 5.How serious a matter is it to do such work? .
  6. 6.How many of the commandments are more or less directly involved in the four things mentioned by the assembly at Jerusalem?
  7. 7.Which of the commandments are not referred to? Is there any hint in the letter about the necessity of obedience to parents, refraining from taking the name of God in vain, or lying and stealing? Are we therefore to conclude that there is no importance attached to these things?
