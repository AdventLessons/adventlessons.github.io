Lesson 4 · October 27, 1906

The Captain of Our Salvation

PDFShare

Questions.

  1. 1.By what title is Jesus designated in His relation to the plan of salvation? Heb. 2:10.
  2. 2.What is He to all who obey Him? Heb. 5:9.
  3. 3.Name one person who was said to be waiting for the advent of this Captain and Author of salvation. Luke 2:25.
  4. 4.What had been revealed to Him by the Holy Spirit? Luke 2:26.
  5. 5.Being directed to the temple at the exact moment of our Saviour's presentation, what did he say? Verses 30-32.
  6. 6.What was the Saviour called more than 500 years before He was born into the human family? Dan. 7:13. Note 1.
  7. 7.Why did He become the Son of Man? Heb. 2:17.
  8. 8.Unto whom was He born? Isa. 9:6. What titles are here ascribed to Him?
  9. 9.What record have we of His pre-existence? Gen. 1:26; Micah 5:2; John 17:5.
  10. 10.What testimony is given of His agency in creation? John 1:1-3; Col. 1:15, 16; Heb. 1:1, 2.
  11. 11.What exhibition of creative power is seen in connection with the plan of salvation? Eph. 2:10; 4:22-24.
  12. 12.What evidence did He give of creative power revealed through Him while in the world? Note 2.
  13. 13.To what extent is He able to bring complete salvation to man? Heb. 7:25.
  14. 14.Of what did He speak, by His Spirit, to the prophets before He become man? 1 Peter 1:9-11.
  15. 15.What was the Saviour's purpose in coming into the world? 1 Tim. 1:15; Matt. 1:21.
  16. 16.For what does He come the second time? Heb. 9:28.
  17. 17.What will be said in that day by those who are waiting for him? Isa. 25:9.
  18. 18.What will the wicked do and say? Rev. 6:14-17; Isa. 2:18-21.

Notes

1. The Spirit called Christ the "Son of Man" through the prophet 500 years before He was born into the human family, and nearly 2,500 years prior to the actual fulfilment of the prophecy in anticipation of His being born "unto us."

2. Christ gave abundant evidence of His creative power while in the world, by healing the lame and those who had the leprosy; restoring sight to the blind, and hearing to the deaf; by supplying bread to the multitude, turning water into wine, and raising the dead.
