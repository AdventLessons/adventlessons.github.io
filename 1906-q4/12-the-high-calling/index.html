Lesson 12 · December 22, 1906

The High Calling

PDFShare

Questions.

  1. 1.What is one thing essential to our salvation? Rom. 10:13, 14.
  2. 2.What thing is of paramount importance with the preacher? Verse 15.—That he be sent of God.
  3. 3.If he is truly sent of God, what is laid upon him? 1 Cor. 9:16.
  4. 4.What should he preach? 2 Tim. 4:1, 2. To what is the Word of the Lord likened? Jer. 23:28, 29.
  5. 5.Whose approval should he seek? 2 Tim. 2:15. Of what should he make full proof? 2 Tim. 4:5.
  6. 6.What is the declared purpose of the ministry? 2 Tim. 2:10.
  7. 7.What solemn responsibility does the Lord lay upon the ministry? Eze. 33:7, 8.
  8. 8.What responsibility will those have to meet to whom the minister delivers God's message? Verse 9.
  9. 9.By delivering God's message, what does the minister become to the two classes who hear him? 2 Cor. 2:15, 16. What makes the difference?
  10. 10.What divine unction should attend his preaching? 1 Cor. 2:4; Acts 10:44.
  11. 11.To this end whom must he preach? 1 Cor. 2:2; 3:11.
  12. 12.How diligent should the ministry be in the exercise of their gift? 2 Tim. 1:6; Acts 6:4.
  13. 13.How should the minister regard himself? 1 Cor. 3:5-9.
  14. 14.How should he be regarded by the church? 1 Tim. 5:17; 1 Thess. 5:12, 13.
  15. 15.How has the Lord ordained that they which preach the Gospel should be supported? 1 Cor. 9:14.
  16. 16.From whom does the commission come, and what promise goes with it? Matt. 28:19, 20.
  17. 17.What promise is for soul-winners? Dan. 12:3.

Notes

It is said of Jesus, that "for the joy that was set before Him," He "endured the cross, despising the shame." That joy is in seeing the blood-bought throng before the throne, "filled with joy unspeakable and full of glory," and of knowing that all this bliss could not have been experienced had it not been for His sacrifice. Isa. 53:11. The faithful minister will share in the joy of his Lord, when he himself stands before the throne and sees around him the vouchers of his ministry, the flock his care has nourished, fed, and saved.
