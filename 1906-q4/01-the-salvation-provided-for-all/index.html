Lesson 1 · October 6, 1906

The Salvation Provided for All

PDFShare

Questions.

  1. 1.What is the necessity for the plan of salvation? Rom. 3:23.
  2. 2.What natural and inevitable result would sin bring upon the sinner, were it not for the means of salvation provided? Rom. 6:23; Rev. 20:14, 15.
  3. 3.What is the meaning of "salvation"? Matt. 1:21. Note.
  4. 4.By whom was this plan devised? John 3:16.
  5. 5.To how many is it proclaimed? Titus 2:11; Rev. 22:17.
  6. 6.What is God declared to be? 1 John 4:8.
  7. 7.What are those who hate God, and reject the plan of salvation, said to love? Prov. 8:35, 36.
  8. 8.What is the nature of the salvation which is assured to the Israel of God? Isa. 45:17.
  9. 9.What is this salvation able to do for those who accept it? Jude 24, 25.
  10. 10.What warning is given to those who "neglect" it? Heb. 2:3.
  11. 11.After so full provision for free salvation, what will be the final experience of those who "neglect" it? Jer. 8:20.
  12. 12.When only is the "accepted time" for receiving salvation? 2 Cor. 6:2; Heb. 3:15.
  13. 13.When is it to be received in all its fulness? Heb. 9:28; 1 Peter 1:7-9.

Notes

Salvation means to save from sin, and from all of its woeful results, of which the "second death" is the ultimate. It is, therefore, a message of life, instead of death, a message of fulness of joy, instead of unutterable woe. This is, in brief, the "Great Salvation." The whole scheme is beyond our comprehension, yet we can joyfully and thankfully avail ourselves of all of its benefits.
