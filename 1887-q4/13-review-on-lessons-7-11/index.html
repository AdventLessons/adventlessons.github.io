Lesson 18 · December 24, 1887

Review on Lessons 7-11

PDFShare
  1. 1.When David was brought nigh unto death, how did he seek help? Ps. 88:2, 3; 13:3; 39:13.
  2. 2.What instruction is given to Christians who are sick or in affliction? Jas. 5:13-16.
  3. 3.How are we encouraged to offer prayer in such cases? Last part of verse 16; Mark 6:13; 16:17, 18.
  4. 4.What instances seem to indicate that sin is a cause of sickness? Num. 12:1-12; Matt. 9:2; John 5:14.
  5. 5.Is it always best that the request for the recovery of the sick should be granted? 2 Sam. 12:15-18; 2 Chron. 32:24, 25.
  6. 6.How may we hope to be guided in such cases? Rom. 8:26, 27.
  7. 7.How did Moses cry out for deliverance, and how was he answered? Ex. 17:4-6.
  8. 8.From what depths of anguish was David delivered in answer to prayer? Ps. 40:12; 69:2; 40:13; 69:15; 18:6; 118:5.
  9. 9.How does God regard the afflicted, the poor, and the needy? Ps. 22:24; 40:17.
  10. 10.How has God shown his great mercy to those who have penitently returned from wandering in sin? Ps. 107:11-14.
  11. 11.What was David's refuge when persecuted by enemies? Ps. 59:1, 2; 64:1, 2.
  12. 12.Describe some remarkable instances of deliverance in answer to prayer.
  13. 13.How should a Christian commit himself to God in times of great danger and distress? Ps. 57:1.
  14. 14.Show that God seems to inspire and answer prayers for personal success. Gen. 24:12; Neh. 1:11; 1 Chron. 4:10.
  15. 15.What generous prayers does David make for the humble who seek God with uprightness of heart? Ps. 40:16; 36:10; 10:12.
  16. 16.What was Paul's custom with reference to praying for the churches which he had raised up? Phil. 1:3-5; Col. 1:9; 1 Thess. 3:10.
  17. 17.Mention some of the blessings which he invoked upon them. 1 Thess. 3:11, 12; 2 Thess. 1:11, 12.
  18. 18.What persistent supplications did Paul urge the brethren to make for him? Eph. 6:18-20; Col. 4:3; 2 Thess. 3:1.
  19. 19.Show that Paul believed that the prayers of the saints could promote his deliverance from persecution, and even aid him in securing final salvation. Rom. 15:30, 31; 2 Thess. 3:2; Phil. 1:19.
  20. 20.How broad should be the field of our prayers? Eph. 6:18; 1 Tim. 2:1, 2.
