Lesson 13 · March 27, 1920

Living in Christ—A Review

PDFShare

Study and fix in memory one or more verses each that give a key to the themes listed below. This exercise, if faithfully done, will give you the gist of the book of Philippians, and the key to living in Christ.

Daily Study Outline

  1. 1.Read the book of Philippians.
  2. 2.Magnifying and abounding in Christ, themes 1-3.
  3. 3.Suffering and the mind of Christ, themes 4, 5.
  4. 4.Willing and working with Christ, themes 6, 7.
  5. 5.Rejoicing and striving in Christ, themes 8, 9.
  6. 6.Doing all things through Christ, themes 10-12.
  7. 7.Summarize the lesson.

Themes

  1. 1.Come over and help us. Acts 16:6-40.
  2. 2.Abounding in Christ. Phil. 1:1-11.
  3. 3.Magnifying Christ. Phil. 1:12-20.
  4. 4.Suffering for Christ. Phil. 1:21-30.
  5. 5.Having the mind of Christ. Phil. 2:1-11.
  6. 6.Willing and doing for Christ. Phil. 2:12-18.
  7. 7.Working together with Christ. Phil. 2:19-30.
  8. 8.Rejoicing in Christ. Phil. 3:1-7.
  9. 9.The high calling in Christ. Phil. 3:8-14.
  10. 10.Walking with Christ. Phil. 3:15-21.
  11. 11.Standing fast in Christ. Phil. 4:1-9.
  12. 12.Doing all things through Christ. Phil. 4:10-23.

Bible texts in this lesson
