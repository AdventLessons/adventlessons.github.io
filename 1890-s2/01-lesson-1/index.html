Prophecy

April 19, 1890

Lesson 1

PDFShare
  1. 2.When did he besiege Jerusalem? and what was the result? Verses 1, 2.
  2. 3.Who are named as prominent among the captives carried from Jerusalem to Babylon? Verse 6.
  3. 4.For what were these persons noted, especially Daniel? Verse 17.
  4. 5.Upon inquiry, what did the king learn concerning their wisdom? Verse 20.
  5. 6.What caused trouble and loss of sleep to Nebuchadnezzar? Dan. 2:1.
  6. 7.To whom did he resort for relief? Verse 2.
  7. 8.Could they interpret his dream? Verse 10.
  8. 9.How did their answer affect the king, and what did he immediately do? Verses 12, 13.
  9. 10.Upon learning of the decree, what course did Daniel pursue? Verses 14-16.
  10. 11.Did Daniel understand the interpretation of the dream when he promised to make it known unto the king? Verses 17, 18.
  11. 12.How did he get the desired information? Verse 19.
  12. 13.What did Daniel then do? Verse 24.
  13. 14.When Daniel was brought in before the king, what question was asked him? Verse 26.
  14. 15.What humble but wise answer did Daniel make? Verses 27, 28.
  15. 16.To whom does God reveal secrets? Amos 3:7.
  16. 17.Was this secret revealed to Daniel because of his position among the king's counselors? Dan. 2:30.
