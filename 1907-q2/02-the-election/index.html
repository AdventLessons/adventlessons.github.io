The Election

  1. 1.What earnest admonition is given us in ?
  2. 2.What is the meaning of election?—"Selection," "choosing after trying," "calling out of."
  3. 3.Of whom is the choosing? .
  4. 4.To what does God sometimes choose men temporarily? Ans. To a special work which does not of itself insure salvation.
  5. 5.What did He choose Abraham to be? Ans. The father of the promised Seed. .
  6. 6.To perpetuate His promise, which of Abraham's two sons was chosen? .
  7. 7.Of Isaac's two sons, which was chosen? .
  8. 8.Of Jacob's twelve sons, which one was chosen as progenitor of the promised Seed? .
  9. 9.Did the choice of any of these insure his eternal salvation? Did it deprive the unchosen of life?
  10. 10.What ancient king did God choose to glorify His name? . Note 1.
  11. 11.Was there salvation in this for him? Did he obtain it?
  12. 12.What other great ruler did God choose for a special work? . Did he recognize the call as from God? . Note 2.
  13. 13.Name another ruler whom the Lord chose for a special work . Did he honor God's call? .
  14. 14.Who of David's sons was chosen as an ancestor of Christ? .
  15. 15.Who of all the maidens of Israel was honored as the mother of Jesus? . Did this insure her salvation? Did it preclude others? .
  16. 16.Although God has used, or is using, us in His work, what is necessary that we may obtain eternal salvation? ; .
  17. 17.What must we choose that God's choice may be effected in us? ; .

Notes

1. "Show in Thee My Power."—God is no respecter of persons. He sent Abraham to Egypt to save Egypt. He sent Joseph there for the same purpose. And when Moses presented God's reasonable message, "Let My people go, that they may serve Me," Pharaoh might have received that message, and it would have brought him life and blessing, and would have made of Egypt, in its knowledge of the true God, a light center. Not until God had exhausted every effort upon Pharaoh's heart, did He withdraw His Spirit, and so leave Pharaoh to his fate.

2. "My Servant."—God had a work for Nebuchadnezzar to do; but it took Nebuchadnezzar long to learn the lesson. , , and are records of his education and advancement, until he came to the knowledge of God.
