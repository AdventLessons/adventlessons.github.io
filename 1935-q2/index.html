Southern European Division Missions

Map

The fields to which the Thirteenth Sabbath Offering will go this second quarter of the year are the missions of the Southern European Division. These fields are widely separated, as will be seen by the map. They are designated by the straight lines, and the islands within dashes. In the past, Thirteenth Sabbath Offering Overflows have been given to the Southern European Division, and disbursed as follows:
