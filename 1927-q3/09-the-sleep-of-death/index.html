Lesson 9 · August 27, 1927

The Sleep of Death

PDFShare

Memory Verse: "For the living know that they shall die: but the dead know not anything." Eccl. 9:5.

Lesson Helps: "Patriarchs and Prophets," pp. 60-62; "Testimonies," Vol. 1, p. 344; "The Great Controversy," pp. 531-550.

Suggestions for Daily Family Study

  1. 1.Study questions and notes under "Origin of Death." Children may read paragraphs 1 and 2 of the primary lesson. Drill on the memory verse each day. Read "Patriarchs and Prophets," pp. 60-62.
  2. 2.Study questions under "Condition Of Man in Death." Children may read paragraphs 3-5 of the primary lesson. Read "Testimonies," Vol. 1, p. 344.
  3. 3.Study questions under "The Power That Frees from Death." Children may read paragraphs 6 and 7 of the primary lesson. Read "The Great Controversy," pp. 531-534.
  4. 4.Study questions and note under "Appropriation of the Delivering Power." Children may read paragraphs 8 and 9 of the primary lesson. Read "The Great Controversy," pp. 545-549.
  5. 5.Discuss the following: What popular belief is opposed to the truth set forth in these scriptures? If the saved are taken to heaven when they die, what need is there of any resurrection at a future time? How can the teachings of the Bible concerning a day of judgment be harmonized with the doctrine that men enter into their reward at the hour of death? Children may answer questions in the primary lesson.
  6. 6.Tell the lesson as a story, each member of the family taking part.
  7. 7.Review the lesson, asking questions from any of the lesson sheets. Read the notes in the "Youth's Instructor," if the paper is at hand.

The Lesson

Origin of Death

  1. 1.What did God declare to be the penalty for disobedience? Gen. 2:16, 17; Rom. 6:23.

Notes

"Immortality, promised to man on condition of obedience, had been forfeited by transgression. Adam could not transmit to his posterity that which he did not possess; and there could have been no hope for the fallen race, had not God, by the sacrifice of His Son, brought immortality within their reach."—"The Great Controversy," p. 533.

  1. 2.What was done to prevent sinners from living forever? Gen. 3:22-24.

Notes

"The warning given to our first parents—'In the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die'—did not imply that they were to die on the very day when they partook of the forbidden fruit. But on that day the irrevocable sentence would be pronounced."—"Patriarchs and Prophets," p. 60.

  1. 3.What sentence passed upon all men when sin entered this world? Rom. 5:12.
  2. 4.Who only has immortality? 1 Tim. 6:15, 16.

Notes

The meaning of the word "immortal" is "having unending existence; exempt from death." The word "mortal" means "subject to death; destined to die."

  1. 5.How should we regard death? 1 Cor. 15:26.

Condition of Man in Death

  1. 6.What is said concerning the living and the dead? Eccl. 9:5,
  2. 7.How is the condition of man in death further described? Ps. 146:3, 4.
  3. 8.What do the dead not know concerning those whom they have left behind? Job 14:21.
  4. 9.What reason is given for great faithfulness in our work? Eccl. 9:10.

The Power That Frees from Death

  1. 10.What power is equal in strength to death? Song of Solomon 8:6, first part.
  2. 11.By whom was such love revealed? John 3:16.
  3. 12.What could death not do with the Son of God? Acts 2:24.
  4. 13.What does Jesus say of Himself? What did He bring with Him when He came from the grave? Rev. 1:18.
  5. 14.What glorious event has given man hope of a resurrection from the dead? 1 Cor. 15:20-2$:
  6. 15.How is the change from mortality to immortality described? 1 Cor. 15:51-55.
  7. 16.What was Job's hope? Job 14:12-15.

Appropriation of the Delivering Power

  1. 17.How can man appropriate the power of the resurrection of Christ? John 5:24.

Notes

To know Christ and the power of His resurrection, is to know Him here and now as a personal Saviour from sin—that is, from the practice of sin in our daily experience. And knowing salvation from sin—that is, from sinning—we know the power of His resurrection. (See 2 Cor. 4:6.)

  1. 18.In what sure hope may the faithful die? 1 Thess. 4:14-16; 2 Cor. 4:14.
  2. 19.What should be our constant endeavor? Phil. 3:10, 11, 20, 21.
