Lesson 13 · September 24, 1927

The Review

PDFShare

Suggestions for Daily Family Study

  1. 1.Study questions under topics "The Beginning and the End of Sin" and "The Coming of the Lord Jesus." Children may study sections 1 and 2 of the primary lesson.
  2. 2.Study questions under topics "Signs of the Coming of Jesus" and "The Millennium." Children may study sections 2 and 3 of the primary lesson.
  3. 3.Study questions under topics "The Law of God" and "The Sabbath." Children may study section 4 and first part of section 5 of the primary lesson.
  4. 4.Study questions under topics "The Change of the Sabbath" and "Sabbath Reform; Proper Sabbath Observance." Children may study second part of section 5 of the primary lesson.
  5. 5.Study questions under topics "The Sleep of Death" and "The Judgment." Children may study sections 6 and 7 of the primary lesson.
  6. 6.Study questions under topics "The Ordinances" and "Tithes and Offerings." Children may study sections 8 and 9 of the primary lesson.
  7. 7.Review the questions under all topics, children reviewing the questions in the primary lesson.

Questions

Lesson 1—The Beginning and the End of Sin

  1. 1.With whom did sin originate? Ezek. 28:14-17; Isa. 14:12-14.
  2. 2.What shows that the reign of sin will end and the dominion be restored? Rev. 21:26, 27; 22:14.

Notes

Lesson 2—The Coming of the Lord Jesus

  1. 3.What will the coming of Jesus mean to the righteous? To the wicked? 1 Thess. 4:16-18; 2 Thess. 1:7-9.

Notes

Lesson 3—Signs of the Coming of Jesus

  1. 4.What signs show when the coming of Jesus is near at hand? Matt. 24:29; Luke 21:25-27; Matt. 24:14.

Notes

Lesson 4—The Millennium

  1. 5.What will take place when Jesus comes? 1 Thess. 4:16, 17.
  2. 6.When the thousand years are ended, what takes place? Rev. 20:5-10.

Notes

Lesson 5—The Law of God

  1. 7.In what relation does the law stand to the gospel? Rom. 3:20; Gal. 3:24.

Notes

Lesson 6—The Sabbath

  1. 8.When and how was the Sabbath made? Gen. 2:1-3.
  2. 9.What record have we of Jesus and the apostles keeping the Sabbath? Luke 4:16; Acts 17:1, 2.

Notes

Lesson 7—The Change of the Sabbath

  1. 10.What prophecy foretells an attempt to change the law of God? Dan. 7:25; 2 Thess. 2:3, 4.
  2. 11.What claim is made by the papacy in the matter of changing the Sabbath commandment? See notes in Lesson 7.

Notes

Lesson 8—Sabbath Reform; Proper Sabbath Observance

  1. 12.What indicates that there will be a reform in Sabbath observance in the last days? Isa. 56:1, 2.
  2. 13.How should the Sabbath be kept? Isa. 58:13, 14.

Notes

Lesson 9—The Sleep of Death

  1. 14.What is man's condition in death? Ps. 146:4; Eccl. 9:5, 6.
  2. 15.When will the righteous be awakened from the sleep of death? 1 Thess. 4:16.

Notes

Lesson 10—The Judgment

  1. 16.Describe the judgment scene as portrayed by the prophets. Dan. 7:9, 10; Rev. 20:11, 12.

Notes

Lesson 11—The Ordinances

  1. 17.What events are commemorated by the ordinance of baptism? Rom. 6:3-5.
  2. 18.What lesson in humility did Jesus give? John 13:3-5, 14-17.
  3. 19.In what manner did Jesus institute the ordinance of the Lord's Supper? 1 Cor. 11:23-26.

Notes

Lesson 12—Tithes and Offerings

  1. 20.What portion of our income does the Lord claim as His own? Lev. 27:30.
  2. 21.Upon what conditions does the Lord promise great blessings? Mal. 3:10, 11.

Notes

"If those to whom God's money has been intrusted will be faithful in bringing the means lent them to the Lord's treasury, His work will make rapid advancement. Many souls will be won to the cause of truth, and the day of Christ's coming will be hastened."—"Testimonies," vol. 9, p. 58.

"The light of the gospel shining from the cross of Christ rebukes selfishness, and encourages liberality and benevolence. It should not be a lamented fact that there are increasing calls to give."—"Testimonies," vol. 9, p. 254.

"The gospel is to be presented, not as a lifeless theory, but as a living force to change the life."—"The Desire of Ages," p. 826.
