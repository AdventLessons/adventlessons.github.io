Lesson 17 · January 22, 1887

The Putting Forth of the Fig-Tree, Etc

PDFShare
  1. 1.As Jesus went out of the temple at evening after giving his last discourse there, to what did some of the disciples call his attention? Mark 13:1; Matt. 24:1.
  2. 2.What did Jesus say to them? Matt. 24:2.
  3. 3.Later in the evening, as they sat upon the Mount of Olives looking down upon the temple and the city, what questions did they ask him? Verse 3.,
  4. 4.What warning did he give them? Verse 4.
  5. 5.Why would such a warning be needed? Verse 5.
  6. 6.What great tribulation was to come before they could look for the second advent of Christ? Verses 21, 22.
  7. 7.To what special period do these verses apply?—To the 1260 years of papal persecution.
  8. 8.When did this period terminate?—Near the close of the eighteenth century.
  9. 9.What need was there to be of guarding against deception even after the close of this period? Verses 23-26.
  10. 10.By what figure did Jesus illustrate the manner of his second coming? Verse 27.
  11. 11.What did he give as the true signs of his second advent? Verse 29.
  12. 12.By what parable did he illustrate the certainty with which they might then look for his coming "in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory"? Repeat verses 32, 33.
  13. 13.In view of these things what admonition did he give to those who should be living at that time? Verse 42.
  14. 14.By what figure did he illustrate the importance of heeding this admonition? Verses 43, 44.
  15. 15.What question did Jesus then ask? Verse 45.
  16. 16.Who are represented by this wise servant?—The faithful ministers of Christ who truly feed the flock of the great Shepherd.
  17. 17.What does Jesus say of such a servant? V. 46.
  18. 18.What promise does he make? Verse 47.
  19. 19.Who are represented by the evil servant in verse 48?—Unfaithful ministers who do not feed the flock of God, but fatten upon it.
  20. 20.How is such a servant described? Verse 49.
  21. 21.How will such a one be disappointed? V. 50.
  22. 22.What will be his fate? Verse 51.
