Lesson 4 · February 26, 1887

Their Interest in Man's Salvation

PDFShare
  1. 1.What character is ascribed to the angels who have not fallen? Acts 10:22.
  2. 2.How does Jesus speak of them? Matt. 25:31.
  3. 3.Who is evidently referred to as doing God's will in heaven? Matt. 6:10; Mark 13:32.
  4. 4.Do these angels keep the commandments of God? Ps. 103:20.
  5. 5.What lesson should we draw from this for ourselves?
  6. 6.Did some of these angels disobey God? what was the result? 2 Pet. 2:4.
  7. 7.What warning should this be to us?
  8. 8.Then must not the angels have been placed on probation, the same as man?
  9. 9.Could even the purest of mortal men see God's face and live? Ex. 33:20.
  10. 10.But how is it with the holy angels? Matt. 18:10; Luke 1:19.
  11. 11.What does this indicate as to their character? Heb. 12:14.
  12. 12.Who else will finally be permitted to see God? Matt. 5:8.
  13. 13.What is the chief occupation of the angels since the fall of man? Heb. 1:14.
  14. 14.Who announced the birth of Jesus? Luke 2:8, 9.
  15. 15.Did they seem to have much interest in this event? Verses 10, 13.
  16. 16.What did they say? Verse 14.
  17. 17.If angels take so deep an interest in the salvation of men, how much interest should men feel in their own salvation? Matt. 6:33, first part.
  18. 18.How is the conversion of one sinner regarded by the angels in heaven? Luke 15:10.
  19. 19.Then must they not watch all men with interest, and know when one turns to God?
  20. 20.Where are the names of the righteous written? Luke 10:20.
  21. 21.By whom is it probable that these names are recorded? Eccl. 5:6.
  22. 22.What does Jesus confess before the angels? Rev. 3:5.
  23. 23.Then must not these names be well known and talked over in heaven?
  24. 24.Whenever the visits of angels are mentioned, is it not always assumed that they are well acquainted with the saints and all that relates to them? Luke 1:13.
  25. 25.Did Gabriel, when he came to Zacharias, know his name and the name of his wife? Same verse.
  26. 26.When the angel came to Cornelius, how did he greet him? Acts 10:3, last part.
  27. 27.Was the angel acquainted with the pious deeds of Cornelius? Verse 4.
  28. 28.Do the angels know the towns in which the saints live? Verse 5.
  29. 29.Do they know the streets on which they reside? Acts 9:11.
  30. 30.Do they know what business they follow? Acts 10:6.
  31. 31.How much do they know about us?
  32. 32.How should this influence us?

Notes

The character of the angels.—The scriptures referred to under the first eleven questions in this lesson plainly show that the angels were put on probation, the same as man; for none of them could have fallen (2 Pet. 2:4) if they had not been on probation. They must be subject to law, or they could not have sinned. Rom. 4:15. The fact that they "do his commandments" (Ps. 103:20), and do his will (Matt. 6:10), shows that they do keep God's law.

Recording angels.—Many have a form of godliness, their names are upon the church records, but they have a spotted record in heaven. The recording angel has faithfully written our deeds. Every selfish act, every wrong word, every unfulfilled duty, and every secret sin, with every artful dissembling, is faithfully chronicled in the book of records kept by the recording angel.—Mrs. E. G. White.

*Doubtless our readers have discovered the mistake made in omitting the date of the Fifth Sabbath in January; the lessons have been given in their regular order, the mistake being in the date only. You will also observe that the date of the Sabbath on which the lesson is to be recited is now given, together with the number of the Sabbath in the month.
