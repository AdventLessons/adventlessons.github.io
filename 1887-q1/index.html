Notices
*Doubtless our readers have discovered the mistake made in omitting the date of the Fifth Sabbath in January; the lessons have been given in their regular order, the mistake being in the date only. You will also observe that the date of the Sabbath on which the lesson is to be recited is now given, together with the number of the Sabbath in the month.
☞ By some means an erroneous statement crept into lesson one on the ministration of angels. It was said that the angels were mentioned in every book of the Bible; but this is not so.
☞ As much controversy has arisen over the lesson on the parable of the ten virgins, we propose to print in our next number a brief article from Eld. Smith, explaining some of the controverted points.
