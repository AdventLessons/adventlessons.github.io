Lesson 14 · January 1, 1887

The Wicked Husbandmen

PDFShare
  1. 1.What attracted many people to Bethany on the last Sabbath our Lord spent upon the earth? John 12.
  2. 2.Under what circumstances did he enter Jerusalem the next day? Matt. 21.
  3. 3.What did he say when the Pharisees asked him to rebuke his disciples for shouting his praises? Luke 19:39, 40.
  4. 4.What important thing occurred on the second day of the week? Matt. 21:12, 13.
  5. 5.How did this rebuke affect the chief priests and scribes? Luke 19:47.
  6. 6.How were they perplexed? Verse 48.
  7. 7.How did they confront him the next morning when he came into the temple? Matt. 21:23.
  8. 8.How did Jesus confound them? Verses 24-27.
  9. 9.What parable did he then put forth? Verses 28-30.
  10. 10.How did Jesus make this parable condemn the Jewish rulers?
  11. 11.What other parable did he put forth for the same purpose?—The parable of the wicked husbandmen.
  12. 12.With what words did he introduce the parable? Matt. 21:33.
  13. 13.Who were the characters introduced in this parable?
  14. 14.Give a brief account of what the householder did at first? Verse 33.
  15. 15.What did he do when the time of fruit drew near? Verse 34.
  16. 16.How were these servants treated? Verse 35.
  17. 17.What was his next effort, and how did it succeed? Verse 36.
  18. 18.What did he then do as a last resort? Verse 37.
  19. 19.How did the husbandmen reason among themselves when they saw the son? Verse 38.
  20. 20.How did they carry out their purpose? V. 39.
  21. 21.When our Lord had thus related the parable, what question did he ask the scribes and Pharisees? Verse 40.
  22. 22.How did they answer him? Verse 41.
  23. 23.How is God the Father represented in this parable?
  24. 24.How are the holy prophets and true religious teachers represented?
  25. 25.How was Jesus himself represented?
  26. 26.How were the Jewish people and especially the priesthood represented?
  27. 27.How did God plant the vineyard?—By dispensing the seeds of divine truth.
  28. 28.What fruit had he a right to expect?—The fruit of righteousness; due honor to himself; and acceptable worship; and character ripe for the kingdom of heaven.
  29. 29.Could the priests and Pharisees understand and apply this parable? Matt. 21:45.
  30. 30.Instead of being penitent for their sins, what was their chief trouble? Verse 46.
  31. 31.How did Jesus show that in rejecting him they were fulfilling the Scriptures? Verse 42.
  32. 32.What did he say should happen to them? Verse 43.
  33. 33.With what words did Jesus conclude his remarks? Verse 44.
