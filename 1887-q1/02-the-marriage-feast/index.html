Lesson 15 · January 8, 1887

The Marriage Feast

PDFShare
  1. 1.When our Lord again spake to the people in parables, to what did he compare the kingdom of heaven? Matt. 22:1, 2.
  2. 2.In what sense does the Saviour mean to compare the kingdom of heaven to this king?—As this king did in furnishing the feast with guests, so will the King of heaven do with those that receive the sweet invitations of the gospel.
  3. 3.What did the king send forth his servants to do? Verse 3.
  4. 4.What had been previously done?—The guests had been bidden. See same verse.
  5. 5.How had the guests been bidden?—By the word of God, as given by the prophets.
  6. 6.How were they called to the wedding?—By the preaching of John the Baptist, and by the apostles.
  7. 7.When the first call had been refused, with what emphasis was the call repeated? Verse 4.
  8. 8.How did they treat this call? Verse 5.
  9. 9.What enormous cruelty did some of them practice? Verse 6.
  10. 10.Mention some of the disciples that were so treated.
  11. 11.How did the king punish the ungrateful people that rejected his call, and slew his servants? Verse 7.
  12. 12.To what people does this verse refer?—To the Jews.
  13. 13.How did Christ foretell this terrible punishment? Luke 11:49-51; Matt. 23:34-38.
  14. 14.How did he foretell the overthrow of their city? Matt. 24:2.
  15. 15.How was Jerusalem taken and the temple burned?—By the Roman army under Titus, in A. D. 70.
  16. 16.What did the king then say to his servants? Matt. 22:8, 9.
  17. 17.How was this part of the parable fulfilled? Acts 9:15; 10:14, 15; 22:17-21.
  18. 18.According to the parable, what did the king discover when the guests had assembled? Matt. 22:11.
  19. 19.What did the king say to this man? Verse 12.
  20. 20.How did the man plainly show that he had no good cause for appearing in the presence of the king clad in an unsuitable garb? Last part of the same verse.
  21. 21.What does this wedding garment represent?—The righteousness of Christ.
  22. 22.Why had the careless guest no excuse for not clothing himself in a wedding garment?—Because such a garment was furnished by the king, free of cost, to every guest.
  23. 23.How is it with the righteousness of Christ?—It is offered free to all who will repent, believe in Christ, and strive with all the heart to obey him.
  24. 24.What doom was pronounced upon the man who ventured into the king's presence without putting on a wedding garment? Matt. 22:13.
  25. 25.What statement does our Lord make in conclusion? Verse 14.
  26. 26.What do we understand by this?—That the gospel call is to the whole world, but those alone will be chosen who have met its requirements.
