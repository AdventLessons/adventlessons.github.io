Lesson 5 · April 30, 1898

Paul a Prisoner in Cæsarea

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(.)

  1. 1.Tell the circumstances of Paul's appearance before the council.
  2. 2.In what danger was Paul, and how was he encouraged?
  3. 3.Tell about the conspiracy among the Jews against Paul.
  4. 4.To whom did the conspirators make known their purpose? .
  5. 5.What did they devise for furtherance of their design? .
  6. 6.Who learned of this conspiracy? What did he at once do? .
  7. 7.How was the chief captain made acquainted with it? .
  8. 8.What measures did the officer at once take for Paul's safety? How strong an escort was provided for him? To what place was Paul to be sent? To whom? .
  9. 9.What sort of letters did the captain send to the governor? .
  10. 10.How did he set forth his own connection with the matter of Paul's seizure? . See note.
  11. 11.What did he say as to the charge against Paul? What testimony did he give as to Paul's innocence? .
  12. 12.What commandment did he say he had given to Paul's accusers? .
  13. 13.How far did the armed escort accompany Paul? When the horsemen arrived at Cæsarea with Paul, what did they do? .
  14. 14.What question did the governor ask in reading the captain's letter? .
  15. 15.What did he say to Paul? What order did he give concerning him? .

Notes

"And should have been killed of them." . This is an ancient form of speech; the Revised Version renders it, "Was about to be slain of them."
