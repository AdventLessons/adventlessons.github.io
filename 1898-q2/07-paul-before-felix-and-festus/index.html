Lesson 7 · May 14, 1898

Paul before Felix and Festus

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(.)

  1. 1.Who was Felix? How did it come to pass that Paul appeared before him?
  2. 2.With what was Paul charged? Give the substance of his defense.
  3. 3.How was Paul treated while in confinement?
  4. 4.On what occasion and for what purpose did Paul appear before Felix the second time? .
  5. 5.Of what did Paul reason? With what result? What did Felix say to Paul? .
  6. 6.Did he ever send for Paul again? What for? What was the only thing that stood in the way of Paul's release? .
  7. 7.How long was he thus unjustly kept a prisoner? For what purpose did Felix leave Paul in bondage upon his going out of office? Who succeeded him? .
  8. 8.What was the first act of Festus in coming into the province? Chapter 25, .
  9. 9.By whom was he at once beset? .
  10. 10.What was their petition? What was their object? .
  11. 11.What did Festus reply? .
  12. 12.What did he say Paul's adversaries should do? .
  13. 13.How soon was this opportunity given them? .
  14. 14.What did the Jews do as soon as Festus opened his court? What was lacking in their accusations? .
  15. 15.What did Paul reply to all this? Was anything more necessary than this simple statement? .
  16. 16.What question did Festus put to him? What was the object of this question? .
  17. 17.What did Paul say as to the irregularity of such a procedure? By what statement did he show the injustice of Festus' proposal? .
  18. 18.How did he show his entire willingness to suffer any just punishment? By what statement did he at once put an end to the sham trial? .
  19. 19.What did Festus then do and say? .
  20. 20.Who came to visit Festus some time afterwards? .
  21. 21.How did Festus bring Paul's case before Agrippa? .
  22. 22.What did he say of the demand of the Jews, and of his reply? .
  23. 23.What did he say as to his disappointment when the case came up for trial? .
  24. 24.In what words did he put the whole of the real case of the Jews against Paul? .
  25. 25.For what purpose did he say he was now keeping Paul? .
  26. 26.What wish did Agrippa express? When did Festus say that his curiosity should be satisfied? .

Additional Questions and Suggestions

  1. 1.What striking comments have we in this lesson on "Roman justice"?
  2. 2.Note how lightly Festus put the cause of contention between Paul and the Jews. It was concerning "one Jesus, which was dead, whom Paul affirmed to be alive." How much more, if any, is there to the Gospel than the statement of this question? Is Jesus alive? How do you know?
