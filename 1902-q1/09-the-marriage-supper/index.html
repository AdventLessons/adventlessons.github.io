Lesson 9 · March 1, 1902

The Marriage Supper

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

Lesson Scripture.—.

Aid.—.

  1. 1.With what simple statement did Jesus introduce this parable?
  2. 2.Whom were the servants to call to the marriage?
  3. 3.What shows that they had professed to accept the invitation?
  4. 4.What did they do when they were called?
  5. 5.How did they treat the second message, "All things are now ready; come"?
  6. 6.What did they do to the messengers?
  7. 7.Describe the fate of those who thus slighted the king's invitation and killed his servants.
  8. 8.What is illustrated by the two calls to the marriage and their rejection?
  9. 9.Afterward, what did the king say to his servants?
  10. 10.Who were to be bidden?
  11. 11.What does this third call represent?
  12. 12.How was the wedding finally furnished with guests?
  13. 13.What was provided for each one?
  14. 14.What is the wedding-garment?
  15. 15.What insult was offered the king by one who accepted his invitation to the marriage?
  16. 16.What question was asked him by the king?
  17. 17.How did he reply? What does this show?
  18. 18.What was done with him?
  19. 19.Like the parables of the two sons, the Lord's vineyard, etc., what special and solemn meaning had this parable for those who heard it spoken?
  20. 20.How does its lesson apply to the church to-day?
  21. 21.Why are few chosen? Show how this is taught in the parable.
