Lesson 7 · April 19, 1890

The Love of God in Christ

PDFShare
  1. 1.What is the limit of human love? John 15:13.
  2. 2.Show that God's love exceeds this. Rom. 5:8.
  3. 3.How great was the sacrifice of God for us? John 3:16.
  4. 4.Show that the angels are given to help us? Heb. 1:14.
  5. 5.Of what may we be assured? Rom. 8:32.
  6. 6.What is first extended to us? 1 Cor. 1:4.
  7. 7.What is thus brought to us? Rom. 3:24.
  8. 8.Describe the condition of a justified man. Rom. 5:1, 2.
  9. 9.What do we thus become? Titus 3:7.
  10. 10.What is evidence that we are the children of God? Verse 8.
  11. 11.What must be the final result, if faithful? 1 John 3:2.
  12. 12.What will this hope lead us to do? Verse 3.
  13. 13.What made this hope a living one? 1 Peter 1:3.
  14. 14.What is included in it? Verse 4.
  15. 15.Is there power to keep us faithful? Jude 24, 25.
  16. 16.How is this help obtained? 1 Peter 1:5.
  17. 17.How strong is the love which binds us to God? Rom. 8:38, 39.
