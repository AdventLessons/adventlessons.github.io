Lesson 2 · March 15, 1890

Why Jesus Lived in This World as a Child, a Youth, and a Man

PDFShare
  1. 1.In what way could man have retained life?
  2. 2.Sin having entered, what promise was made?
  3. 3.Name some of the results of sin?
  4. 4.What was the mission of Christ? Luke 19:10.
  5. 5.What name was given him by the angel? Matt. 1:21.
  6. 6.Where was Jesus born? Matt. 2:1, 5.
  7. 7.What remarkable thing took place in connection with his birth? Matt. 2:2; Luke 2:9, 13.
  8. 8.Did he develop gradually, as other children do? Luke 2:52.
  9. 9.Where did he spend his boyhood days? Matt. 2:23; Luke 2:51.
  10. 10.What can you say of this city? John 1:46.
  11. 11.What can you say of Christ's condition in manhood? Isa. 53:3; Matt. 8:20.
  12. 12.How was he treated by men? Isa. 53:3, 5, 7.
  13. 13.Why was it necessary that he should become a babe and pass through all the stages of life? Heb. 2:10, 17.
  14. 14.Why would this fit him especially to be our leader and priest? Heb. 4:15.
  15. 15.Having experienced all this, what is he able to do? Heb. 2:18.
  16. 16.What assurance have we concerning him? Heb. 7:25.
  17. 17.In view of all this, what are we admonished to do? Heb. 4:16.

Notes

Whether you are a child or a man it is your privilege in every temptation to think, Jesus, my Saviour, has been here before me; he has passed through this very way; his foot-prints are here. I have only to follow his footsteps and all will be well; and having been here he knows just how to sympathize with and help me. How this thought ought to drive away the darkness and give strength and cheer!
