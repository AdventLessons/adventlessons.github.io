Lesson 1 · March 8, 1890

The Demand for Christ

PDFShare
  1. 1.Repeat the first promise made concerning a Saviour. Gen. 3:15.
  2. 2.To whom was this promise renewed? Gen. 22:18.
  3. 3.What is meant by the seed here referred to? Gal. 3:16.
  4. 4.What was the announcement of this promise to Abraham? Verse 8.
  5. 5.What is the gospel? Ans. Good news of salvation through the promised Seed.
  6. 6.What made the gospel necessary? Ans. Man's having sinned, or disobeyed God. Gen. 3:11-13.
  7. 7.What had God said would be the result of this disobedience? Gen. 2:17.
  8. 8.How, then, might man have retained life? Ans. Since disobedience brought death, it is evident that by obedience he would have retained life.
  9. 9.What was the result of this disobedience? Rom. 5:12; Gen. 3:17, 19.
  10. 10.Since man has sinned, through whom only can he obtain life? John 3:36.
  11. 11.How can a man avail himself of the benefits of Christ? Rom. 3:25; John 1:12.
  12. 12.Soon after the promise was made, how did the sons of Adam show that it was understood by them? Gen. 4:3-5.
  13. 13.Describe the offerings of Cain and Abel. Verses 3, 4.
  14. 14.Why was Abel's offering accepted and Cain's rejected? Heb. 11:4.
  15. 15.In what particular was Abel's faith manifested? Heb. 9:22.
  16. 16.Because of this lack of faith, what was the result to Cain? Gen. 4:7.
  17. 17.What was indicated by the slaying of the victim of sacrifice? Ans. That the sinner deserved death, but that the Promised One, typified by the victim, would die in his stead.
  18. 18.What will always accompany true faith? James 2:20.
  19. 19.What will the remnant of God's people be found doing? Rev. 12:17; 14:12.
