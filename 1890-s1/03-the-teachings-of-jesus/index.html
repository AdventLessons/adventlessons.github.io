Lesson 3 · March 22, 1890

The Teachings of Jesus

PDFShare
  1. 1.What event marks the beginning of Christ's public ministry? Matt. 3:13-17.
  2. 2.In what way must he bring the truth before the people? Ans. By precept and example.
  3. 3.In his teachings how high did he place the standard of morality? Matt. 5:48, 8.
  4. 4.What promise does he make to those who earnestly seek such a state? Verse 6.
  5. 5.What is the standard of righteous living? Deut. 6:25; Eccl. 12:13.
  6. 6.What was the teaching of Christ in reference to the law? Matt. 5:17.
  7. 7.What did he say of those who should teach after him? Verse 19.
  8. 8.What is one of the purposes now served by the law? Rom. 3:20.
  9. 9.In what way did Christ show the righteous and abiding nature of the law? Ans. By teaching and obeying it in life, and in dying in man's stead to meet its demands.
  10. 10.To the dying what did he declare? John 5:40.
  11. 11.What to those who are in the darkness of sin? John 8:12.
  12. 12.What to the thirsty? John 4:14.
  13. 13.What to the hungry? John 6:48, 51.
  14. 14.What to those who were sick and weary of sin? Matt. 11:28, 30.
  15. 15.What did he command his followers to do? Matt. 28:19, 20.
  16. 16.In carrying out this commission what did Paul teach? Acts 20:21.
  17. 17.What then was the teaching of Christ? Ans. The law of God to convince men of sin, and faith in himself as the only escape from its terrible penalty.
