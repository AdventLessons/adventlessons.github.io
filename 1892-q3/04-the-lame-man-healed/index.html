Lesson 4 · July 23, 1892

The Lame Man Healed.—Acts 3:1-16

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(Memory Verses, 6-8.)

Golden Text.—"And His name through faith in His name hath made this man strong." .

  1. 1.As Peter and John went up to the temple one afternoon at the hour of prayer, whom did they see? .
  2. 2.How long had the man been unable to walk? ; .
  3. 3.What did he ask of the apostles? .
  4. 4.What did Peter reply? .
  5. 5.From this what did the lame man expect? .
  6. 6.What did Peter then say? .
  7. 7.What was the immediate result? .
  8. 8.Who saw it? and how were they affected? .
  9. 9.When Peter saw their astonishment, what did he say? .
  10. 10.To whom did he at once direct their minds? .
  11. 11.To what particular thing in connection with Christ did he refer? .
  12. 12.Was it for the purpose of taunting them that Peter referred to the crucifixion of Christ? .
  13. 13.What fact did he want especially to impress on their minds? , last part.
  14. 14.What power did he then say had made the lame man whole? .
  15. 15.Because of the humility of Jesus, what has he received? .
  16. 16.What was it that gave the lame man the perfect soundness? , last part.
  17. 17.Is there anything else that comes by the faith of Jesus Christ? ; .
  18. 18.Did the man who received strength by faith have actual power, or only imaginary power? .
  19. 19.Then what kind of righteousness must it be that comes from the same faith? , last part.
  20. 20.How does this righteousness compare with that which a man gets by his own efforts? .

Notes

In this lesson we have a notable example of the working of faith. Similar ones may be found in ; . Note the statement concerning these cases, that it was faith that healed them. In each case it was utterly impossible for the afflicted one to do anything for his own recovery. The only way they could even show their good will to be better was to submit to the power that could make them better. The same faith that restored these afflicted ones to health will make men righteous; and nothing else will. What the Lord requires of us is to walk humbly with God. . But we are "without strength" () and cannot walk. So God in his mercy lifts us up out of the pit, and sets our feet upon the Rock, and establishes our goings. . He makes us to walk in the way of his commandments; and the righteous acts done by faith are as real as were the steps taken by the man in the temple.
