Lesson 13 · September 28, 1918

A Review of Revelation, Chapters 1 to 7; 12; 13

PDFShare

Chapter 1:

  1. 1.How was the Revelation given?
  2. 2.What great name gives the scope of the book? Verse 8.

Chapters 2 and 3:

  1. 1.Into how many phases of history and experience is the church life of the Christian era divided? What are they?
  2. 2.What is the approximate period covered by each?
  3. 3.What is the special exhortation for the church of the judgment hour?

Chapters 4:1 to 7:8:

  1. 1.How wide interest was taken in the opening of the sealed book?
  2. 2.What did the breaking of each seal disclose?
  3. 3.At what point in the sixth chapter are we now living?
  4. 4.What is the special work for our time?
  5. 5.How is the way still held open for this work to be done?

Chapter 12:

  1. 1.Who are the two great opposing characters in the controversy of chapter 12?
  2. 2.Who is the victor in the conflict?
  3. 3.Who are represented by Michael? By the dragon? By the woman?
  4. 4.Who is behind all the persecutions of this world?

Chapter 13:

  1. 1.By what symbol is the Old World church-and-state history depicted?
  2. 2.Give three reasons why the two-horned beast could not be another phase of the first beast.
  3. 3.What must be an essential of the image to the beast?
  4. 4.What is the mark of the beast?—Sunday enforced by law as a substitute for the Lord's Sabbath.
  5. 5.What is God's mark?—The Sabbath of the Lord.

Bible texts in this lesson

Revelation 1:8 · Revelation 4:1-999 · Revelation 5 · Revelation 6 · Revelation 7:1-8
