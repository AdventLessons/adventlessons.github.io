Lesson 13 · March 31, 1906

Rewards and Punishments

PDFShare

Questions.

  1. 1.What statement is made in Eccl. 3:17?
  2. 2.What assurance has God given that He will judge the world? Acts 17:31.
  3. 3.Where must all appear, and for what purpose? 2 Cor. 5:10. See also Eccl. 11:9.
  4. 4.Because all wickedness is not summarily punished, what presumptuous course do many pursue? Eccl. 8:11.
  5. 5.When Jesus comes, what will He bring with Him? Rev. 22:12.
  6. 6.When did He say those who had dealt kindly with the poor and needy would be recompensed? Luke 14:13, 14. Compare Prov. 19:17.
  7. 7.Where are all, both good and bad, to be recompensed? Prov. 11:31.
  8. 8.What is to be the reward of the righteous? Matt. 5:5; Isa. 65:21, 22.
  9. 9.What is to be the fate of the ungodly? Mal. 4:1; Ps. 9:17; 37:20; 2 Thess. 1:7-9.
  10. 10.In view of all this, what message of warning must every faithful minister of God proclaim? Isa. 3:10, 11; Eze. 3:18.
  11. 11.In what words did John the Baptist do this? Matt. 3:11, 12. David? Psalms 1. Christ? Matt. 25:46. Peter? 2 Peter 3:10.
  12. 12.How is the final scene of destruction described by the Revelator? Rev. 20:9.
  13. 13.When will this take place? Verse 7. What is it called? Verse 14.
  14. 14.What will then appear? Rev. 21:1.
  15. 15.Who will dwell in the new heaven and the new earth? 2 Peter 3:13.
