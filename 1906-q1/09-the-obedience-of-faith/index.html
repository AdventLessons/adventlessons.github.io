The Obedience of Faith

Questions.

  1. 1.What is faith? . The American Revised Version reads: "Now faith is assurance of things hoped for, a conviction of things not seen."
  2. 2.What does it do? ; 1 John ⟦5:4?⟧. Note 1.
  3. 3.How did it manifest itself in the worthies of old? .
  4. 4.What is the proper evidence of living faith? .
  5. 5.How did Paul's labors in the gospel affect the believing Gentiles? .
  6. 6.What does Paul say was God's object in making known the mystery of the gospel to all nations? .
  7. 7.By what does genuine faith work? . And what is love? .
  8. 8.What does faith bring into the heart? .
  9. 9.What does Christ give to those who receive Him? .
  10. 10.How much can we do without Him? .
  11. 11.What can we do with Christ dwelling within? .
  12. 12.What alone can give us the victory over all the evil that is in the world? .
  13. 13.What, therefore, is inseparably connected with genuine faith? ; .

Notes

"We do not earn salvation by our obedience; for salvation is the free gift of God, to be received by faith. But obedience is the fruit of faith." "That so-called faith in Christ which professes to release men from the obligation of obedience to God is not faith, but presumption."—.
