Lesson 13 · March 28, 1903

The Wisdom of the Righteous

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

.

> He turneth rivers into a wilderness, > And water-springs into a thirsty ground; > A fruitful land into a salt desert, > For the wickedness of them that dwell therein. > He turneth a wilderness into a pool of water, > And a dry land into water-springs. > And there He maketh the hungry to dwell, > That they may prepare a city of habitation, > And sow fields, and plant vineyards, > And get them fruits of increase. > He blesseth them also, so that they are multiplied greatly; > And He suffereth not their cattle to decrease. > Again, they are diminished and bowed down > Through oppression, trouble, and sorrow. > He poureth contempt upon princes, > And causeth them to wander in the waste, where there is no way. > Yet setteth He the needy on high from affliction, > And maketh him families like a flock. > The upright shall see it, and be glad; > And all iniquity shall stop her mouth. > Whoso is wise will give heed to these things; > And they will consider the loving-kindnesses of Jehovah.

Questions

  1. 1.What change is God able to make in the flowing streams?
  2. 2.What change can He make in the springs?
  3. 3.Into what is He able to turn productive land?
  4. 4.What leads to such a reversal of the usual order of things?
  5. 5.What change can God make in the desert? In dry ground?
  6. 6.For whom is a dwelling-place thus provided?
  7. 7.What will they prepare?
  8. 8.What labor will they undertake? With what result?
  9. 9.What blessing is bestowed upon their homes?
  10. 10.What temporal blessing is also bestowed upon them?
  11. 11.What different experience is brought upon them? Through what means?
  12. 12.How is the vanity of human greatness made apparent?
  13. 13.How are men of high rank shown that their way is not in themselves?
  14. 14.What blessing is bestowed upon the needy?
  15. 15.What blessings come to them in their home life?
  16. 16.How will the righteous regard these experiences?
  17. 17.What effect will they have upon evil-disposed persons?
  18. 18.How will the wise treat these lessons?
  19. 19.What will they observe in these dealings of Jehovah with men?

Notes

While to 37 show God's marvelous power to provide for His people, even in solitary places, an experience which awaits every faithful child of God before the final triumph of His work, there is no doubt that they also refer to the wondrous provision He has made for His people in the new earth, when the desert shall rejoice and blossom as the rose. ; also 65:17-25.

to 42 are a wonderful commentary on that familiar verse, . Everything is in the hands of God. If there is prosperity, it comes from Him; if there are reverses, He permits them. God deals with man in the best way that will cause him to recognize his Creator. The righteous see these things, and rejoice now, and the time is coming when even the mouth of the wicked will be led to acknowledge the wisdom of God. Then it will be too late to accept His mercy, which is still offered. "To-day, if ye will hear His voice, harden not your hearts." "Behold, now is the day of salvation."
