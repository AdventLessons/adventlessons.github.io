Lesson 7 · February 14, 1903

God's Purpose in Deliverance

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

.

> He spread a cloud for a covering, > And fire to give light in the night. > They asked, and He brought quails, > And satisfied them with the bread of heaven. > He opened the rock, and waters gushed out; > They ran in the dry places like a river. > For He remembered His holy word, > And Abraham His servant. > And He brought forth His people with joy, > And His chosen with singing. > And He gave them the lands of the nations; > And they took the labor of the peoples in possession; > That they might keep His statutes, > And observe His laws. > Praise ye Jehovah [Hallelujah].

.

> Praise ye Jehovah. > Oh give thanks unto Jehovah; for He is good; > For His loving-kindness endureth forever. > Who can utter the mighty acts of Jehovah, > Or show forth all His praise? > Blessed are they that keep justice, > And He that doeth righteousness at all times. > Remember me, O Jehovah, with the favor that Thou bearest unto Thy people; > Oh visit me with Thy salvation, > That I may see the prosperity of Thy chosen, > That I may rejoice in the gladness of Thy nation, > That I may glory with Thine inheritance.

Questions

  1. 1.What shield did God provide for His people by day?
  2. 2.What furnished a light for them in the darkness?
  3. 3.What did God give them in response to their request?
  4. 4.With what did He fill them?
  5. 5.How did God provide water for His people?
  6. 6.How abundant was the supply of water?
  7. 7.What was thus being fulfilled?
  8. 8.Whom did God still keep in mind?
  9. 9.How did His people give expression to their thanksgiving for their deliverance?
  10. 10.What were now given to them?
  11. 11.What return did they now receive for their unrequited labor?
  12. 12.For what purpose was this great deliverance wrought?
  13. 13.What significant word closes the psalm?
  14. 14.With what exhortation does open?
  15. 15.What exhortation follows? What reason is given?
  16. 16.How enduring is His kindness?
  17. 17.How is the number of the great deeds of Jehovah emphasized?
  18. 18.How is the greatness of His praise set forth?
  19. 19.What class are counted happy? Who is included in this class?
  20. 20.How does the psalmist desire to be identified with God's people?
  21. 21.What personal request is made?
  22. 22.What is the first expected result of a favorable response to these petitions? The second? The third?

Notes

; ; should be studied with verse 39.

"By day the cloud directed the journeyings, or spread as a canopy above the host. It served as a protection from the burning heat, and by its coolness and moisture afforded grateful refreshment in the parched, thirsty desert. By night it became a pillar of fire, illuminating their encampment, constantly assuring them of divine presence." Read . "Here reference is made to the pillar of cloud and of fire to represent God's care for His people in the great final struggle with the power of evil."

The cloud that flooded the entire encampment of the Hebrews with the radiance of day was a wall of darkness to the Egyptians. Read with this , R. V. and . The way that God marks out for His people will be flooded with light, although the unbelieving will not see it.

The manna was a type of Jesus, the Bread of life. . Read also ; .

With verse 41 read . "Moses smote the rock, but it was the Son of God who, veiled in the cloudy pillar, stood beside Moses, and caused the life-giving water to flow." ; ; ; .

Verse 42; , and , record the covenant with Abraham. For the blessing of this covenant to Abraham's seed and heirs according to the promise, see .

Verse 43, with ; .

God brought forth His people that they might observe His statutes and keep His laws. The same is true in regard to His people to-day.

In all these experiences God designed to teach His people their utter dependence upon Him, and not merely on the man Moses, who was their leader.
