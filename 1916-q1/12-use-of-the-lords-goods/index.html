Lesson 12 · March 18, 1916

Use of the Lord's Goods

Lesson Scripture: Matt. 25:14-30.

Questions

  1. 1.What parable does Jesus introduce to throw further light on the coming kingdom? Matt. 25:14, first part. Note 1.
  2. 2.What did the man about to travel do before he left home? Verse 14, last part.
  3. 3.How did he distribute his goods among his servants? Verse 15, first part.
  4. 4.On what basis did he distribute them? Verse 15, last part. Note 2.
  5. 5.How did the servant receiving five talents make use of them? Verse 16.
  6. 6.How did the servant receiving two use his? Verse 17.
  7. 7.What did the servant who had received one talent do with his lord's money? Verse 18.
  8. 8.After a long time, what did the lord of those servants do? Verse 19.
  9. 9.What report did the servant receiving five talents make to his lord? Verse 20.
  10. 10.What response did the lord make to this servant? Verse 21.
  11. 11.What report was made by the servant who had received two talents? Verse 22.
  12. 12.How did the lord respond to this report? Verse 23.
  13. 13.What preface to his report did the servant receiving one talent make? Verse 24.
  14. 14.What course had he taken with his lord's money? Verse 25.
  15. 15.To what admission did the lord call his servant's attention? Verse 26.
  16. 16.What did his lord say he ought to have done, and why? Verse 27.
  17. 17.What did the lord command to be done with the one talent? Verse 28.
  18. 18.What reason did he give for this command? Verse 29.
  19. 19.What did he direct to be done with the unprofitable servant? What statement did he add? Verse 30.

Notes

1. The introduction of this parable with "For" indicates a continuation of the Saviour's theme as to how it will be at His coming. The parable of the faithful and the evil servant in chapter 24 would seem to apply primarily to leaders, those made responsible for others, and shows the evil result of misusing the time of delay before the Lord's coming. The parable of the ten virgins shows the danger of being thoughtless or careless, during the tarrying time, in making necessary a complete preparation for the Bridegroom's coming. In both these parables, it is urged that the only means of safety is to watch. Now this parable of the ten talents shows how to use the time till the Lord's return. The best way to watch is to engage actively in work for the Lord.

2. The lord is represented here as giving his goods to each servant according to his ability. No more is required of any man than the faithful, energetic use of his God-given ability, whatever that ability may be. Ability, talents, grow by use. It is not the Lord's plan that we merely preserve from waste the ability He gives us, if that were possible, without using it, but He expects an increase.
