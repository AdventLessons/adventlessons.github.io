Lesson 2 · July 10, 1897

Power from Above

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(Chapter 2, .)

Questions.

  1. 1.Where were the disciples on the day of Pentecost, and in what frame of mind were they?
  2. 2.What suddenly happened? What was the nature of the sound? Whence did it come? What did it do?
  3. 3.What appeared? What were these tongues like? Where did this phenomenon manifest itself?
  4. 4.What did the disciples receive, and in what measure? What was the immediate result?
  5. 5.Who were at this time dwelling in Jerusalem?
  6. 6.What took place as soon as the sound from heaven was heard? How were the multitude affected?
  7. 7.What question did they ask?
  8. 8.What was it that so astonished them?
  9. 9.What different nationalities, or tongues, were represented in the crowd that came together?
  10. 10.What did the people say they heard the Galilean fishermen speak in their various languages?
  11. 11.By what question did they express their amazement?
  12. 12.How did others account for the wonder?
  13. 13.Who proceeded to explain the wonderful occurrence?
  14. 14.How did he reply to the mockers?
  15. 15.What did he say this manifestation was?
  16. 16.From what prophecy, and what portion of the prophecy, did he quote?
  17. 17.What had the prophet said should be done? At what time was it to take place?
  18. 18.How extensively was the Spirit to be poured out? What would be the result?
  19. 19.What are God's servants and handmaidens to do in the last days?
  20. 20.What will appear in connection with this manifestation of the Spirit?
  21. 21.Name some of these wonders and signs.
  22. 22.What will they immediately precede?
  23. 23.What precious promise is sure?

Notes

Additional Questions and Suggestions.

1. Note that the Spirit of God comes in fulness and power where there is unity. By what is the Spirit grieved away?

2. Is there in this lesson any instruction as to how to "reach the masses," and to get crowds to listen to preaching? Can we use the Spirit to this end, or must the Spirit use us?

3. With this lesson compare . What special significance is there in the admonition not to be filled with wine, but to be filled with the Spirit?

4. What time does the Scripture say the Spirit was to be poured out as fulfilled at Pentecost? In what days must we, therefore, be living?

5. The Spirit of God is poured out on "all flesh;" why, then, are not the fruits of the Spirit seen in all? See . So with the gifts of the Spirit. In the apostolic church, the Lord confirmed the word with signs following. See . But when the church departed from the Word, then the signs departed from the church.
