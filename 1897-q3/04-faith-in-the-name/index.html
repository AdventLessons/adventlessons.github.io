Lesson 4 · July 24, 1897

Faith in the Name

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

(.)

Questions.

  1. 1.Recall Peter's talk on Pentecost.
  2. 2.What was done for those who gladly received the Word?
  3. 3.How many were there?
  4. 4.How did they continue?
  5. 5.What effect was produced on the people? What was done by the apostles?
  6. 6.How did all the believers live?
  7. 7.What did they do with their possessions?
  8. 8.What spirit characterized them?
  9. 9.As they praised God, and their lives honored Him, what did they have? and what did the Lord do?
  10. 10.On one of these days, what did Peter and John do? At what time?
  11. 11.Who was at the gate of the temple? Why was he there? How long had he been afflicted?
  12. 12.When he saw Peter and John, what did he do?
  13. 13.What did Peter do and say?
  14. 14.Why did the man give heed?
  15. 15.What did Peter then say? What did he tell the man to do?
  16. 16.What took place as Peter helped him to his feet?
  17. 17.How did his newly acquired strength affect him?
  18. 18.What was the effect on the people who recognized him?
  19. 19.How did the poor man show his gratitude and affection for those through whom he had been raised up? What did the people do?
  20. 20.When Peter saw it, what did he say to the people?
  21. 21.To whom did he direct their attention? In what terms did he refer to God? What did he say that God had done? What had they done?
  22. 22.Instead of the Giver of life, what had they desired?
  23. 23.To what did Peter again give witness?
  24. 24.What did he say had made the lame man strong? How complete was the man's cure? and what had given him that perfect soundness?

Notes

Additional Questions and Suggestions.

1. In what scripture do we find the words, "All they that hate Me, love death"? How is this strikingly illustrated in this lesson?

2. By what power was the lame man made to walk? Did he have anything to do with giving himself strength, save to accept the gift in faith? Who, then, was his strength? and by what power did he walk? Did the fact that Christ was his strength, and that it was Christ's power in him that made him walk, make it unnecessary for him to use his legs? What lesson may we learn from this?

3. "With the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation." . How does this lesson show that faith has power to produce actual righteousness?
