Lesson 3 · September 10, 1887

Whose Prayers Are Heard

PDFShare
  1. 1.What comforting statement is made in Psalms 34:15?
  2. 2.What contrast is made between the righteous and the wicked? John 9:31.
  3. 3.Repeat Proverbs 15:29.
  4. 4.How is the same principle brought out in 1 Pet. 3:12?
  5. 5.How does God regard the prayer of those who are given to vanity? Job 35:13.
  6. 6.What is said of the prayer of the hypocrite? Job 27:8, 9.
  7. 7.What hope have those who cherish a love for anything which God has set down as iniquity? Ps. 66:18.
  8. 8.What is the effect of iniquitous conduct? Isa. 59:2.
  9. 9.How does God regard the prayers of those who willfully turn away from his law? Prov. 28:9.
  10. 10.What course was taken by some of the Israelites in ancient times? Zech. 7:11, 12.
  11. 11.What was the consequence of their refusing to hearken to the word of the Lord? Verse 13.
  12. 12.What reason does the Lord give for not hearing the prayers of the people in the time of the prophet Isaiah? Isa. 1:15, last part of the verse.
  13. 13.Must people actually shed blood in order to be condemned by this passage? 1 John 3:15.
  14. 14.What does the prophet say in another place to show why the Lord will not hear the prayers of those who persist in sin? Isa. 59:1, 2.
  15. 15.In what ways do men show forth the promptings of an evil heart? Verse 3.
  16. 16.What is said of the way of the wicked? Prov. 15:9.
  17. 17.What counsel is given to those who are in this sad condition? Isa. 1:16.
  18. 18.What invitation is given to the penitent believer?—"Come now, and let us reason together, saith the Lord." Verse 18.
  19. 19.To what is this equivalent?—To an invitation to prayer.
  20. 20.What promise is made to those who accept this invitation? Last part of the verse.
  21. 21.What further assurance have we that God will hear the prayer of penitent sinners? 2 Chron. 7:14.
