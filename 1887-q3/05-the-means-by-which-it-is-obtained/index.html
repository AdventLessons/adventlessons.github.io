Lesson 2 · July 30, 1887

The Means by Which It Is Obtained

PDFShare
  1. 1.What is the condition of man in his fallen state? Rom. 3:19; Eph. 2:12, last clause.
  2. 2.What is made necessary by this condition?—The change called sanctification.
  3. 3.Who have the promise of seeing God? Matt. 5:8.
  4. 4.What scripture shows that unholy men will never be admitted into the presence of God? Heb. 12:14.
  5. 5.Is holiness attained by an inward work of God's grace or by works of righteousness? Ps. 51:6, 7; Tit. 3:5.
  6. 6.How may it be shown that faith is a prerequisite to justification and sanctification? Acts 10:43; Rom. 5:1.
  7. 7.How may it be proved that obedience is also necessary? Heb. 5:9.
  8. 8.From what scriptures does it appear that the Holy Spirit is an agency in the work of sanctification? Rom. 15:16, last clause; 2 Thess. 2:13.
  9. 9.What else is necessary to salvation? 2 Thess. 2:13, last part.
  10. 10.Through what agency did Christ pray that his followers might be sanctified? John 17:17.
  11. 11.What does Peter testify on this point? 1 Pet. 1:22.
  12. 12.How does David speak of the word of God? Ps. 119:105.
  13. 13.How does he say the young may cleanse their way? Ps. 119:9.
  14. 14.From what do all need to be cleansed?—From sin.
  15. 15.What is sin? 1 John 3:4.
  16. 16.Where is the law,—the commandments of God,—called the truth? Ps. 119:142, 151.
  17. 17.Can one have a true view of the character of sin who disregards the law of God? Rom. 7:7; 3:20.
  18. 18.What does James say about this law of liberty? James 1:25; 2:12.
  19. 19.What did Dr. Adam Clarke say on this point?—"Man cannot have a true notion of sin but by means of the law of God."
