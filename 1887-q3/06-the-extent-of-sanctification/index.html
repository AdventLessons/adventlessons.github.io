Lesson 3 · August 6, 1887

The Extent of Sanctification

PDFShare
  1. 1.What is Paul's prayer respecting the sanctification of believers? 1 Thess. 5:23.
  2. 2.What desire does he express in regard to their preservation in this state? Same verse.
  3. 3.To what time does he refer? Last clause, same verse.
  4. 4.When must those here referred to be found living?—Just before the coming of the Saviour.
  5. 5.How much which pertains to the person does his prayer embrace?—Every mental and physical power and faculty, and every act of the life.
  6. 6.Give passages in which the word spirit manifestly refers to the mind. 1 Cor. 5:3; Col. 2:5.
  7. 7.Then what must the word "spirit" mean in 1 Thess. 5:23?—The mind.
  8. 8.What terms are used to express the conditions of the mind that lead to life or death? Rom. 8:5, 6.
  9. 9.What should the unrighteous man do if he would have his thoughts pleasing to God? Isa. 55:7.
  10. 10.What contrast is drawn between the thoughts of sinful man and those of God? Isa. 55:8, 9.
  11. 11.In what scriptures do the terms "soul" and "life" appear to be used interchangeably? Matt. 16:25, 26.—(See note.)
  12. 12.In the work of conversion and sanctification, what change is wrought in the life? 2 Cor. 5:17; Eph. 2:10.
  13. 13.How may it be known that men have become new creatures in Christ Jesus? Matt. 7:20.
  14. 14.What are the fruits of the Spirit? Gal. 5:22, 23.
  15. 15.What are the works of the flesh? Gal. 5:19-21.
  16. 16.Why did the Saviour give himself for us? Tit. 2:14; 1 Pet. 2:9.
  17. 17.What influence does the blessed hope of the near coming of the Saviour have upon the heart and life of the believer? 1 John 3:1-3.
  18. 18.What encouraging words were spoken by Paul in his farewell address to the brethren at Miletus? Acts 20:32.
  19. 19.How does the apostle speak of the body and its members as sharing in the work of holiness? Rom. 12:1; 6:13, 19, last clause.
  20. 20.How does he elsewhere speak of the body? 1 Cor. 6:19, 20; Heb. 3:6.
  21. 21.What relation does temperance sustain to this work? 1 Cor. 9:25.
  22. 22.What example has Paul left us in overcoming? Verses 26, 27.
  23. 23.How does the apostle show that the appetite should be sanctified? 1 Cor. 10:31.

Notes

On Matt. 16:26, Dr. Adam Clarke says: "On what authority many here translate the word psuche in the 25th verse, life, and in this verse, soul, I know not, but am certain it means life in both places." This same word is rendered life forty times in the New Testament.
