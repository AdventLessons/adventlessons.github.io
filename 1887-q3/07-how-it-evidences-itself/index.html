Lesson 4 · August 13, 1887

How It Evidences Itself

PDFShare
  1. 1.In what condition is man in his fallen state represented to be? Eph. 2:12, last clause.
  2. 2.How generally is this true of men under the power of sin? Rom. 3:11, 12, 19.
  3. 3.What is the cry of the soul when fully aroused to a sense of its condition by the word and Spirit of God? Rom. 7:24; Acts 2:37, last clause.
  4. 4.What was the answer to this question? V. 38.
  5. 5.How fully may the condemnation of sin be removed? Rom. 8:1; Isa. 1:18.
  6. 6.To what class is a special promise made in this connection? Isa. 1:19.
  7. 7.Why did God send his "Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemn sin in the flesh"? Rom. 8:4.
  8. 8.What is said of those who are led by the Spirit of God? Rom. 8:14.
  9. 9.What words of comfort do we find in John to the brethren? 1 John 3:2.
  10. 10.What special preparation of heart is needed to be ready to meet the Lord? Zeph. 2:1-3.
  11. 11.Will the day of the Lord come upon his people as a thief? 1 Thess. 5:4, 5.
  12. 12.What injunctions are here given? Verses 6, 8.
  13. 13.How are those who will stand with the Lamb upon Mount Zion (Rev. 14:1) spoken of? Rev. 14:5.
  14. 14.Are we to expect sanctification without obedience? 1 Pet. 1:2.
  15. 15.What does true holiness teach us? Tit. 2:12, 13.
  16. 16.How does the apostle pray with reference to this subject? Heb. 13:21.
  17. 17.Should we look for this work to be accomplished at once? 1 Pet. 5:10.
