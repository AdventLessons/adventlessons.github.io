Lesson 11 · September 9, 1905

The Holy Spirit Grieved

PDFShare

Questions.

  1. 1.What are we admonished not to do? Eph. 4:30.
  2. 2.How did the unfaithfulness of the children of Israel affect the Lord? Ps. 95:10.
  3. 3.For whom especially was the experience of the children of Israel written? 1 Cor. 10:9-12.
  4. 4.Had Israel heeded the counsel of God, what glorious victories would have been theirs? Ps. 81:13-16.
  5. 5.What is the longing desire of Christ? John 17:24.
  6. 6.What will hasten the accomplishment of this desire? Matt. 24:14.
  7. 7.What experience of special blessing is the Lord waiting to bestow upon His people? Mal. 3:10.
  8. 8.To how many must this message of life be proclaimed? Rev. 14:6.
  9. 9.What is said concerning the closing work of this message? Rev. 18:1; Isa. 60:1-3.
  10. 10.What will be the result to him who knows his duty and does it not? Luke 12:47.
  11. 11.Who only will abide in the burning day? Isa. 33:12-17. Note.
  12. 12.To what are the righteous compared? Ps. 125:1.
  13. 13.What exhortation is given? 1 Cor. 15:58.
  14. 14.What awful warning against grieving the Holy Spirit is recorded in the experience of the early church? Acts 5:1-11.

Notes

In Isa. 33:15, speaking of him that will "dwell" and not be consumed amid the everlasting burnings that cleanse the earth from sin and sinners (2 Peter 3:7), the Revised Version reads: "He that walketh righteously and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of fraud," etc. "To defraud God is the greatest crime of which man can be guilty; and yet this sin is deep and wide-spread. Through the prophet Malachi, God says: 'Will a man rob God? yet ye have robbed Me . . . in tithes and offerings.'"—Mrs. E. G. White, in Review and Herald, Oct. 13, 1896.
