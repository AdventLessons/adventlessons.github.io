Thirteenth Sabbath Offering
June 28, 1941—Southern Asia

Map

When the Silver Jubilee Offering was taken in 1937, the Southern Asia Division was the recipient of $10,000; in 1939, when the Thirteenth Sabbath Offering was devoted to this same division, the overflow amounted to $7,457.37. We rejoice that our Sabbath school members have so generously given of their means in the past to help in the mission work of this field.

On Sabbath, June 28, we have another golden opportunity to give to Southern Asia. The overflow is dedicated to medical missionary work in Burma. Doors of soul-winning opportunity once open to us in foreign fields are now closed. Burma is still open, and millions need the message of the Great Physician. Let us give generously on June 28.

Help your school reach its goal of one dollar a member!
