Lesson 12 · March 20, 1909

Faith and Peace

PDFShare

Phil. 4:1-8.

Questions

  1. 1.With what exhortation does the fourth chapter of Philippians open? Phil. 4:1, 2.
  2. 2.What evidence of care did Paul show for those who, in a humble capacity, had labored with him in the gospel? Verse 3.
  3. 3.What exhortation is given in verse 4?
  4. 4.What is said about anxiety? Verses 5, 6.
  5. 5.Why is there no need of being anxious? Matt. 6:25-34; 1 Peter 5:7. Note.
  6. 6.Knowing all these things, how should we make our requests to God? Phil. 4:6.
  7. 7.What promise is made to those who ask in faith? Mark 11:24.
  8. 8.When one has such faith as this, what will be the result? Phil. 4:7; Rom. 5:1.
  9. 9.What admonition is given? Phil. 4:8.
  10. 10.What determines a person's character? Prov. 23:7.
  11. 11.What, then, is the character of those who think of that which is true, just, pure, and good?
  12. 12.How is love defined? Rom. 13:10.
  13. 13.What is one of its chief characteristics? 1 Cor. 13:5.
  14. 14.What is the first characteristic of the wisdom that comes from above? James 3:17.
  15. 15.How should the word of Christ dwell in us? Col. 3:16.

Notes

The American Revised Version of Ps. 37:8 reads as follows: "Cease from anger, and forsake wrath: fret not thyself, it tendeth only to evil-doing."

This text gives the true idea of all the fretting and worry in life. It all tends simply to evil.

"In the heart of Christ, where reigned perfect harmony with God, there was perfect peace. He was never elated by applause, nor dejected by censure or disappointment. Amid the greatest opposition and the most cruel treatment, He was still of good courage."—"Desire of Ages," page 330.
