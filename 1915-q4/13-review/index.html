Chapter Outlines and Questions

Chapter 16:13-28 — "Thou Art the Christ;" "Get Thee Behind Me, Satan;" Taking Up the Cross

  1. 1.Under what circumstances did Peter declare the deity of Christ?
  2. 2.What was the response of Jesus?
  3. 3.What gave occasion for Christ to rebuke Peter?
  4. 4.Give the reasons and the reward for one's taking up the cross and following Jesus.

Chapter 17 — The Transfiguration; Healing of the Afflicted Son; Paying Tribute

  1. 1.What do you learn from the transfiguration?
  2. 2.What lesson of faith did Jesus connect with the healing of the afflicted son?
  3. 3.What does Christ's example in the paying of the tribute teach us?

Chapter 18 — Lessons from a Little Child; The Ninety and Nine; Reconciling a Trespassing Brother; Parable on Forgiveness

  1. 1.What lessons did Jesus teach from setting a little child in the midst? From the parable of the ninety and nine?
  2. 2.How may a trespassing brother be restored?
  3. 3.Give the occasion for the parable on forgiveness, and the lesson drawn from it.

Chapter 19 — The Marriage Relation; "Suffer Little Children;" Rich Young Man Who Desired Eternal Life; Reward of Forsaking All for Christ

  1. 1.Why could not the rich young man have eternal life? Who then can be saved?
  2. 2.What did Jesus promise Peter for those who forsake all and follow Him?

Chapter 20 — Parable of the Laborers; The Greatness of Service; Healing the Two Blind Men

  1. 1.What principles are taught in the parable of the laborers in the vineyard?
  2. 2.What is the road to greatness among Christ's followers?
  3. 3.What lesson may we learn from the healing of the two blind men?

Chapter 21 — The Triumphal Entry into Jerusalem; Cleansing of the Temple; Cursing the Fig Tree; The Question of Authority; Parable of the Two Sons; Parable of the Wicked Husbandmen

  1. 1.What help is there for the Christian in the story of the triumphal entry? In the cleansing of the temple?
  2. 2.What lesson from the acted parable of the fig tree did Jesus give the Jews? The disciples?
  3. 3.How did Jesus deal with the challenge of His authority by the Jews?
  4. 4.What is the leading lesson in the parable of the two sons? In the parable of the wicked husbandmen?
