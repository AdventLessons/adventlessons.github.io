Lesson 6 · November 6, 1915

Loyalty to God's Plan

Lesson Scripture: Matt. 19:1-15.

Questions

  1. 1.Where did Jesus go from Galilee? Matt. 19:1.
  2. 2.Who followed Him? Verse 2. What did He do for them?
  3. 3.By what question did the Pharisees tempt Jesus? Verse 3.
  4. 4.In reply, how did Jesus say the Creator made mankind at the beginning? Verse 4. Note 1.
  5. 5.In harmony with His plan, what did the Creator say a man should do? Verse 5. Note 2.
  6. 6.What conclusion did Jesus draw from the facts cited? Verse 6, first part.
  7. 7.What admonition does the Saviour then give? Verse 6, last part.
  8. 8.What is the marriage relation employed in the Bible to represent? Eph. 5:24. Note 3.
  9. 9.How are husbands to love their wives? Verse 25.
  10. 10.What was Christ's purpose in giving Himself for the church? Verses 26, 27.
  11. 11.In what other way is it said that men ought to love their wives? Verse 28.
  12. 12.What mutual relation should exist between husband and wife? Verse 33.
  13. 13.What additional instruction is given on the marriage relation? 1 Peter 3:7.
  14. 14.Who were brought to Jesus? Matt. 19:13, first part. For what purpose were they brought?
  15. 15.What did the disciples do? Verse 13, last part.
  16. 16.What did Jesus say? Verse 14.
  17. 17.What did He then do? Verse 15.

Notes

1. In referring the Pharisees to the inspired record, Jesus set us an example of how to answer the questions of unbelievers, or of any who may be seeking light sincerely. It is better to let the Bible answer questions as far as possible. It is our part to become familiar enough with its teachings that we may readily quote from it, or refer to it on any occasion.

2. "And the Lord God said, It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him an help meet for him." Gen. 2:18.

"As the Creator joined the hands of the holy pair in wedlock, saying, 'A man shall leave his father and mother, and shall cleave unto his wife; and they shall be one,' He enunciated the law of marriage for all the children of Adam to the close of time. That which the eternal Father Himself had pronounced good, was the law of highest blessing and development for man."—"Mount of Blessing," pages 99, 100.

3. There are striking points of likeness between the marriage relation and the believer's relation with Christ: 1. It is voluntary; "whosoever will," let him come. 2. It is for life; "no man, having put his hand to the plow, and looking back, is fit for the kingdom of God." 3. It is a holy alliance; nothing but sin can break the tie. 4. It is a renouncing of all others; "Christ, only Christ." 5. It is a sharing in sorrow and in joy; "if so be that we suffer with Him, that we may be also glorified together."
