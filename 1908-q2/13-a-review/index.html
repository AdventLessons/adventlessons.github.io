Lesson 13 · June 27, 1908

A Review

PDFShare

Questions

  1. 1.What is revealed in the Gospel? Rom. 1:16, 17.
  2. 2.What brought salvation to Noah and his family, and what did he preach in view of the impending destruction of the world? Heb. 11:7. 2 Peter 2:5.
  3. 3.When the Gospel was preached to Abraham how did he relate himself to it? Gal. 3:8; Gen. 15:6.
  4. 4.How was Abraham's faith manifested? Heb. 11:8.
  5. 5.Upon what is based the promised inheritance, the new earth? Gal. 3:18; Rom. 4:13.
  6. 6.What will be the experience of those who are permitted to share in the benefits of this promise? Gal. 3:6, 7; Rom. 4:16; Gal. 3:29.
  7. 7.What was manifested on the part of the people of God in the deliverance from Egypt and the conquest of Canaan? Heb. 11:23-30.
  8. 8.What caused the downfall of ancient Babylon? Dan. 5:18, 20, 22-24, 30, 31.
  9. 9.Upon what opposite experience was the deliverance from Babylon based? Hab. 2:4.
  10. 10.What was the work of John the Baptist? Luke 1:17.
  11. 11.What prophecy formed the basis of his message? John 1:23; Isa. 40:6-10.
  12. 12.How is it shown in his teaching of this message that it demanded personal faith in Christ and His righteousness? Matt. 3:9; John 1:29.
  13. 13.In what movement is the message of John the Baptist again proclaimed and his work completed? Isa. 40:10; Rev. 22:12.
  14. 14.What fundamental Gospel principle was the foundation of the Reformation from popery in the sixteenth century? Read Lesson Nine.
  15. 15.What impending event gives special force to the final proclamation of the Gospel? Rev. 14:6, 7.
  16. 16.What work is carried forward while this last message is going to the world? Dan. 8:13, 14.
  17. 17.What warning constitutes a part of this Three-fold Message? Rev. 14:9, 10.
  18. 18.To whose worship is the world called by the preaching of the everlasting Gospel? Verses 6, 7.
  19. 19.Since there is only one Gospel, what must be revealed in the preaching of the last Gospel message? Rom. 1:16, 17.

Notes

In the history of the Gospel there have been seven great reform movements:

1. The Flood and the preaching of Noah.

2. The call of Abraham.

3. The deliverance from Egypt.

4. The restoration from Babylon.

5. The message of John the Baptist.

6. The reformation of the sixteenth century.

7. The great second advent movement.

Each has been a movement to present the Gospel of righteousness by faith in its purity and power.

This series of lessons ought to make it clear that this last Gospel message is in line with the preaching of the Gospel in all the great crises of history in the past, and that it must necessarily be a proclamation of righteousness by faith. In this generation the everlasting Gospel is to be presented according to the outline given in Rev. 14:6-12, and in view of the impending destruction of the world and the setting up of the everlasting kingdom of God at the second advent of Christ. This will give a freshness and power to this great doctrine, and enable it to meet the needs of this time in restoring and placing in their proper setting all the truths which have been either perverted or set aside altogether in the great apostasy.
