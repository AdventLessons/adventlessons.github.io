Lesson 9 · May 31, 1902

The Law of God in the Ark and in the Heart

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

The tabernacle, as a mere building for the indwelling glory (), was a constant prophecy of the divine-human temple in which His glory was to be manifested (). The services of the tabernacle were an actual provision for present salvation, which was also a promise of the ministry of Christ in and through His taking the flesh. . The tabernacle was the home of the ark (), which was the receptacle for the law of God, the ten commandments (). All the services of the tabernacle had reference to man's relationship to that law (; ) and the mediatorial work of Christ, through which the law would be put into the mind and heart of man (). This was accomplished by His coming in the flesh to live the law in and for humanity. . Thus the power of the endless life was provided in the place of merely fleshly effort (), and the dead letter was changed into the living promise (; ). God made choice of Abraham as the head of a people to whom He committed in a special manner the keeping of the truths of the gospel (), that through them He might teach His law to the world as the blessing of His indwelling life to save from sin, through the mediation of His Son in the flesh (). Their whole experience thereafter was a preparation for, and a fact-prophecy of, the coming Messiah, the incarnation of the Son of God. Not only did "all the prophets" teach this fundamental truth (), but it was brought out in their history. Just in proportion as they made the teaching of the sanctuary and its services a reality in their lives, the power of this truth was shown in national prosperity. . If they refused to make this revelation of the truth through the obedience of faith, then came national adversity. . And even in the men whom God raised up to deliver His people when they turned to Him, He was testifying to them and to the world of the salvation through the great Deliverer, who was to reveal Himself in humanity. Questions

  1. 1.What was declared to be the purpose for which the tabernacle was made?
  2. 2.What was to be revealed in it?
  3. 3.Of what was this a prophecy?
  4. 4.Show from the Scripture the lesson which was taught concerning the work of the coming Saviour by the services of the sanctuary.
  5. 5.What useful purpose did the tabernacle serve as a building?
  6. 6.What was deposited in the ark?
  7. 7.To what did all the services of the tabernacle have either direct or indirect reference?
  8. 8.What was the result really sought?
  9. 9.What made such a result as this possible?
  10. 10.What experience was thus provided?
  11. 11.In thus providing this experience, what change was made?
  12. 12.What did God entrust to the keeping of His people?
  13. 13.For what purpose were such gifts bestowed upon them?
  14. 14.What was their whole experience thus intended to be?
  15. 15.How was the truth of the coming of Christ in the flesh taught?
  16. 16.What was the result when they accepted the truths of the sanctuary as a living experience?
  17. 17.What was the result when they failed to do this?
  18. 18.In what way did God use even their experiences of adversity as a means of revealing the great truth of the gospel?
