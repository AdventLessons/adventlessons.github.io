Lesson 13 · December 28, 1918

Review

PDFShare

Lesson Scripture: Rev. 14:1 to 22:21.

Questions

The Threefold Message

  1. 1.Repeat the first angel's message. The second. The third. Rev. 14:6-11.
  2. 2.How are those described who heed this message? Verse 12.
  3. 3.How large a company will gain the victory over the beast and his image? Where did John see them standing? How is their character further described? Verses 1-5.

The Harvest of the Earth

  1. 4.What two classes are gathered at the harvest of the earth? What is done with the wheat when it is reaped? What is done with the vine of the earth? Rev. 14:14-20. Compare Matt. 13:38-43.
  2. 5.What terrible judgments come upon the ungodly at the harvest time? Enumerate the seven last plagues. Revelation 15, 16.

Babylon the Great

  1. 6.How is the character of Babylon set forth? Rev. 17:5, 6; 18:2, 5.
  2. 7.What position will she occupy just before her fall? Rev. 18:7.
  3. 8.How is her end described? Rev. 17:16; 18:8, 21.
  4. 9.Repeat the call to God's people. Rev. 18:4.

The Smiting of the Nations

  1. 10.Who leads the armies of heaven in the conflict with the nations of earth? How is He described? Rev. 19:11-14.
  2. 11.How is the conflict described? What is the outcome of the battle? Verses 19-21.

The Hallelujah Chorus

  1. 12.After Christ and His saints had completed their victory over all opposing forces, what grand chorus did John hear? Rev. 19:6.
  2. 13.What further cause had the saints for rejoicing? Verse 7.
  3. 14.What is said of those who partake of this marriage feast? Verses 8, 9. Compare Matt. 22:11-13.

The Binding of Satan

  1. 15.When, where, and for how long is Satan bound? Rev. 20:1-3.
  2. 16.What is the condition of the earth during the thousand years? Isa. 24:1-3, 19-23; Jer. 4:23-27.
  3. 17.Where are the saints during this time? Rev. 20:4.
  4. 18.When does the second resurrection take place? Who are then raised to life, and what do they do? Verses 5-9.
  5. 19.What is the second death? Verses 10, 13-15.

Our Future Home

  1. 20.What comes forth from the cleansing fires of the last days? Rev. 21:1. Compare 2 Peter 3:10-13.
  2. 21.Describe the capital city of the new earth. Rev. 21:10-21.
  3. 22.Who only can enter this beautiful city? What sins are mentioned which will debar one from its pleasures? Verses 8, 27.
  4. 23.What blessed relation exists between God and man in the new earth state? Verses 3, 4, 7; 22:3-5.
  5. 24.What glorious gospel invitation is found near the close of this book? Rev. 22:17.
  6. 25.Repeat the closing words of the book. Verses 20, 21.
