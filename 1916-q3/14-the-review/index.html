Lesson Scripture: Acts 1:1 to 8:3.

In order to keep clear in mind the thread of the narrative in the book of Acts, we should note carefully the main topics and chapter divisions.

Main Topics

  1. 1.From the Resurrection to the Ascension of Christ. Acts 1:1-11.
  2. 2.Awaiting the Fulfillment of the Promise. Acts 1:12-26.
  3. 3.Events on the Day of Pentecost. Acts 2:1-41.
  4. 4.Steadfastness in the Faith. Acts 2:42-47.
  5. 5.Witnessing by Peter and John. Acts 3:1 to 4:31.
  6. 6.Having All Things Common. Acts 4:32 to 5:11.
  7. 7.Working of Signs and Wonders. Acts 5:12-17.
  8. 8.Persecution of Peter and John. Acts 5:18-42.
  9. 9.Organization of the Church. Acts 6:1-8.
  10. 10.Arrest, Defense, and Martyrdom of Stephen. Acts 6:9 to 8:3.

Chapter Divisions

Read one chapter through each day, and meditate upon its contents.

Chapter 1: Ascension of Christ; Waiting for the Promise of the Father.

Chapter 2: Day of Pentecost; Steadfastness in the Faith.

Chapter 3: Peter and John at the Temple.

Chapter 4: Trial of Peter and John; Second Pentecostal Blessing; Having All Things Common.

Chapter 5: Sin of Ananias and Sapphira; Working of Signs and Wonders; Second Imprisonment and Trial of Peter and John.

Chapter 6: Organization of the Church; Arrest of Stephen.

Chapter 7: Defense and Martyrdom of Stephen.

Lessons for Us

We should endeavor to recall and fix in mind the great outstanding principles and promises in these seven chapters, for our daily life and work; such as:

  1. 1.This same Jesus shall come again. (Memorize Acts 1:11.)
  2. 2."Ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you."
  3. 3."Ye shall be witnesses unto Me" at home and "unto the uttermost part of the earth."
  4. 4.Pentecostal power comes upon believers when they "continue with one accord in prayer and supplication," in confession, and in study of the promises.
  5. 5.The gift of the Holy Spirit is promised to converts on repentance and baptism. (Memorize Acts 2:38, 39.)
  6. 6."Such as I have give I."
  7. 7."Your sins may be blotted out." (Memorize Acts 3:19.)
  8. 8.Salvation in Christ only. (Memorize Acts 4:12.)
  9. 9."We ought to obey God rather than men." (Memorize Acts 5:29.)
  10. 10."Lord, lay not this sin to their charge." (Memorize Acts 7:60.)
