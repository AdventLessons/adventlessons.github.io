Lesson 10 · December 5, 1908

Putting Off the Old Man: Putting On the New

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

.

Questions

  1. 1.In whom should we grow up, and to what extent? .
  2. 2.What beautiful figure of Christian growth is given by the apostle? .
  3. 3.What admonition, therefore, does he give? .
  4. 4.What is the condition of those who are out of Christ? From what are they alienated? What is the condition of the heart? .
  5. 5.To what sad condition does wilful blindness at last lead? .
  6. 6.Are these things practised by Christians? .
  7. 7.What are Christians to put off? How is the "old man" described? .
  8. 8.What change is called for? .
  9. 9.What is the believer to put on? .
  10. 10.How does Jeremiah illustrate the impossibility of bringing about this change by one's own power? .
  11. 11.What things are mentioned which are to be put away? . Note 1.
  12. 12.What exhortation is given regarding our words? .
  13. 13.What should we not grieve? What does the Holy Spirit do for the believer? .
  14. 14.What spirit should we manifest toward others? What should be the measure of our forgiveness toward those who wrong us? . Note 2.

Notes

1. Note the "wherefore" in . If the "old man with his deeds" has been put off, and the "new man" has been put on, then the fruits of godliness will surely follow.

2. "The difficulties we have to encounter may be very much lessened by that meekness which hides itself in Christ. If we possess the humility of our Master, we shall rise above the slights, the rebuffs, the annoyances to which we are daily exposed, and they will cease to cast a gloom over the spirit. The highest evidence of nobility in a Christian is self-control."— page ⟦301?⟧.
