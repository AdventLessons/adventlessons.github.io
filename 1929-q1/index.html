The Next Thirteenth Sabbath Offering

L. L. Caviness [Sabbath School Secretary of the European Division]

The fields which are looking for an overflow offering on Sabbath, March 30, have more than one third of the entire population of Europe. The total appropriations voted for last year for all these countries amounted to a little over $180,000, or about one dollar per thousand population. We feel certain that our faithful Sabbath school members will give generously on the next thirteenth Sabbath that the third angel's message may go rapidly to the Roman Catholic lands of Europe.
