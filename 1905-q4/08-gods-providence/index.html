Lesson 8 · November 25, 1905

God's Providence

This lesson PDF Whole quarter PDF · 13 lessonsShare on WhatsApp

Questions.

  1. 1.Do angels ever visit rulers to bring deliverance to God's people? ; .
  2. 2.How was Ahasuerus affected the night after Esther's first banquet? .
  3. 3.What command was given? .
  4. 4.What was found in the Chronicles? . Was it found by accident?
  5. 5.How long since Mordecai had saved the life of the king? . Compare marginal dates.
  6. 6.On hearing the Chronicles read, what question was asked by the king? .
  7. 7.What reply was given? .
  8. 8.Who entered the court at this time, and for what purpose? . Note 1.
  9. 9.With what question was Haman greeted when he entered the king's presence? .
  10. 10.What similarity is seen between the workings of Haman and the "mystery of iniquity"? (last clause); .
  11. 11.With whom did this evil principle originate? .
  12. 12.What was there in Haman's answer that showed that he, like Satan, coveted the position of the one ruling over him? .
  13. 13.What answer was given by the king? . What followed? .
  14. 14.How could this event be interpreted by the two men? Note 2.
  15. 15.How were the two men affected by the event? . Note 3.
  16. 16.What is there in Zeresh's remarks that shows that she was acquainted with the prophecy, in regard to the destruction of Haman's family by the Jews? .

Notes

1. Haman was like the wicked in the last days. "In some places, before the time for the decree to be executed, the wicked rushed upon the saints to slay them; but angels, in the form of men of war, fought for them."—.

2. Haman, filled with shame and hatred, could but see in it the beginning of his downfall. Mordecai, meekly and humbly trusting God, must have taken it as an evidence that God had answered prayer for the deliverance of the Jews.

3. Mordecai was not filled with pride; he humbly returned to his post of duty at the king's gate; but Haman, unfitted for business, returned to his home.
