Lesson 6 · November 11, 1905

Promulgation of the Decree

PDFShare

Questions.

  1. 1.Give the substance of Ahasuerus's decree. Esther 3:13.
  2. 2.How much time was given to prepare for the execution of the decree? Verses 12, 13.
  3. 3.Was the attack to be made in all parts of the nation on the same day? Verse 13.
  4. 4.Give the points of similarity between the decree to be issued against God's people, and the decree of Ahasuerus. Note.
  5. 5.How urgent were they in the days of Esther? Esther 3:14, 15.
  6. 6.How was the decree received by the people? Verse 15 (last clause).
  7. 7.What effect did the decree have upon Mordecai? Esther 4:1, 2.
  8. 8.As the messengers went from province to province, how were the Jews affected? Verse 3.
  9. 9.How did Esther hear of Mordecai's grief, and what did she do? Verses 4-6.
  10. 10.Give the substance of the message Mordecai sent to Esther. Verses 7, 8.
  11. 11.What excuse did Esther make? Verses 10, 11.
  12. 12.Give Mordecai's reply. Verses 13, 14.
  13. 13.Who at a former time was placed next to the throne that he might deliver God's people in the time of a crisis? Gen. 45:4-8.
  14. 14.What expression shows that Mordecai had the victory before he sent this message to Esther? Esther 4:14.
  15. 15.Give another instance where the victory was gained before the crisis was reached. John 11:41.
  16. 16.Is it our privilege to gain the victory before we come into the conflict? 1 John 5:4.

Notes

"When the protection of human laws shall be withdrawn from those who honor the law of God, there will be, in different lands, a simultaneous movement for their destruction. As the time appointed in the decree draws near, the people will conspire to root out the hated sect. It will be determined in one night to strike a decisive blow, which shall entirely silence the voice of dissent and reproof."—Great Controversy, Chap. 40, first paragraph.
