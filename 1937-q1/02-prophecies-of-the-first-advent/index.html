Lesson 2 · January 9, 1937

Prophecies of the First Advent

PDFShare

Daily Lesson Study: Place a check mark in the proper space.

Memory Verse: "Unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon His shoulder: and His name shall be called Wonderful, Counselor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace." Isa. 9:6.

Study Helps: "Prophets and Kings," pp. 681-702; "The Desire of Ages," pp. 19-30.

The Seed and Deliverer

  1. 1.What use did Jesus make of the testimony of the Old Testament prophets concerning Himself? Luke 24:27, 44.
  2. 2.How extensive was this testimony? Acts 3:24.

Notes

"For more than a thousand years the Jewish people had awaited the Saviour's coming. Upon this event they had rested their brightest hopes. In song and prophecy, in temple rite and household prayer, they had enshrined His name. And yet at His coming they knew Him not. . . . Yet God had chosen Israel. He had called them to preserve among men the knowledge of His law, and of the symbols and prophecies that pointed to the Saviour."—"The Desire of Ages," p. 27.

  1. 3.What is the first promise of a Saviour and Deliverer? Gen. 3:15.

Notes

"The Saviour's coming was foretold in Eden. When Adam and Eve first heard the promise, they looked for its speedy fulfillment. They joyfully welcomed their first-born son, hoping that he might be the Deliverer. But the fulfillment of the promise tarried. Those who first received it, died without the sight. From the days of Enoch the promise was repeated through patriarchs and prophets, keeping alive the hope of His appearing, and yet He came not."—Id., p. 31.

  1. 4.What promise was made to Abraham? Gen. 17:1-3; 22:17, 18.
  2. 5.What is the extent of the promise as to the land? As to the seed? Who is primarily the seed? Rom. 4:13; Gal. 3:29, 16.
  3. 6.What promise did God make to Israel through Moses concerning the Messiah, the One who was to come? Deut. 18:18, 19.
  4. 7.What evidence is there that some in the time of Christ recognized in Jesus of Nazareth the One of whom Moses wrote? John 1:45; Acts 3:20-23.
  5. 8.What prophecy did Jeremiah make concerning the house of King David? Jer. 23:5, 6.
  6. 9.What fact did the Jews in the time of Christ recognize concerning the Messiah's relationship to David? Matt. 22:41, 42; 21:9.

The Virgin Birth

  1. 10.What did the angel Gabriel testify as to the ancestry of the child that was to be born of the virgin Mary? Luke 1:30-33.
  2. 11.How does the prophet describe the child that was to be born? Isa. 9:6, 7.
  3. 12.How does Isaiah set forth the supernatural birth of the Messiah? Isa. 7:14.
  4. 13.How does one of the inspired writers of the Gospels describe the birth of Jesus? Matt. 1:18-21; see also Luke 1:26-38.
  5. 14.What prophecy does he say all this fulfilled? Matt. 1:22, 23.
  6. 15.How did the leaders of the Jews, in the time of Christ, know where the Messiah was to be born? Matt. 2:3-6; Micah 5:2.
  7. 16.How might they have known the very year when Jesus was to be revealed as the Messiah? Dan. 9:24-27.

Notes

The "seventy weeks" of Daniel 9:24-26 were to begin 457 B. C., when Artaxerxes granted to Ezra the commission "to restore and to build Jerusalem." "Taking this as the starting point, there was perfect harmony in the application of all the events foretold in the explanation of that period in Dan. 9:25-27. Sixty-nine weeks, the first 483 of the 2300 years, were to reach to the Messiah, the Anointed One; and Christ's baptism and anointing by the Holy Spirit, A. D. 27, exactly fulfilled the specification. In the midst of the seventieth week, Messiah was to be cut off. Three and a half years after His baptism, Christ was crucified, in the spring of A. D. 31."—"The Great Controversy," p. 410.

The Work and Suffering of Christ

  1. 17.What did the prophet say of the work that the Messiah should do when He appeared? Isa. 61:1-3.
  2. 18.What indicates that Jesus understood this prophecy to refer to Himself? Luke 4:16-21.
  3. 19.To what did Jesus refer repeatedly to prove His Messiahship? Matt. 11:2-5; John 10:38.
  4. 20.What would be the attitude of the people toward the Messiah when He should appear among them? Isa. 53:3.
  5. 21.In what striking language are His suffering and death set forth? Verses 7-9.
  6. 22.Why did the Sinless One endure all this shame and suffering? Verse 5.

Notes

"Christ was treated as we deserve, that we might be treated as He deserves. He was condemned for our sins, in which He had no share, that we might be justified by His righteousness, in which we had no share. He suffered the death which was ours, that we might receive the life which was His. 'With His stripes we are healed.' "—"The Desire of Ages," p. 25.

Lesson Outline

[Faith in Christ as the Son of God, our Saviour and Redeemer, is confirmed and strengthened by an understanding of the prophecies concerning Him, and their fulfillment in His life and ministry. Without the background of prophecy, the sonship of Christ is to many a mystery.]

  1. I.The Seed and the Deliverer 1. Christ used Old Testament prophecies to teach His disciples concerning Himself. Luke 24:27, 44. 2. The seed mentioned in Genesis 3:15; 22:17, 18 is primarily Christ, the son of David. 3. Christ was recognized while among men as the Messiah, the one of whom Old Testament writers spoke. John 1:45.
  2. II.The Virgin Birth 1. The angel Gabriel testified that the Child Jesus was the Son of God, the seed of David. Luke 1:30-33. 2. Christ's miraculous birth was predicted by Isaiah, who is quoted by Matthew. Isa. 7:14. 3. Daniel predicted the year of His baptism. Dan. 9:24-26.
  3. III.The Work and Suffering of Christ 1. Christ's work for the lost and undone was foretold by Isaiah. Isa. 61:1-3. 2. The same prophet predicted His sufferings and death. Isa. 53:3-9.
