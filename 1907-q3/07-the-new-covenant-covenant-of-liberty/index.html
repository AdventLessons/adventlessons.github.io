Lesson 7 · August 17, 1907

The New Covenant—Covenant of Liberty

  2. 2.Who alone can make us free? .
  3. 3.Are there others who promise liberty? What is their condition? .
  4. 4.Did Satan promise greater liberty when he led Eve to break God's covenant? .
  5. 5.But what was and is always the result? .
  6. 6.Who are free, and how? .
  7. 7.When thus made free what are we admonished to do? .
  8. 8.What in Christ makes us free? .
  9. 9.How did the psalmist say he could walk in freedom? .
  10. 10.What is the law of liberty? .
  11. 11.How do we attain the truth which is in Christ? .

  15. 15.What was the manner anciently of confirming a covenant of liberty? .
  16. 16.When God confirmed His covenant with Abraham, what was done? .
  17. 17.What did this divided sacrifice represent? Ans. It represents our Sacrifice who is both Son of God and Son of Man, or Immanuel.
  18. 18.How do we find liberty? ; .

Notes

The bondage is sin or transgression of the law. Christ alone can save the sinner, and make him righteous or obedient. When a man assumes by his own works to make himself righteous, he is taking upon himself an obligation that he can never bear. The more one seeks to justify himself by his own acts, the more he becomes entangled in the yoke of bondage, or sin. Any theory or system which seeks righteousness by works is but a yoke of bondage.
