Lesson 4 · October 23, 1886

The Good Samaritan

PDFShare
  1. 1.Who was it that on a certain occasion stood up and tempted Jesus? Luke 10:25.
  2. 2.Who were called lawyers in those days?
  3. 3.What question did the lawyer ask? Same verse.
  4. 4.What way did Jesus take to answer him?—He asked him a question in return.
  5. 5.Repeat the question. Verse 26.
  6. 6.How did the lawyer answer the Saviour? Verse 27.
  7. 7.What encouragement did Jesus give him? Verse 28.
  8. 8.What spirit did the lawyer manifest? Verse 29.
  9. 9.What did he say? Same verse.
  10. 10.How did Jesus answer the question?—By giving a parable.
  11. 11.In this parable, what befell the man who was traveling from Jerusalem to Jericho? Verse 30.
  12. 12.How did the thieves treat him? Same verse.
  13. 13.Who first chanced to come that way? Verse 31.
  14. 14.How did the priest treat the suffering traveler? Same verse.
  15. 15.What course was taken by a Levite who happened that way? Verse 32.
  16. 16.Give a brief account of the offices and duties of the priests.
  17. 17.What were the chief duties of the Levites?
  18. 18.What would be expected of men in such holy offices?
  19. 19.What other traveler was journeying from Jerusalem to Jericho that day? Verse 33.
  20. 20.How was he affected at the wounded sufferer? Same verse.
  21. 21.How did he manifest his compassion? Verse 34.
  22. 22.How much did the Samaritan pay the host when he went away the next morning? Verse 35.
  23. 23.How did the good Samaritan show that his kindness was a settled principle rather than a sudden impulse? Last part of the same verse.
  24. 24.When Jesus had finished the parable, what question did he ask the lawyer? Verse 36.
  25. 25.What answer did the lawyer give? Verse 37.
  26. 26.What instruction did Jesus then give him? Same verse.
  27. 27.What relation existed between the Jews and the Samaritans?—They were bitter enemies.
  28. 28.How then could this Samaritan regard the unfortunate Jew as his neighbor, and treat him so kindly?
  29. 29.What beautiful lesson may we learn from this parable?
  30. 30.How does the Saviour impart the same lesson by direct precept? Matt. 5:43-47.

Notes

A certain lawyer.—One who professed to be well skilled in the laws of Moses, and whose business it was to explain them.—Barnes.

A priest ... a Levite.—The Levites, as well as the priests, were of the tribe of Levi, and were set apart to the duties of religion. The peculiar duty of the priest was to offer sacrifice at the temple, to present incense, prayers, the morning and evening services of the temple, etc. The office or duty of the Levites was to render assistance to the priests in their services. In the journey of the Israelites through the wilderness, it was their duty to transport the various parts of the tabernacle, and the various sacred utensils. It was their duty to see that the tabernacle and the temple were kept clean: to prepare supplies for the sanctuary, such as oil, incense, wine, etc. They had the care of the sacred revenues, and, after the time of David, they conducted the sacred music in the temple service.—Ibid.
