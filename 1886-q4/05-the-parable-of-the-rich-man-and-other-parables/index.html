Lesson 5 · October 30, 1886

The Parable of the Rich Man, and Other Parables

PDFShare
  1. 1.On a certain occasion what request was presented to Jesus by one of the company who had gathered around him? Luke 12:13.
  2. 2.How did Jesus answer him? Verse 14.
  3. 3.What warning did he then give to all? Verse 15.
  4. 4.Why should not men be too anxious to gain wealth? Same verse.
  5. 5.Who did Jesus then take as a subject of a parable? Verse 16.
  6. 6.What perplexed this man? Verse 17.
  7. 7.What did he decide to do? Verse 18.
  8. 8.How did he congratulate himself upon his wealth and prosperity? Verse 19.
  9. 9.Upon what was he evidently counting?—Upon many years to come.
  10. 10.How was he disappointed? Verse 20.
  11. 11.What question does God ask the foolish rich man? Same verse.
  12. 12.What conclusion does Jesus draw from the parable? Verse 21.
  13. 13.How may such a man become rich toward God? Matt. 19:21.
  14. 14.What special instruction did Jesus give his disciples? Luke 12:22.
  15. 15.Why is it not best to take too much thought for food and clothing? Verse 23.
  16. 16.How does Jesus illustrate the care which God has over his children? Verse 24.
  17. 17.What question did Jesus ask in order to show how vain is all our thought and anxiety without the help and providence of God? Verse 25.
  18. 18.How did he set home this lesson? Verse 26.
  19. 19.What other parable did he give for the purpose of inspiring implicit trust in God? Verse 27.
  20. 20.By what question did he deepen the impression of the truth he had taught? Verse 28.
  21. 21.What direct admonition did he then give them? Verse 29.
  22. 22.Why is it entirely safe to follow this precept? Verse 30.
  23. 23.What should be made the chief object in life? Verse 31.
  24. 24.What assurance with reference to temporal wants is given to those who follow this precept? Same verse.
  25. 25.Why should the children of God dismiss all fear? Verse 32.
  26. 26.How may this happy state be secured? 1 Jno. 4:18.
  27. 27.Of what is love the fruit? Gal. 5:22.
  28. 28.How is the spirit of God to be secured? Luke 11:13.
