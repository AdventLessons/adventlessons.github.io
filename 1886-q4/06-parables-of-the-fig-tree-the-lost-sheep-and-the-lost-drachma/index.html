Lesson 6 · November 6, 1886

Parables of the Fig-Tree, the Lost Sheep, and the Lost Drachma

PDFShare
  1. 1.What cruel act of punishment was related to Jesus? Luke 13:1.
  2. 2.What question did Jesus then ask? Verse 2.
  3. 3.Repeat his answer to his own question. Verse 3.
  4. 4.To what other sad instance did Jesus refer? Verse 4.
  5. 5.What similar question did he ask concerning this? Same verse.
  6. 6.How did he answer the question? Verse 5.
  7. 7.In what other way did our Saviour teach the same lesson?—By the use of a parable.
  8. 8.In this parable, by what object is the unrepentant sinner represented? Verse 6.
  9. 9.Who is represented by the dresser of the vineyard?
  10. 10.What is the owner of the vineyard represented as saying to him? Verse 7.
  11. 11.Who is the owner of the vineyard?
  12. 12.In what way does the parable illustrate the labors and pleadings of Christ and his followers for the salvation of sinners? Verse 8.
  13. 13.How does the parable illustrate the fate of those who persist in sin, and remain unfruitful? Verse 9.
  14. 14.What provoked the jealousy and ill-will of the Pharisees and the scribes? Luke 15:1.
  15. 15.What complaint did these men make? Verse 2.
  16. 16.By what two parables did Jesus show the inconsistency of their murmuring?—By the parables of the lost sheep and the lost piece of money.
  17. 17.What does the shepherd do when a sheep has strayed from the flock? Verse 4.
  18. 18.What is this meant to illustrate?
  19. 19.What did Jesus, at another time, say about his mission to earth? Luke 5:31, 32.
  20. 20.What called forth these remarks? Verse 30.
  21. 21.What does the shepherd do when he has found the lost sheep? Verse 5.
  22. 22.How does he manifest his joy on arriving at home? Verse 6.
  23. 23.What does this illustrate? Repeat verse 7.
  24. 24.Relate the parable of the lost piece of money. Verses 8 and 9.
  25. 25.What parallel did Jesus draw? Repeat verse 10.
  26. 26.What error do you think Jesus meant to correct when he gave the parable of the fig-tree?
  27. 27.What practical lesson may we draw from the last two parables given in this lesson?

Notes

Note.—The lesson-writer takes great pains not to make the lessons too hard; and if any should find them too short, it might be a good thing to spend some time in looking up parallel texts.
