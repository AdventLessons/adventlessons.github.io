Lesson 11 · December 11, 1886

The Laborers in the Vineyard

PDFShare
  1. 1.How did our Lord introduce the parable of the laborers? Matt. 20:1.
  2. 2.How much did the householder agree to pay the laborers that began early in the morning? Verse 2.
  3. 3.At what time did he hire other laborers? Verse 3.
  4. 4.What agreement did he make with them? Ver. 4.
  5. 5.When, and with what understanding, did he hire still others? Verse 5.
  6. 6.What did he find, when he went out at the eleventh hour? Verse 6.
  7. 7.What did he say to them? Same verse.
  8. 8.What excuse did the men make for being idle? Verse 7.
  9. 9.What did the master then say to them? Same verse.
  10. 10.What order was given at evening? Verse 8.
  11. 11.How much was paid to those that were hired at the eleventh hour? Verse 9.
  12. 12.What did the others expect, when they saw how generous the master was with their fellow-laborers? Verse 10.
  13. 13.How were they disappointed? Same verse.
  14. 14.What complaint did they make? Verses 11, 12.
  15. 15.How did the master justify himself to one of these complainers? Verse 13.
  16. 16.What did he tell the laborer to do? Verse 14.
  17. 17.What questions did he ask the dissatisfied man? Verse 15.
  18. 18.When Jesus had finished the parable, what principle did he lay down? Verse 16.
  19. 19.What seems to have called out the parable of the laborers?—It was probably the conversation with the rich young man who could not give up his worldly possessions for the sake of being saved.
  20. 20.What question did this young man ask the Saviour? Matt. 19:16.
  21. 21.What did Jesus tell him he must do? Verse 17.
  22. 22.What inquiry did the young man then make? Verse 18.
  23. 23.Which of the ten commandments did our Lord then mention? Verses 18, 19.
  24. 24.What reply did the young man then make? Verse 20.
  25. 25.What did Jesus tell him was still necessary for him to do in order to have treasure in heaven? Verse 21.
  26. 26.How did the young man receive this instruction? Verse 22.
  27. 27.What remark did Jesus then make? Verse 23.
  28. 28.What anxiety did Peter manifest? Verse 27.
  29. 29.What assurance did the Saviour give him? Verses 28, 29.
  30. 30.How did he then illustrate the saying, "Many that are first shall be last, and the last shall be first"? Chapter 20:1-16.
  31. 31.What makes labor valuable in the sight of God?—Not the time and strength it requires, but the love and faith that actuate it.

Notes

"The householder thus made the first last, and the last first, because the first had been working for hire, while the others had simply trusted his promise. He who works in my kingdom for the sake of a reward hereafter, may do his work well, but he honors me less than others who trust in me, without thinking of future gain. The spirit in which you labor for me gives your service its value. He who is called late in life, and serves me unselfishly, will stand higher at the great day than he who has served me longer, but with a less noble motive. Many are called to join my kingdom and work in it, but few show themselves by their spirit and zeal especially worthy of honor. If the first find themselves last, it will depend on themselves, for though no one can claim reward as his due in the kingdom of God, yet I give it, of favor, to those first who serve me most purely. He, I repeat, who works most devotedly, without thought of reward, will be first, though, perhaps, last to be called: he will be chosen to honor, while others less zealous and loving, though earlier called, will remain undistinguished."—Paraphrase, by Dr. Cunningham Geikie.
