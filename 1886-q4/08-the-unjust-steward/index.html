Lesson 8 · November 20, 1886

The Unjust Steward

PDFShare
  1. 1.What accusation was brought against the steward of a certain rich man? Luke 16:1.
  2. 2.After calling the steward to him, what question did the master ask him? Verse 2.
  3. 3.What did he ask him to do? Same verse.
  4. 4.What decision did the master make?
  5. 5.What inquiry did the steward then put to himself? Verse 3.
  6. 6.By what difficulties was he beset? Same verse.
  7. 7.What good did he hope to secure by the course he resolved to pursue? Verse 4.
  8. 8.What question did he ask each of his lord's debtors, as he called them to him one by one? Verses 5, 7.
  9. 9.What answer did the first one make? Verse 6.
  10. 10.What did the unjust steward tell him to do?
  11. 11.What did the second one say? Verse 7.
  12. 12.What was he told to do? Same verse.
  13. 13.How was the steward's dishonest artifice regarded by his master? Verse 8.
  14. 14.What remark is made concerning the worldly wisdom often manifested by ungodly men? Same verse.
  15. 15.What counsel is then given? Verse 9.
  16. 16.What is said about faithfulness? Verse 10.
  17. 17.What about those who are unjust in small matters? Same verse.
  18. 18.What important question is then asked? Verse 11.
  19. 19.What is here meant by "the true riches"?—Probably the riches of grace, and the enjoyments of the world to come.
  20. 20.What is meant by "the unrighteous mammon"?—The wealth of this world.
  21. 21.How can Christians be faithful with this kind of wealth?—By using it as God has commanded.
  22. 22.What has he said with reference to it? Prov. 3:9; Luke 6:38; 12:33.
  23. 23.What encouragement is given to those who make such a good use of worldly means? Prov. 11:25; 19:17; 28:27.
  24. 24.How is true generosity illustrated? Matt. 25:35-40.
  25. 25.How does our Lord show the folly of having the heart set upon worldly riches, and trying to serve God at the same time? Luke 16:13.
  26. 26.How is it that the "children of this world are in their generation wiser than the children of light"?—Those who seek worldly wealth and honor devote all their energies and the best of their ability to the purpose they have in view; while those who seek heavenly riches often put forth weak or divided efforts.
  27. 27.How did the Pharisees treat the doctrine of liberality which Jesus was trying to impress? Verse 14.
  28. 28.Why did they deride him? Same verse.
  29. 29.How did Jesus reprove them? First part of verse 15.
  30. 30.How does God's estimation of things often differ from the judgment of men? Last part of verse 15.
  31. 31.How were men showing their appreciation of the new light which was given them by the preaching of John the Baptist and our Lord? Verse 16.
  32. 32.How did Jesus set forth the importance and steadfastness of God's law? Verse 17.
  33. 33.How did he intimate that the Jews had been careless in the observance of that law, even while claiming to guard it so jealously? Verse 18.

Notes

What called out the parable.—Among the great multitudes who had thronged after him, the publicans of the district were especially noticeable. Many of them were, doubtless, in a good position in life, and some even rich, but all were exposed to peculiar temptations in their hated calling. Not a few seem to have listened earnestly to the first teacher who had ever treated them as men with souls to save, and it was of the greatest importance to them that they should have wise and true principles for their future guidance... The following parable seems to have been delivered specially to them, as part of an address when they had gathered in more than usual numbers.—Dr. Geikie.
