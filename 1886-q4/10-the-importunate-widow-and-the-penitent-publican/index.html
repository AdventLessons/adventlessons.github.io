Lesson 10 · December 4, 1886

The Importunate Widow and the Penitent Publican

PDFShare
  1. 1.To what end, or for what purpose, did Jesus give the parable of the importunate widow? Luke 18:1.
  2. 2.Who were the leading characters in the parable? Verses 2, 3.
  3. 3.Describe the judge. Verse 2.
  4. 4.What request did the widow make? Verse 3.
  5. 5.How did the judge at first regard her petition? Verse 4.
  6. 6.What good motives ought to have influenced him? Same verse.
  7. 7.Did these things have any power to move him? Same verse.
  8. 8.Why did he finally conclude to take up her cause? Verse 5.
  9. 9.What admonition does Jesus give? Verse 6.
  10. 10.Did the admonition apply alone to those who were present when it was spoken, or does it apply to us as well?
  11. 11.By what question did Jesus try to set home the meaning of the parable? Verse 7.
  12. 12.Is there any promise here for those who do not cry to God?
  13. 13.What seems to be the teaching of the parable?—That if a wicked man, who cared not to please God, and who had no sympathy for human suffering, would yield to the persistent supplications of a poor woman, it is certain that God, who tenderly loves all his creatures, will heed the prayers of his saints.
  14. 14.What question seems to imply that in the last days there will be but few who will still have faith that the Lord Jesus is coming, according to his promise, to deliver his people? Verse 8.
  15. 15.For whose special benefit did the Lord give the parable of the Pharisee and the publican? Verse 9.
  16. 16.How are these two characters introduced? Verse 10.
  17. 17.For what did the Pharisee thank God? Verse 11.
  18. 18.Of what did he boast? Verse 12.
  19. 19.How did the publican show by his behavior that he was both penitent and humble? Verse 13.
  20. 20.Repeat the words of his prayer. Same verse.
  21. 21.What did Jesus say about the way in which God looked upon the worship of these two men? First part of verse 14.
  22. 22.What general principle did our Lord then lay down? Last part of verse 14.
  23. 23.Whom did the disciples rebuke? Verse 15.
  24. 24.What did Jesus do to show the disciples how they had erred in judgment? Verse 16.
  25. 25.How did he still further exalt the grace of humility? Verse 17.
  26. 26.What is the first part of this lesson meant to inspire?—Faith in prayer.
  27. 27.What is the last part of the lesson meant to teach?—That God loves a humble, penitent heart.

Notes

Shall not God avenge his own elect?—A question used in this way is called a figure of interrogation. It is not asked for the purpose of obtaining an answer, but to make a stronger impression of a truth than could be made by a direct statement. It is put under such circumstances as to admit of but one answer, and that answer must declare the truth which the speaker wishes to impress. In this way, the hearer, in answering the question, is compelled to testify to the fact which he is called upon to admit. If this unfeeling judge, who feared not God, nor regarded man, could in any way be induced to administer justice in favor of the widow, certainly God, a being of infinite love, will without unnecessary delay, give help and protection to his own elect, whom he regards with such tender compassion. Nevertheless, to remove all doubt, and perhaps for the purpose of adding another thought, our Lord proceeds to answer the question himself.
