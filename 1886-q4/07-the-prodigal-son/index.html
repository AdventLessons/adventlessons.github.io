Lesson 7 · November 13, 1886

The Prodigal Son

PDFShare
  1. 1.What two parables are given in the first part of Luke 15, to show how great God's compassion is toward those who have strayed from the path of duty? Luke 15:1-10.
  2. 2.Relate the first of these parables.
  3. 3.Relate the parable about the lost piece of silver.
  4. 4.What parable did Jesus then give to show how God pities and loves the truly penitent, no matter how great their sins may have been?—That of the prodigal son.
  5. 5.How is this parable introduced? Luke 15:11.
  6. 6.What did the father do for these two sons? Last part of verse 12.
  7. 7.What induced him to take this step? First part of verse 12.
  8. 8.What course was taken by the younger son? Verse 13.
  9. 9.What misfortune overtook him in that strange land? Verse 14.
  10. 10.How did he seek relief? Verse 15.
  11. 11.What employment did the citizen give him? Same verse.
  12. 12.What would the suffering young man have been glad to do? Verse 16.
  13. 13.What unconcern was manifested toward him? Last part of verse 16.
  14. 14.When he had come to himself, what were his reflections? Verse 17.
  15. 15.What is probably meant by his coming to himself?—That affliction brought him to look upon things in their true light.
  16. 16.What did he determine to do? First part of verse 18.
  17. 17.How did he propose to humble himself? Verses 18, 19.
  18. 18.How was the forlorn wanderer received by his father? Verse 20.
  19. 19.Repeat the son's confession. Verse 21.
  20. 20.How did the father honor him? Verse 22.
  21. 21.What other token of joy did the happy parent manifest? Verse 23.
  22. 22.How did his happiness find expression in words? Verse 24.
  23. 23.Where was the elder son when his brother came home? Verse 25.
  24. 24.What surprised him as he came near the home? Same verse.
  25. 25.How did he try to ascertain the cause of these things? Verse 26.
  26. 26.What explanation was made by the servant? Verse 27.
  27. 27.How did this elder son receive the news of his brother's return? Verse 28.
  28. 28.What complaint did he make when his father entreated him to come in? Verses 29, 30.
  29. 29.How was he assured of his father's love and appreciation? Verse 31.
  30. 30.How did the father justify the unusual tokens of rejoicing by which he was celebrating the prodigal's return? Verse 32.
