Lesson 13 · December 25, 1886

The Two Sons

PDFShare
  1. 1.Where did Jesus spend the last Sabbath before his crucifixion? John 12:1, 2.
  2. 2.What occurred after supper? Verse 3.
  3. 3.What attracted great numbers of people when they learned that Jesus was at Bethany? Verse 9.
  4. 4.What troubled the chief priests? Verse 11.
  5. 5.How did they propose to put an end to this annoyance? Verse 10.
  6. 6.What remarkable event occurred the next day? Matt. 21:1-11.
  7. 7.What was among the chief causes for this demonstration by the people? John 12:17, 18.
  8. 8.When the Pharisees saw such honor paid to Jesus, what did they say among themselves? Verse 19.
  9. 9.On the next morning (Monday), as Jesus was again on his way to the temple, what caused him to curse a fig tree? Matt. 21:18, 19.
  10. 10.What was the effect of this curse?
  11. 11.What bold action did Jesus take in the temple the same day? Mark 11:15-17.
  12. 12.What acts of mercy did he perform? Matt. 21:14.
  13. 13.How did these things affect the chief priests and scribes? Verse 15.
  14. 14.What did they say to Jesus? Verse 16.
  15. 15.How did he answer them? Same verse.
  16. 16.How might the cursing of the fig tree illustrate the fate of these hypocritical priests and rulers?—The curse of God was about to fall upon them, because they were unfaithful, and did not bear the fruits of good works.
  17. 17.Where did Jesus lodge at night? Matt. 21:17.
  18. 18.What did the disciples notice on Tuesday morning, as they were returning to the temple? Mark 11:20.
  19. 19.What lesson of faith did Jesus draw from this? Verse 24.
  20. 20.What important condition did he lay down for the prayer of faith? Verse 25.
  21. 21.As Jesus came into the temple, and began to teach, how was his authority questioned? Matt. 21:23.
  22. 22.What proposition did he make? Verse 24.
  23. 23.What question did he then ask? Verse 25.
  24. 24.How did the priests then reason among themselves? Verses 25, 26.
  25. 25.How did the conversation end? Verse 27.
  26. 26.How did Jesus then illustrate the condition of these conceited priests?—By the parable of the two sons.
  27. 27.Relate the parable. Matt. 21:28-30.
  28. 28.When Jesus had related the parable, what question did he ask? Verse 31.
  29. 29.How did they answer him? Same verse.
  30. 30.What startling announcement did Jesus then make to them?
  31. 31.What reasons did he give for making such a statement? Verse 32.
  32. 32.Which of the sons represented the priests to whom Jesus was talking?
