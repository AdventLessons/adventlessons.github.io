Lesson 3 · October 16, 1886

The Stray Sheep, and Other Parables

PDFShare
  1. 1.By what parable did our Lord represent the growth of grace in the heart? Matt. 13:31.
  2. 2.How does he describe the growth of the plant here called mustard?
  3. 3.What may be said of this plant as grown in the East?—It attains in Palestine a height of ten feet, and the linnets and finches settle on it in great flocks for the sake of its seeds.
  4. 4.What other parable seems to be given to illustrate the same thing as that of the mustard? Matt. 13:33.
  5. 5.How does our Lord illustrate the development of Christian character in a parable recorded in St. Mark? Mark 4:26-29.
  6. 6.What is here given again as the first step in the work? Verse 26.
  7. 7.What is said of our inability to trace spiritual growth? Verse 27.
  8. 8.How are its successive stages illustrated? Verse 28.
  9. 9.What follows as soon as the fruit reaches perfect maturity? Verse 29.
  10. 10.What warning does Jesus give against despising the little ones of his kingdom? Matt. 18:10.
  11. 11.What does Christ say of his mission upon earth? Verse 11.
  12. 12.By what parable does he illustrate his love and care for those who stray from the path of duty? Verses 12-14.
  13. 13.In giving this parable, what question does he ask concerning the course of a shepherd toward a sheep that has gone astray? Verse 12.
  14. 14.What are a shepherd's feelings when he has recovered a lost sheep? Verse 13.
  15. 15.What conclusion is then drawn? Verse 14.
  16. 16.In harmony with the principle of love and forbearance taught in this parable, what course should one Christian take toward another that has injured him? Verse 15.
  17. 17.What course must be pursued when the offender will not be thus conciliated? Verse 16.
  18. 18.What must be done if this means fails? Verse 17.
  19. 19.What question did Peter ask? Verse 21.
  20. 20.How did Jesus answer the question? Verse 22.
  21. 21.By what further parable did Jesus illustrate the spirit of love and forbearance which prevails in the kingdom of heaven? Verse 23.
  22. 22.Who was brought to the king as he was reckoning with his servants? Verse 24.
  23. 23.What command did the king give with regard to him? Verse 25.
  24. 24.How did the servant humble himself before his master? Verse 26.
  25. 25.How was the king affected by the poor man's condition? Verse 27.
  26. 26.What kindness did the king show him? Same verse.
  27. 27.Describe the course pursued by this servant toward one of his fellow-servants who owed him only a hundred pence. Verses 28-30.
  28. 28.How did the other servants feel when they saw such cruelty practiced by one who had been so kindly treated by the king? Verse 31.
  29. 29.What did they do? Same verse.
  30. 30.When the king had called the wicked servant to him, what did he say to him? Verses 32, 33.
  31. 31.What just retribution was inflicted upon this wicked servant? Verse 34.
  32. 32.How did Jesus apply this parable? Verse 35.
