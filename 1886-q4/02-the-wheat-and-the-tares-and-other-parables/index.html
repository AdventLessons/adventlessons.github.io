Lesson 2 · October 9, 1886

The Wheat and the Tares, and Other Parables

PDFShare
  1. 1.To what did our Lord liken the kingdom of heaven in Matt. 13:24?
  2. 2.What took place while men slept? Verse 25.
  3. 3.What was observed when the blade had sprung up and brought forth fruit? Verse 26.
  4. 4.What did the servants of the householder say to him? Verse 27.
  5. 5.How did he answer them? Verse 28.
  6. 6.What inquiry did the servants then make? Same verse.
  7. 7.Why did the master object to having the tares plucked out from among the wheat? Verse 29.
  8. 8.How long were the wheat and the tares to be left to grow together? Verse 30.
  9. 9.What was to be done when the grain should be harvested? Same verse.
  10. 10.What did the disciples say to Jesus when the multitude had been sent away, and he had gone into a house? Verse 36.
  11. 11.According to the Saviour's explanation, who sows the good seed? Verse 37.
  12. 12.What is the field? Verse 38.
  13. 13.What does the good seed represent?
  14. 14.What did the tares represent?
  15. 15.Who sows the bad seed? Verse 39.
  16. 16.When does the harvest come?
  17. 17.Who are the reapers?
  18. 18.What comparison does the Saviour make? Verse 40.
  19. 19.On what errand will the Son of man send his angels when the end of the world shall come? Verse 41.
  20. 20.What shall be done with these offenders? Verse 42.
  21. 21.What will be the condition of the righteous? Verse 43.
  22. 22.What is clearly taught in this parable?—That neither the righteous nor the wicked will receive their reward until the end of the world.
  23. 23.Repeat the parable concerning the hidden treasure. Verse 44.
  24. 24.What lesson does this parable seem to teach?—That the kingdom of heaven is more to be valued than all things else, and that whoever would obtain it must be willing to give all things for it.
  25. 25.What other parable teaches the same lesson? Verses 45, 46.
  26. 26.Relate the parable of the drag net. Verses 47, 48.
  27. 27.Give the Saviour's explanation of this parable. Verses 49, 50.
