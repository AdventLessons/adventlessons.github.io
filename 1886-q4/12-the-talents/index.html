Lesson 12 · December 18, 1886

The Talents

PDFShare
  1. 1.How did some of the followers of Jesus feel as he was on his last journey to Jerusalem? Mark 10:32.
  2. 2.What cause had they for fear? Luke 13:31.
  3. 3.When Jesus had taken the twelve apart, what did he tell them? Mark 10:32, last part of the verse.
  4. 4.Unto whom did he say he should be delivered when he arrived at Jerusalem? Verse 33.
  5. 5.How would they treat him? Same verse.
  6. 6.What did he say the Gentiles would do when the Jews had delivered him into their hands? Verse 34.
  7. 7.Did the disciples comprehend what Jesus told them? Luke 18:34.
  8. 8.Why could they not understand such plain statements? Same verse.
  9. 9.What did they seem to be expecting that Jesus would do when he arrived at Jerusalem?—They evidently thought he was about to set up his kingdom upon the earth.
  10. 10.In view of this, what request was made by the sons of Zebedee? Mark 10:35-37.
  11. 11.Because of these false notions, what did Jesus do? Luke 19:11.
  12. 12.What did he say the kingdom of heaven was like? Matt. 25:14.
  13. 13.How did the nobleman dispose of his goods before starting on his journey? Same verse.
  14. 14.How did he distribute them? Verse 15.
  15. 15.Why did he thus give unequal portions to different ones? Same verse.
  16. 16.What course was pursued by the servant who received five talents? Verse 16.
  17. 17.What by the one who received two talents? Verse 17.
  18. 18.What did the servant do who received but one talent? Verse 18.
  19. 19.After a long time what did the lord of these servants do? Verse 19.
  20. 20.What report was made by the man who had received the five talents? Verse 20.
  21. 21.What did the man report who had received the two talents? Verse 22.
  22. 22.What did their lord say to each of them? Verses 21, 23.
  23. 23.When the servant to whom but one talent was intrusted was called up, what preamble did he make? Verse 24.
  24. 24.What account did he finally give of his lord's money? Verse 25.
  25. 25.How did his lord condemn him from his own words? Verses 26, 27.
  26. 26.What command was given concerning the slothful servant? Verse 28.
  27. 27.What general principle did our Lord then lay down? Verse 29.
  28. 28.To what was the unprofitable servant doomed? Verse 30.
  29. 29.What are the talents which the Lord of the earth has given to his servants?—Talents of health, strength, intellect, wealth, skill, and everything else by which a man may benefit others.
  30. 30.How may these talents be improved upon to divine acceptance?—By using them to the glory of God and the good of mankind.
  31. 31.What special incentive should prompt all men to faithfulness at the present time?—The Lord of the earth is about to return and reckon with his servants.
