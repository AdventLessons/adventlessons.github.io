Lesson 3 · July 15, 1944

A King's Pride Humbled; the Handwriting on the Wall

PDFShare

Lesson Scripture: Daniel 4; 5.

Memory verse

, last part.

Study Help: "Prophets and Kings," pages 514-538.

The Lesson

A King's Pride Humbled

  1. 1.With what acknowledgment of God's wisdom and power does Nebuchadnezzar introduce the experience whereby he was humbled by the Lord? Dan. 4:1-3.
  2. 2.What was Nebuchadnezzar's state when God spoke to him in a dream? What effect did the dream have upon him? For whom did he call? Verses 4-7.
  3. 3.Who was finally called to interpret the dream? What name had the king given Daniel? What did the king acknowledge concerning Daniel? Verses 8, 9.
  4. 4.What had the king seen in his dream? Verses 10-12.
  5. 5.When the holy Watcher came down from heaven, what did He command? What part of the tree was to be left? What indicates that the subject of the dream was a man? What change was to come to the individual and for how long? Verses 13-16.
  6. 6.What importance to himself and his subjects did Nebuchadnezzar recognize in the dream? Why did the king call upon Daniel for help? Verses 17, 18.
  7. 7.How was Daniel affected by the dream? With what words did the king encourage Daniel to explain the dream? What answer did Daniel give? Verses 19-23.

Notes

"To Daniel the meaning of the dream was plain, and its significance startled him. . . . The prophet realized that upon him God had laid the solemn duty of revealing to Nebuchadnezzar the judgment that was about to fall upon him because of his pride and arrogance. Daniel must interpret the dream in language the king could understand; and although its dreadful import had made him hesitate in dumb amazement, yet he must state the truth, whatever the consequences to himself."—Prophets and Kings, page 517.

  1. 8.What was the explanation of the dream? Why had God given the dream to the king? Verses 24-26.
  2. 9.What counsel did Daniel give the king? How long afterward was the king, in his pride, found boasting? What did the voice from heaven say? What immediate change came to Nebuchadnezzar? Verses 27-33.
  3. 10.At the end of the period what experience came to the king? How did he express his regard for the God of heaven? Verses 34-37.

Notes

The Jewish historian Josephus tells the story of Nebuchadnezzar's dream and subsequent humiliation, using the expression "seven years," for the "seven times" of verses 16 and 25 of Daniel 4. Thus is confirmed the interpretation of the prophetic period of a "time and times and the dividing of time," as used in chapter 7:25, where a "time" is used to symbolize a literal year.

The Handwriting on the Wall

  1. 11.In the days of Belshazzar, the last king of Babylon, what great feast did he make? Who were invited? While the revelers drank and desecrated the vessels of the temple, whom did they praise? Dan. 5:1-4.

Notes

Following Nebuchadnezzar, Evilmerodach his son reigned for a short time. Finally Nabonidus, the son-in-law of Nebuchadnezzar, took the throne. Belshazzar, the son of Nabonidus, later reigned conjointly with his father.

  1. 12.What mysterious vision suddenly caught the eye of the king? How deeply was he affected? For whom did he call? What reward did he offer his wise men? Verses 5-8.
  2. 13.What effect did the failure of the wise men have on the king and his lords? What counsel did the queen give to the king? Verses 9-12.

Daniel Called to Interpret

  1. 14.When Daniel was brought in, what acknowledgments did Belshazzar make and what promises of reward? Verses 13-16.
  2. 15.What answer did Daniel give the king? Verse 17.
  3. 16.As Daniel recalled the experiences of the past, what did he say Belshazzar had not done? What did he say the king had done that was wrong? Verses 18-23.
  4. 17.What words had been written on the wall? What did each mean? Verses 24-28.
  5. 18.How fully was the king's promise of preferment carried out? Verse 29.
  6. 19.How swiftly did retribution come to Belshazzar? Who took the kingdom? Verses 30, 31.
