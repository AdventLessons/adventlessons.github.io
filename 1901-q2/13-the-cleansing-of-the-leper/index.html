Lesson 13 · June 29, 1901

The Cleansing of the Leper

PDFShare
  1. 1.Give a brief description of the leprosy. What was done with the leper? Lev. 13:44, 45; "Desire of Ages," pp. 262, 263.
  2. 2.Give a brief description of the sinner. Isa. 1:5, 6. What, then, is represented by leprosy?
  3. 3.Was there human cure for it? 2 Kings 5:5-7.
  4. 4.Is there any human cure for sin? Jer. 2:22; 13:23.
  5. 5.What was the divine cure for leprosy? 2 Kings 5:10, 13, 14; Luke 5:12, 13.
  6. 6.What provision has been made for the cure of sinners? Zech. 13:1; Isa. 1:16, 18.
  7. 7.When Christ cleansed the leper, what did He tell him to do? Luke 5:14.
  8. 8.What was the law commanded by Moses? Lev. 14:1-7.
  9. 9.When the leper was to be cleansed, what was done with him? Where did the priest meet him? Lev. 14:2, 3.
  10. 10.Who was represented by the priest? Where, then, does Christ go to find the sinner? Where must we go? Heb. 13:12, 13. Note 1.
  11. 11.What did the leper bring for offerings? Lev. 14:10, 11.
  12. 12.State the order in which they were offered. Verses 12-20, 53. Note 2.
  13. 13.How many offerings do we have to bring to accomplish far more than was done for the leper? Heb. 10:10-14.

Notes

1. The leper was brought by some one to the priest, so we are to bring sinners to Christ. Christ suffered outside of Jerusalem, that He might save the world, so we are to go outside the church to bring sinners to Him, who sees them when they are "yet a great way off" and runs to meet them.

2. The blood being touched upon the right ear would indicate that our hearing should be consecrated to God. We must know no voice but His. John 10:4, 5. It being put upon the thumb and toe would indicate that the service of our hands and feet must be only for Him. Ps. 119:101. The oil was placed upon the blood. Wherever there is cleansing and consecration there is also the presence of the Holy Spirit. Notice that the same offerings and consecration were required of the leper that God required of the high priest. Exodus 29. So the humblest child of God must be as devoted and consecrated as was Christ. John 17:19.
