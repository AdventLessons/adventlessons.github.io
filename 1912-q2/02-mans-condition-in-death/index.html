Lesson 2 · April 13, 1912

Man's Condition in Death

PDFShare

Lesson Helps: "Christ's Object Lessons," pages 260-267; Sabbath School Worker.

Questions

  1. 1.What passed upon all men as a result of sin? Rom. 5:12.
  2. 2.From what is the man alienated who is separated from Christ? Eph. 4:17, 18.
  3. 3.What is death called? John 11:11-13; Matt. 9:24.
  4. 4.During the time of sleep where does man wait? Job 17:13.
  5. 5.How long must he wait in the grave? Job 14:12. Compare John 5:28, 29.
  6. 6.During this time what is said of his knowledge concerning the affairs of earth? Job 14:20, 21.
  7. 7.How is his utter unconsciousness described? Ps. 146:4; Eccl. 9:5, 6.

CHRIST'S DEATH

  1. 8.What testimony is borne concerning the death of Christ? 1 Cor. 15:3; Isa. 53:5, 8; John 19:28-30.
  2. 9.What deep significance is attached to His death? Isa. 53:12.
  3. 10.From what was Jesus exempt? Ps. 16:10; Acts 2:30, 31.
  4. 11.What blessed hope for us was involved in Jesus' coming forth from the tomb? 1 Cor. 15:20-23.
  5. 12.When Jesus arose from the tomb what did He bring with Him? Rev. 1:17, 18.
  6. 13.What was brought to light through the gospel? 2 Tim. 1:9, 10. Note 1.
  7. 14.What hope therefore lightens the darkness of the tomb? 1 Thess. 4:13, 14.
  8. 15.What will satisfy the patriarch David? Ps. 17:15. What assurance have we that he has not ascended to heaven? Acts 2:29, 34.
  9. 16.To what time did followers of the Lord look forward to meet their loved ones fallen in death? John 11:23, 24.
  10. 17.How did Isaiah speak of this same time? Isa. 26:19.
  11. 18.When did Paul expect to receive his reward? Who besides him would receive their reward at the same time? 2 Tim. 4:6-8.
  12. 19.Whose voice alone can awaken the dead? John 5:28, 29; 1 Thess. 4:16-18. Note 2.

Notes

1. There is a distinction to be made between eternal life and immortality. He who receives Christ by faith thereby lays hold upon "that eternal life, which was with the Father, and was manifested unto us," and so Jesus said, "He that believeth on Me hath everlasting life" (John 6:47); but that which is thus acquired by faith is retained only by faith. After immortality has been put on at the second coming of Christ, the eternity of being is fully assured. This was the purpose in view in the gift of Christ to take upon Himself humanity.

2. "Wherefore comfort one another with these words." That our loved ones are not troubled by the harrowing scenes of this life; that they are unconscious of what is transpiring about us, like one asleep; that to them, if saved, the next moment will be the greeting of their Saviour, never more to be separated from Him,—to believe thus, as the Scriptures so plainly teach, is not a comfortless, cheerless hope.
