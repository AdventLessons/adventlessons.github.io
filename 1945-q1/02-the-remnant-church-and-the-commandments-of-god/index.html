Lesson 2 · January 13, 1945

The Remnant Church and the Commandments of God

PDFShare

Memory verse

Study Helps: "The Great Controversy," pp. 582-592 (new ed., pp. 657-667); "Patriarchs and Prophets," pp. 33-43.

The Lesson

The Law of God's Kingdom

  1. 1.What is the extent of God's government? Ps. 103:19.
  2. 2.Who is our lawgiver? Isa. 33:22, margin.

Notes

The law of God is the expression of His goodness and love, a transcript of His character.

  1. 3.What can be said of the nature of God's law? Ps. 19:7, 8; Rom. 7:12.
  2. 4.Who of heaven's inhabitants are said to be subject to God's law? Ps. 103:20.
  3. 5.What may be expected of the inhabitants of this earth? Eccl. 12:13. See Deut. 6:25.

Sin—Transgression, Unrighteousness, Disobedience

  1. 6.How do the Scriptures define sin? 1 John 3:4.
  2. 7.Since the law of God is righteousness, what can we expect sin to be? 1 John 5:17.
  3. 8.What evidence have we that Satan was the first transgressor of God's law? John 8:44.
  4. 9.What was the result of Adam's disobedience? Rom. 5:12.

Perpetuity of God's Law

  1. 10.What do the Scriptures teach regarding the perpetuity of God's law? Ps. 111:7, 8; Matt. 5:18.
  2. 11.What is the greatest evidence of the divinity, sacredness, perfection, and perpetuity of God's law? Heb. 2:9; 1 Peter 2:22-24.

Notes

"It was because the law was changeless, because man could be saved only through obedience to its precepts, that Jesus was lifted up on the cross. Yet the very means by which Christ established the law, Satan represented as destroying it. Here will come the last conflict of the great controversy between Christ and Satan."—The Desire of Ages, pages 762, 763.

Great Controversy Between Christ and Satan

  1. 12.Since the controversy between Christ and Satan began over the law of God, how may we expect it to close? Rev. 12:17.
  2. 13.Though Adam and ancient Israel failed to obey God's law, what is God expecting of the remnant church? Isa. 58:12; Rev. 14:12.

Notes

"Among earth's inhabitants, scattered in every land, there are those who have not bowed the knee to Baal. Like the stars of heaven, which appear only at night, these faithful ones will shine forth when darkness covers the earth and gross darkness the people. In heathen Africa, in the Catholic lands of Europe and of South America, in China, in India, in the islands of the sea, and in all the dark corners of the earth, God has in reserve a firmament of chosen ones that will yet shine forth amidst the darkness, revealing clearly to an apostate world the transforming power of obedience to His law."—Prophets and Kings, pages 188, 189.

  1. 14.What is the price of disobedience or obedience? Isa. 24:4, 5; Matt. 19:16-19; Rev. 22:14.
  2. 15.What assurance of help and of inestimable reward is offered those who will honor God in these days when the controversy is about to close? Isa. 51:7; Rev. 14:4, 5.

Notes

Read Prophets and Kings, pages 186, 187.
