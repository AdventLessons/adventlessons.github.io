Lesson 1 · January 6, 1945

The Church in All Ages

PDFShare

Memory verse

Study Help: "The Acts of the Apostles," pp. 593-599.

The Lesson

God's Family One in Heaven and Earth

  1. 1.What sacred tie binds together the family in earth and in heaven? Eph. 3:14, 15.
  2. 2.Through whom do we become members of this family? Eph. 2:12, 13, 18, 19.

Notes

"From that scene of heavenly joy, there comes back to us on earth the echo of Christ's own wonderful words, 'I ascend unto My Father, and your Father; and to My God, and your God.' The family of heaven and the family of earth are one. For us our Lord ascended, and for us He lives."—The Desire of Ages, page 835.

  1. 3.What is the church called? 1 Tim. 3:15.
  2. 4.Upon what sure foundation is this edifice built? Isa. 28:16; 1 Cor. 3:9-11.

Notes

"To those who believe, Christ is a sure foundation. Upon this living stone, Jews and Gentiles alike may build. It is broad enough for all, and strong enough to sustain the weight and burden of the whole world."—The Acts of the Apostles, page 175.

  1. 5.Who have been built into this foundation? Eph. 2:19, 20.
  2. 6.How may we share in constructing this building? 1 Cor. 3:12-17.

Notes

"Paul and the other apostles, and all the righteous who have lived since then, have acted their part in the building of the temple. But the structure is not yet complete. We who are living in this age have a work to do, a part to act."—The Acts of the Apostles, page 599.

Relation of Christ to the Church

  1. 7.To what other figure besides that of the temple does Paul frequently refer? Eph. 1:22, 23; Col. 1:18.
  2. 8.How close and sympathetic should be the relationship between members? Rom. 12:4, 5; 1 Cor. 12:12-27.

The Church Before the Christian Era

  1. 9.In the patriarchal age, what covenant did God make with Abraham? Gen. 17:4-8.
  2. 10.How may it be shown that God looked upon the children of Israel in their wilderness experience as His church? Deut. 7:6; Acts 7:38; 1 Cor. 10:1-4.
  3. 11.What proof do we have that the believers met in local groups in the time of David? Ps. 22:22, 25.

Notes

By comparing Psalm 22:22 with the way it is quoted in Hebrews 2:12, we note that the "congregation" in which David desired to praise God was none other than the "church" in his day.

The Church in the Christian Era

  1. 12.Through what parable did Jesus teach that the church of God would be taken away from the Jewish leaders and transferred to Christian leaders? Luke 20:9-18.
  2. 13.In what words did Jesus announce the foundation of the Christian church? Matt. 16:18.

Notes

The rock upon which the church was to be built was His own divine Self. The statement of Peter in verse 16 was that Jesus was "the Christ, the Son of the living God." Compare Deut. 32:4.

  1. 14.During the long years of papal persecution from 538 A. D. to 1798 A. D., where was the true church? Rev. 12:6, 14.

Notes

"For hundreds of years the church of Christ found refuge in seclusion and obscurity. Thus says the prophet: 'The woman fled into the wilderness.' "—The Great Controversy, page 55.

  1. 15.How is the last church identified in the Scriptures? Rev. 12:17; 14:12.
