About Advent Lessons

Advent Lessons is a free collection of Bible studies for anyone who wants to know God better through His Word. Every week, we share a new video study, with Scripture readings, memory verses from the King James Version and questions to help families, friends and Bible study groups learn together. You can also explore lessons from years past, read them online or download them for personal study and class discussion. Our hope is to make the Bible easier to understand, its promises easier to remember and its teachings more meaningful in everyday life. Advent Lessons is a sister project of Advent Hymnals, a free collection of Christian hymns.

Watch every study on our YouTube channel, @adventlessons, and sing with Advent Hymnals.
