Lesson 11 · June 12, 1937

The Marriage Supper of the Lamb

PDFShare

Daily Lesson Study: Place a check mark in the proper space.

Memory verse

Study Helps: "Daniel and the Revelation," pp. 725-728; "Christ's Object Lessons," pp. 219-237 (new ed., pp. 223-241).

The Marriage of the Lamb

  1. 1.After beholding in vision the casting down of Babylon, what did John hear? Where were the "much people"? Rev. 19:1.

Notes

"There is a day just about to burst upon us, when God's mysteries will be seen, and all His ways vindicated; when justice, mercy, and love will be the attributes of His throne. When the earthly warfare is accomplished, and the saints are all gathered home, our first theme will be the song of Moses, the servant of God. The second theme will be the song of the Lamb, the song of grace and redemption. This song will be louder, loftier, and in sublimer strains, echoing and re-echoing through the heavenly courts."—"Testimonies to Ministers and Gospel Workers," p. 433.

  1. 2.What do the redeemed saints say of God's judgments on Babylon? Verse 2.

Notes

Who can so clearly and justly declare God's righteousness, or justice, in the fearful judgments visited on the wicked, as those who have just passed through the anguish, anxiety, and suspense of the "time of Jacob's trouble"?

  1. 3.How does the prophet John describe that glad hallelujah chorus in heaven? What is the occasion of such rejoicing? Verses 6, 7.
  2. 4.How is the apparel of the bride described? Verse 8.
  3. 5.What is said of those who are called to the supper of the Lamb? Verse 9.

Notes

"Soon we heard His lovely voice again, saying, 'Come, My people, you have come out of great tribulation, and done My will; suffered for Me; come in to supper, for I will gird Myself, and serve you.' We shouted, 'Alleluia! glory!' and entered into the city. And I saw a table of pure silver; it was many miles in length, yet our eyes could extend over it. I saw the fruit of the tree of life, the manna, almonds, figs, pomegranates, grapes, and many other kinds of fruit."—"Early Writings," p. 19.

Our Lord's Parable

  1. 6.What parable did Jesus put forth concerning this occasion? Matt. 22:1, 2.

Notes

While this parable had its application to the call given the Jews, later to the Gentiles, in Christ's day, yet how much greater its fulfillment in our day, just before the great marriage feast is to take place!

  1. 7.When the marriage feast was all ready, what call was sent out? With what result? Verse 3.

Notes

Like the Jews the professed followers of Christ today are no more ready to respond to the invitation of grace than in former times, and especially when all things are now ready for participation in heaven's inheritance.

  1. 8.What shows there are repeated calls, growing more urgent, as the end draws near? Verse 4.

Notes

For more than ninety years the advent message has been preached in the Christian countries of the world.

  1. 9.What excuses are offered for not accepting the invitation? Verses 5, 6; Luke 14:18-20.

Notes

Men today are engrossed in the temporary affairs of a world that is soon to pass away. They are interested in getting gain, in social affairs, in mere intellectual attainments, in gaining positions of power and honor, while there is little or no time for the consideration of the urgent call, "Come; for all things are now ready." This thought comes as a solemn appeal to each of us.

  1. 10.To whom did the king turn for guests? How urgent was the call? Matt. 22:9, 10; Luke 14:23.

Notes

The last call was to be made in the streets and lanes, and in the hedges. It is significant that today the message is gathering its greatest harvest in the out-of-the-way places of the earth. The guests are responding, under the compelling message, and quickly the number will be made up.

Without a Wedding Garment

  1. 11.Before the great marriage feast, what precaution is taken? Matt. 22:11; Rev. 14:6, 7.

Notes

The examination of guests, in the parable, before they are seated, is a fitting illustration of the investigative judgment just before the marriage supper of the Lamb.

  1. 12.What is provided for each guest? What is this otherwise called? Isa. 61:10; Jer. 23:6; Rom. 3:26.
  2. 13.What experience is essential that we may be admitted to the marriage supper? Zech. 3:3-7.
  3. 14.Who are said to go in when the Bridegroom comes? Matt. 25:10.

Notes

While every truly converted soul has been cleansed from sin and clothed with the garments of Christ's righteousness, yet there must be maintained a watchfulness unto prayer, a cherishing and heeding of the Holy Spirit in the daily life. The oil, a symbol of the Spirit, must be kept aflame. This constitutes being ready.

Teaching Outline

  1. I.The Marriage of the Lamb 1. In heaven the people of God praise Him for His true judgments on Babylon. Rev. 19:1, 2. 2. The prophet describes the heavenly scene. Verses 6, 7. 3. The bridegroom's wife is robed in pure white. Verse 8.
  2. II.Our Lord's Parable 1. Christ illustrated the marriage supper by a parable. Matt. 22:1, 2. 2. The king repeatedly called His guests. Verses 3, 4. 3. Those invited ignored or resented this invitation. Verses 5, 6. 4. The king called guests in from highway and byway. Verses 8-10.
  3. III.Without a Wedding Garment 1. Each guest must have a wedding garment. Verses 11, 12. 2. This white raiment is essential for a guest to enter the marriage feast. Zech. 3:3-7. 3. Only those that are ready can attend. Matt. 25:10.
