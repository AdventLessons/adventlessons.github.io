Lesson 7 · February 15, 1913

The High Priest as Builder of the House of God

PDFShare

Questions

  1. 1.By being made "like unto His brethren," what kind of High Priest did Jesus become? Heb. 2:17.
  2. 2.In what exhortation was special attention directed to the faithfulness of Jesus the High Priest? Heb. 3:1, 2.
  3. 3.In what statement is reference made to the work of the priest as the builder of God's house? Verse 3.
  4. 4.From what statements of the Scriptures is it clear that this building of the house of God is a creative work identical with the new creation through the mediation of the eternal Son? Verse 4; Eph. 3:9; John 1:1, 3; Ps. 102:18. Note 1.
  5. 5.What is the house of God declared to be? 1 Tim. 3:15.
  6. 6.By what other name is the church known? 2 Cor. 6:16.
  7. 7.What did the prophet Zechariah declare that the priest upon the throne would do? Zech. 6:12, 13.
  8. 8.What statement of Christ's corresponds to this prophecy? Matt. 16:18.
  9. 9.How is an earthly house built? Ruth 4:11. Note 2.
  10. 10.In order that the Son of God might build the house of God, whose Son did He become? Matt. 22:42; 21:9; Luke 9:58.
  11. 11.What was the Son of God able to accomplish as the result of taking the flesh? Gal. 4:4, 5. Note 3.
  12. 12.How is this work of building the church defined in another scripture? Ps. 147:2.
  13. 13.At the council at Jerusalem, what instruction was given by the apostle James which shows that the building of the church is accomplished by the creative power of the Spirit of God? Acts 15:7-9, 13-16.
  14. 14.By the outpouring of the Spirit on the day of Pentecost, how many were built into the house of God? Acts 2:37-41.

Notes

1. The same God who is said to be the Builder of all things is also declared to be the Creator of all things; and this creative work is accomplished through the eternal Son, the Word of God. The work of building the church is also a creative work (2 Cor. 5:17, R.V., margin), and this is accomplished through the mediation of the same eternal Son.

2. An earthly house or family is built up by having children born into the family. This is emphasized in the Hebrew language in the fact that the root of the verb "to build" and the root of the noun "son" are the same.

3. Jesus was none the less the Son of God after He had been born in the flesh through the agency of the Holy Spirit. Luke 1:35. He was both Son of man, or Son of David, and the Son of God. Through His mediatorial work as priest, and the gift of the Holy Spirit, all believers are born again as children of God (1 John 3:1), and the house of God is thus built.
