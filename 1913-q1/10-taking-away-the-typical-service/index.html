Lesson 10 · March 8, 1913

Taking Away the Typical Service

PDFShare

Questions

  1. 1.What shows that the ritual service of the earthly sanctuary was not to be maintained permanently? Heb. 9:10.
  2. 2.What time is meant by "the time of reformation"? Verse 11.
  3. 3.What statement indicates the insufficiency of the sacrifices which were offered according to the ritual service? Heb. 10:1.
  4. 4.What proves that there was no perfection through the Levitical priesthood? Heb. 7:11. Note.
  5. 5.What became necessary on account of this inherent weakness of the ceremonial system? Verse 18.
  6. 6.Who took the place of the priests of the typical service? Verse 28.
  7. 7.What sanctuary took the place of the earthly sanctuary? Heb. 8:1, 2.
  8. 8.What sacrifice took the place of the many sacrifices offered by the priests in the earthly sanctuary? Heb. 10:11, 12; 9:26.
  9. 9.What expressions show that the new order of things brought in at "the time of reformation" was better than the old? Heb. 7:19, 22; 9:23.
  10. 10.What prediction made through the prophet Daniel concerning the taking away of the typical service by the Messiah was thus fulfilled? Dan. 9:27.
  11. 11.At the death of Christ, what remarkable occurrence indicated that the sacrifice of the earthly sanctuary to cease? Matt. 27:50, 51.
  12. 12.What indicates that Stephen, in his preaching, explained to the people that the typical service had been taken away? Acts 6:13, 14.
  13. 13.What charge brought against the apostle Paul shows that he was teaching that the real service had taken the place of the typical? Acts 21:27, 28.
  14. 14.What blessed experience is made possible through the one true offering? Heb. 10:14.
  15. 15.When Jesus had made purification of sins, where did He take His position? Heb. 1:3.
  16. 16.Upon what work did He then enter? Heb. 8:1, 2.
  17. 17.Where and to whom are we now to look for help in overcoming sin and living the triumphant life? Heb. 12:1, 2.
  18. 18.What prayer of David will thus be granted? Ps. 20:1, 2.

Notes

The Levitical priesthood was not an end, but a means. There was no spiritual efficacy in the ceremonies of the ancient ritual, and salvation could not be secured by following the duties prescribed in the Mosaic law. This whole system was intended to reveal Christ and His salvation to the people. To those who discerned this purpose, and looked beyond the type to the reality, a present salvation was just as real an experience as is possible since Christ was manifested in the flesh.
