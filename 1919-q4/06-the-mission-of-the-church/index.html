Lesson 6 · November 8, 1919

The Mission of the Church

PDFShare

Daily Study Outline

SEED THOUGHT: "If there had been but one lost soul, Christ would have died for that one."—"Christ's Object Lessons," page 187. "For one sinner Christ would have laid down His life."—Id., page 196.

  1. 1.Read "Christ's Object Lessons," pages 284-306.
  2. 2.Mission of the church, questions 1-3.
  3. 3.Individual responsibility, questions 4-11.
  4. 4.Fields ready for harvest, questions 12, 13.
  5. 5.The reward, questions 14-17.
  6. 6.Power promised, question 18.
  7. 7.Review the lesson.

Questions

  1. 1.What was Christ's object in placing His church in the world? Matt. 5:14-16; John 17:18.
  2. 2.What was the mission of Christ to the world? Luke 19:10; John 10:10, 11. Note 1.
  3. 3.By whose authority did He work? John 8:42; 12:44-46.
  4. 4.For how many did Jesus die? 1 Tim. 2:3-6; Heb. 2:9. Note 2.
  5. 5.What has He given to every man? Mark 13:34. Note 3.
  6. 6.With whom do we labor? 1 Cor. 3:9; Deut. 31:8.
  7. 7.How will even small acts of labor, if done for Jesus, be rewarded? Mark 9:41. Note 4.
  8. 8.How extensively is the gospel to be preached? Rev. 14:6, 7; Mark 16:15.
  9. 9.What assurance is given by the Lord that His word will accomplish its purpose? Isa. 55:10, 11.
  10. 10.How are we to labor? Ps. 126:5, 6; Acts 20:31. Note 5.
  11. 11.With what confidence may we labor? 1 Cor. 15:58.
  12. 12.What fact should now invite us to earnest labor? John 9:4.
  13. 13.What may be truly said of the field at the present time? John 4:35, 36; compare Rev. 14:14, 15.
  14. 14.How much zeal should we manifest in our labor? Eccl. 9:10. Note 6.
  15. 15.What will be the crown of rejoicing of the redeemed? Phil. 4:1; 1 Thess. 2:19, 20.
  16. 16.What will be the reward of those who bring sheaves to God? Dan. 12:3.
  17. 17.What is said of those who win souls? Prov. 11:30.
  18. 18.What power is promised to those who labor for the lost? Matt. 28:18-20.

Notes

1. "In the parable the shepherd goes out to search for one sheep,- the very least that can be numbered. So if there had been but one lost soul, Christ would have died for that one."—"Christ's Object Lessons," page 187.

2. "He [Christ] left His home of security and peace, left the glory that He had with the Father before the world was, left His position upon the throne of the universe. He went forth, a suffering, tempted man; went forth in solitude, to sow in tears, to water with His blood, the seed of life for a world lost."—Id., page 36.

3. "Not more surely is the place prepared for us in the heavenly mansions than is the special place designated on earth where we are to work for God."—Id., page 327.

4. "The smallest duty done in sincerity and self-forgetfulness, is more pleasing to God than the greatest work when marred with self-seeking."—Id., page 402.

5. It is our part to sow the seed (Eccl. 11:6); it is God's part to give the increase (1 Cor. 3:6).

6. "As the will of man cooperates with the will of God, it becomes omnipotent. Whatever is to be done at His command, may be accomplished in His strength. All His biddings are enablings."—Id., page 333.
