Lesson 13 · July 17, 1886

Redeemed from the Curse of the Law

PDFShare
  1. 1.From what has Christ redeemed us? Gal. 3:13, first part.
  2. 2.What is the keeping of the commandments? 1 John 5:3.
  3. 3.If keeping the commandments is love, can it be also the curse of which Paul speaks?
  4. 4.Upon whom does the curse of the law fall? Gal. 3:10; Jer. 11:3, 4.
  5. 5.What is the curse for disobedience? Rom. 6:23.
  6. 6.How many people have been perfectly obedient? Rom. 3:9, 10, 23.
  7. 7.Then upon how many does the curse of the law fall? Rom. 3:19.
  8. 8.How many, consequently, stand in need of redemption?
  9. 9.How has Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law? Gal. 3:13.
  10. 10.How was he made a curse for us? Gal. 3:13.
  11. 11.What does this prove the curse of the law to be?
  12. 12.Having suffered the curse of the law, death, what does he give to those who believe in him? John 3:16.
  13. 13.For what is the blessing of life promised? Gal. 3:12; Matt. 19:17.
  14. 14.And what is inseparably connected with faith? Rom. 3:31; Rev. 14:12.
  15. 15.How only is it possible for us to keep the commandments, and thus gain the blessing of life? John 15:4, 5; Heb. 11:6.
