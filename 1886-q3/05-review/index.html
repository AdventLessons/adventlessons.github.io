Lesson 15 · July 31, 1886

Review

PDFShare
  1. 1.What is sin? 1 John 3:4.
  2. 2.In what condition are all the world? Rom. 3:23.
  3. 3.Then in what service are all men? Rom. 6:16, 17.
  4. 4.If men are overcome by sin, in what condition are they?—In bondage. 2 Peter 2:19.
  5. 5.What is it that declares that all men are thus in bondage? Rom. 3:19.
  6. 6.What are the works of the flesh? Gal. 5:19-21.
  7. 7.What are the fruits of the Spirit? Gal. 5:22, 23.
  8. 8.With what are the fruits of the Spirit in harmony?
  9. 9.Then of what must the works of the flesh be the transgression?
  10. 10.In what condition are those who are not led by the Spirit? Gal. 5:18.
  11. 11.Then what is it to be "under the law"?
  12. 12.In order to redeem men, what position was it necessary for Christ to take? Heb. 2:17.
  13. 13.Then since he came to redeem sinners, those under the law, how was he made? Gal. 4:4, 5.
  14. 14.Was Christ indeed counted as a sinner? 2 Cor. 5:21; Isa. 53:12.
  15. 15.What curse is pronounced upon transgressors of the law? Gen. 2:17; Rom. 6:23.
  16. 16.How did Christ redeem us from this curse? Gal. 3:13.
  17. 17.For what purpose did Christ thus take upon himself sin and death? 2 Cor. 5:21, last clause.
  18. 18.What is the righteousness of God? Isa. 51:6, 7.
  19. 19.Since "in Christ" we become doers of the law, what does that insure to us? Matt. 19:17, last part.
  20. 20.What then may we say that Christ is to us? 1 Cor. 1:30.
