Lesson 16 · August 7, 1886

The Sabbath

PDFShare
  1. 1.Quote a testimony from the Psalms concerning the perpetuity of the law of God. Ps. 119:152.
  2. 2.Is there any exception to this fact? Ps. 119:160.
  3. 3.What did Jesus say on this subject? Matt. 5:18; Luke 16:17.
  4. 4.What did he say of those who should break one of them and should teach others to do so? Matt. 5:19.
  5. 5.Repeat the fourth commandment. Ex. 20:8-11.
  6. 6.According to the words of Inspiration, how long must this commandment endure? See texts quoted above.
  7. 7.How much of it may be changed?
  8. 8.Might not God or Christ change it? Compare Matt. 5:18 with 2 Tim. 2:13.
  9. 9.What reason is given in the fourth commandment for the sanctification of the Sabbath? Ex. 20:11.
  10. 10.In how many days did the Lord create the heaven and the earth?
  11. 11.Where do we find the record of creation? Genesis 1.
  12. 12.What is said of each of these six days of creation? Gen. 1:5, 8, 13, 19, 23, 31.
  13. 13.What did God make to rule over these days? Gen. 1:16.
  14. 14.Then what kind of days are referred to in the first chapter of Genesis?—Days consisting of a dark and a light part, regulated by the sun.
  15. 15.What is the length of such days?—24 hours.
  16. 16.At the end of the first six days, what had been done? Gen. 2:1.
  17. 17.What is said of the seventh day? Gen. 2:2.
  18. 18.Did God do any work on the seventh day? Gen. 2:2; Ex. 20:11.
  19. 19.After God had rested on the seventh day, what action did he take concerning it? Gen. 2:3.
