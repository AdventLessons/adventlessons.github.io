Lesson 21 · September 11, 1886

The Shadowy Sabbaths and the Enduring Sabbath

PDFShare
  1. 1.What does Paul say that Christ has blotted out? Col. 2:14.
  2. 2.What else has he done to these ordinances? Col. 2:14, last part.
  3. 3.Because this has been done, in respect to what are we not to be judged? Col. 2:16.
  4. 4.On what were the ten commandments written? Deut. 4:12, 13; 5:22.
  5. 5.How were they graven on these tables of stone? Ex. 32:15, 16.
  6. 6.Could words engraved in stone be blotted out?
  7. 7.Would it be proper to speak of "nailing to the cross" a law that was engraved in stone?
  8. 8.Then what must we conclude concerning the law spoken of in Col. 2:14?—That it has no connection with the ten commandments.
  9. 9.How did the ordinances that were blotted out stand related to men? Col. 2:14.
  10. 10.Is the Sabbath of the Lord contrary to us? Mark 2:27.
  11. 11.When was the Sabbath made for man? Gen. 2:1-3.
  12. 12.What was man's condition at that time? Gen. 1:31; Eccl. 7:29.
  13. 13.Did man then stand in need of a Saviour?—No. See 1 Tim. 1:15.
  14. 14.But what was the object of the Sabbaths mentioned in Col. 2:16? Col. 2:17.
  15. 15.Where do we find a record of sabbaths that were ceremonial and shadowy? Lev. 23:5-7, 24, 27, 34, 39.
  16. 16.How often did each of these sabbaths come?—Once a year.
  17. 17.Did the Lord command these sabbaths with his own voice as he did the Sabbath of the fourth commandment? Lev. 23:1, 4.
  18. 18.Were they to be confounded with the seventh-day Sabbath? Lev. 23:37, 38.
  19. 19.Repeat the fourth commandment.
  20. 20.How long is this to remain unchanged? Ps. 111:7, 8; 119:152, 160.
