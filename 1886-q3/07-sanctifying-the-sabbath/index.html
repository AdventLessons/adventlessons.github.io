Lesson 17 · August 14, 1886

Sanctifying the Sabbath

PDFShare
  1. 1.In how many days were the heavens and the earth completed? Ex. 20:11.
  2. 2.What can you say of the seventh day?
  3. 3.After God had rested on the seventh day, what did he do?
  4. 4.Why did he hallow, or sanctify, the seventh day? Gen. 2:3.
  5. 5.When Moses, according to the Lord's instruction (Ex. 19:12), had set bounds about Mount Sinai, what did he say had been done to it? Ex. 19:23.
  6. 6.What did the children of Israel do with certain cities? Josh. 20:7, margin.
  7. 7.In what words did the Lord, through one of his prophets, command that a fast should be appointed? Joel 2:15.
  8. 8.From these instances, what must be the meaning of "sanctify"?—To appoint; to set apart for a special use; to make sacred or holy.
  9. 9.Then what is really meant by the expression, "God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it"?—That God appointed, or set apart, the seventh day for a special use; in short, that he commanded that the seventh day should be kept holy.
  10. 10.Was any one living to whom such a command could have been given? Gen. 1:26, 27.
  11. 11.For whom did Jesus say the Sabbath was made? Mark 2:27.
  12. 12.In what sense is the word "man" here used?—In the sense of "the human race; mankind; the totality of men."—Webster.
  13. 13.Did the Saviour mean that the Sabbath belongs to man? Ex. 20:10.
  14. 14.Then is it proper to speak of the seventh day as the Sabbath of any particular class of people?—Certainly not; it is God's own property.
  15. 15.Did God bless the Sabbath institution or the Sabbath day? Ex. 20:11.
  16. 16.What is the great design of the Sabbath? Ezek. 20:12, 20.
  17. 17.How is the true God distinguished from false gods? Ps. 96:4, 5; Jer. 10:10-12.
  18. 18.From what may we learn the power and glory of God? Ps. 19:1; Rom. 1:19, 20.
  19. 19.Then how does the Sabbath serve as a sign to remind people of the true God? Gen. 2:1-3.
  20. 20.Does the Lord wish to be remembered by only a few? Ps. 33:8.
  21. 21.Then how extensively should the Sabbath be kept?
