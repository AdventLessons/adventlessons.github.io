Lesson 14 · July 24, 1886

The Law That Was Abolished, and the Law of God

PDFShare
  1. 1.What has Christ abolished in his flesh? Eph. 2:15.
  2. 2.What is love? 1 John 5:3.
  3. 3.Can the same thing be both love and enmity?
  4. 4.In another place what testimony did Paul give concerning the law of God? Rom. 7:12.
  5. 5.How did he feel towards it? Rom. 7:22.
  6. 6.Then can it be the law of God, or ten commandments, that is spoken of as "the enmity" in Eph. 2:15?
  7. 7.What did God speak to the people from Sinai? Deut. 4:12, 13.
  8. 8.Did he speak any other words in the hearing of the people, besides the ten commandments? Deut. 5:22.
  9. 9.When the Lord had finished speaking the ten commandments, what did the people say? Ex. 20:19.
  10. 10.What did Moses and the people then do? Ex. 20:21.
  11. 11.How did the Lord, after he had given the ten commandments, give other instruction to the people? Neh. 9:14, last part.
  12. 12.Where do we find some of the things which the Lord gave through Moses? Exodus, chapters 25-30.
  13. 13.Is there any part of the instruction that has reference to flesh? Lev. 4; 6:25-30; 10:16-18.
  14. 14.Did these offerings really take away sin? Heb. 10:4.
  15. 15.Who only can really take away sin? John 1:29; Heb. 9:26.
  16. 16.How did Christ bear our sins? 1 Peter 2:24.
  17. 17.Of what must we be partakers in order to have eternal life? John 6:53, 54.
  18. 18.Were the sacrifices to be continued after Christ suffered? Heb. 10:4, 5.
  19. 19.Then how are they done away?—Christ abolished them "in his flesh;" that is, they found their fulfillment in the body of Christ.
  20. 20.Were these ordinances a cause of enmity between the Jews and the Gentiles? Acts 11:2, 3.
  21. 21.After Christ did this cause of enmity exist? Gal. 6:15.
  22. 22.Through Christ what union was effected? Eph. 2:16-18.
  23. 23.Are those thus brought together planted on a new foundation? Eph. 2:19, 20.
  24. 24.After circumcision and the ceremonies connected with it had passed away, what still remained in full force? 1 Cor. 7:19.
