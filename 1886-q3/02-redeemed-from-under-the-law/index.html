Lesson 12 · July 10, 1886

Redeemed from Under the Law

PDFShare
  1. 1.What does Paul say of those who are led by the Spirit? Gal. 5:18.
  2. 2.Then what must be the condition of those who are not led by the Spirit?
  3. 3.What is directly contrary to the Spirit? Gal. 5:17.
  4. 4.Since those who are in opposition to the Spirit are under the law, and the flesh is also opposed to the Spirit, to what is being in the flesh equivalent?—To being under the law.
  5. 5.What are the works of the flesh? Gal. 5:19-21.
  6. 6.What are the fruits of the Spirit? Gal. 5:22, 23.
  7. 7.With what are the fruits of the Spirit in harmony? Gal. 5:23.
  8. 8.Then since the flesh and the Spirit are contrary to each other, of what must the works of the flesh be a transgression?
  9. 9.And since following the flesh is being "under the law," what is it to be under the law?
  10. 10.In another place what does Paul say of us when we were "children"? Gal. 4:3.
  11. 11.To what were we in bondage? Gal. 4:3.
  12. 12.To what does he say the Galatians were desirous of returning? Gal. 4:9.
  13. 13.While in that state of bondage, what had they done? Gal. 4:8.
  14. 14.Then what is meant by the elements of the world? Eph. 2:1, 2.
  15. 15.What did God do in the fullness of time? Gal. 4:4.
  16. 16.For what purpose was his Son sent? Gal. 4:5.
  17. 17.In order to redeem those under the law, how was Christ made? Gal. 4:4.
  18. 18.What does Paul elsewhere say Christ was made for us? 2 Cor. 5:21.
  19. 19.Why was he made to be sin for us? 2 Cor. 5:21, last part.
  20. 20.Being redeemed from under the law, what do we receive? Gal. 4:5.
  21. 21.Who alone are the sons of God? Rom. 8:14.
  22. 22.And with what are their works in harmony? Gal. 5:22, 23.
  23. 23.What is said of such? Rom. 8:1.
