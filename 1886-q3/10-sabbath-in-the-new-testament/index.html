Lesson 20 · September 4, 1886

Sabbath in the New Testament

PDFShare
  1. 1.How did Jesus regard the commandments? John 15:10.
  2. 2.Then must he not have kept the Sabbath?
  3. 3.What direct evidence have we that Christ kept the Sabbath? Luke 4:16.
  4. 4.What is the meaning of the word "custom"?—"Frequent repetition of the same act; way of acting; ordinary manner; habitual practice; usage."
  5. 5.How can people show themselves to be Christians? 1 John 2:6.
  6. 6.What apostle is specially noted as being a follower of Christ in respect to the Sabbath? Acts 17:1, 2.
  7. 7.Can you cite other instances of this practice by Paul and his fellow-laborers? Acts 13:14, 42, 44; 18:4.
  8. 8.While at Antioch in Pisidia, what did Paul say of the relation of the Jews to the prophecies concerning Christ? Acts 13:27.
  9. 9.How often did he say these prophecies were read?—Every Sabbath day.
  10. 10.What did James say on the same subject? Acts 15:21.
  11. 11.What do these texts prove?—They prove that in the New Testament no day is recognized as the Sabbath except the day on which the Jews habitually worshiped. The regular reading of the Scriptures in the synagogues was only on the seventh day of the week, and since that is said to have been "every Sabbath day," it shows that no day but the seventh day is recognized as the Sabbath.
  12. 12.When the prophets and apostles wrote the Bible, by whose authority did they write? 2 Peter 1:21.
  13. 13.Then what is indicated by the fact that the seventh day both in the Old Testament and the New, is always spoken of as the Sabbath?—That the Spirit of God attests that the seventh day is the Sabbath. Christians must call a thing by the same name that God does.
  14. 14.After Christ had healed a man on the Sabbath day, what did he say? Matt. 12:12.
  15. 15.What does this indicate?—That Christ recognized a Sabbath law.
  16. 16.Where is the only law for the Sabbath? Ex. 20:8-11.
  17. 17.Why is not this commandment repeated in the New Testament?—"Express warrant is needed to warrant a change; but it is not needed to warrant a continuation..... When once bidden to walk in a straight line, it does not require the successive impulse of new biddings to make us persevere in it. But it would require a new bidding to justify our going off from the line, into a track of deviation."—Dr. Chalmers; 14th lecture on Romans.
  18. 18.On whose authority does the Sabbath rest? Gen. 2:3; Ex. 20:11.
  19. 19.Why then must we expect to find no change in the Sabbath law? James 1:17.
