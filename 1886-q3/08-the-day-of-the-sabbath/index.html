Lesson 18 · August 21, 1886

The Day of the Sabbath

PDFShare
  1. 1.How many days are there in a week?
  2. 2.How are the first six days of the week to be occupied? Ex. 20:9; Lev. 23:3.
  3. 3.What example have we of such a use of the first six days of the week? Ex. 20:11.
  4. 4.What is to be done on the seventh day? Ex. 20:10; Lev. 23:3.
  5. 5.On what day was Jesus crucified? Luke 23:54.
  6. 6.What day followed the preparation day? Ib.
  7. 7.How did the women who followed Jesus pass that day? Luke 23:56.
  8. 8.What did they do "when the Sabbath was past"? Mark 16:1, 2.
  9. 9.On what day was this? Luke 24:1.
  10. 10.Since they rested on the day before the first day of the week, on what day did they rest?—On the seventh day of the week.
  11. 11.In so doing, with what did they agree? Luke 23:56, last part.
  12. 12.Then what day of the week is enjoined in the commandment?
  13. 13.And what is the name of the seventh day of the week? Ex. 20:10, first part.
  14. 14.Was it a particular day of the week, or only the Sabbath in a general way, that God blessed and sanctified? Gen. 2:3.
  15. 15.Is there any doubt as to which day is the seventh day, the true Sabbath?—Bishop E. O. Haven, of the M. E. Church, while president of Michigan University, said: "There is no good reason for denying that the Jewish Sabbath [by this he meant the Sabbath which the Jews keep] is the true seventh day, reckoning from the creation of man, and that the Christian Sunday is the first day of the Hebrew week, or of the genuine week."—Pillars of Truth, p. 89.

Notes

Webster's Unabridged Dictionary defines Saturday as follows: "The seventh or last day of the week; the day following Friday and preceding Sunday."

  1. 16.For whom was the Sabbath made? Mark 2:27.
  2. 17.On how much of the earth did God design that man should dwell? Acts 17:24-26.
  3. 18.Then did not God design that the Sabbath should be kept in every part of the world?
  4. 19.Since it is evident that God designed that the Sabbath, the seventh day, should be kept in every part of the world, what can you say as to the possibility of doing so?
  5. 20.Upon whom does the Lord pronounce a blessing? Isa. 56:2.
  6. 21.Is this promise extended to any besides the Jewish people? Isa. 56:6.
