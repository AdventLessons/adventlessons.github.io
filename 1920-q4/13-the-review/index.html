Lesson 13 · December 25, 1920

The Review

PDFShare

Creation and Fall

Lesson 1

  1. 1.What did God create in the beginning?
  2. 2.How did He create all things?

Lesson 2

  1. 1.Tell what you can of Adam's home and occupation.
  2. 2.Point out differences between nature then and now.

Lesson 3

  1. 1.Describe the evil work of doubt in the Garden of Eden.
  2. 2.What was the curse pronounced upon man? Upon woman? Upon the earth?

Truth and Error about Creation

Lesson 4

  1. 1.Show that the work of creation was completed in six days, and is not now going on.
  2. 2.What modern error does this truth overthrow?

Lesson 5

  1. 1.Give proofs that the six days of creation were literal days.
  2. 2.What is the relation of the Sabbath to the literal week?

The Flood and Its Effects

Lesson 6

  1. 1.Describe the beginnings of apostasy before the Flood.
  2. 2.How did God deal with this apostasy?

Lesson 7

  1. 1.Show that the Flood was universal.
  2. 2.What covenant did God make with Noah regarding the Flood?

Lesson 8

  1. 1.Mention some of the changes in nature effected by the Flood.
  2. 2.How did apostasy arise again after the Flood?

Nature and Revelation

Lesson 9

  1. 1.How is the book of nature related to the book of revelation (the Bible)?
  2. 2.What great law of life established at creation still holds good?

Lesson 10

  1. 1.Mention some scientific facts revealed by writers of the Bible.
  2. 2.What prophecy predicts a marked increase of scientific and spiritual knowledge?

Apostasy and the Message

Lesson 11

  1. 1.Point out some ways in which false science and religion have been mixed in these last days.
  2. 2.What timely message does God send to this generation?

Lesson 12

  1. 1.Give important reasons why the truth of God as Creator should be recognized and preached in the last days.
  2. 2.What special message of reform was predicted of the last days? What is the sign of loyalty to the Creator?
