Lesson 10 · September 4, 1920

The School in the Advent Movement

PDFShare

Daily Study Outline

SubjectAssignment
1.Jesus' love for His workQues. 1-3
2.The teaching and works of JesusQues. 4,5
3.The Holy Spirit as teacherQues. 6-11
4.Preparation for the second adventQues. 12,13
5.Kind of workmen neededQues. 14-18
6.Personal preparation of the workerQues. 19,20
7.Review the lesson.

Questions

  1. 1.How is the work of Jesus while on earth characterized? Acts 1:1.
  2. 2.What did Jesus say it was His daily food to do? John 4:34.
  3. 3.How did Jesus express the intimate association of the Father and Himself in all His work? John 5:17, 19. Literally these verses read: "My Father is working up till the present moment and I am working. . . . The Son is not able to be doing anything of Himself but what He sees the Father doing; for whatsoever things He may be doing, these also the Son is likewise doing."
  4. 4.What does Jesus say of His teaching? John 8:28, last part; 14:10, 24; 17:8.
  5. 5.What kind of works is the believer to do? John 14:12.
  6. 6.Who succeeds Jesus as teacher? Verse 26, first part.
  7. 7.What does the Holy Spirit teach? Verse 26, last part; John 16:13.
  8. 8.Whom does the Holy Spirit teach? Same verses; John 20:21, 22. (In a special sense, the Spirit teaches those who are sent.)
  9. 9.Whom are the sent ones—the missionaries—to teach? Matt. 28:19, 20.
  10. 10.How long is their teaching to continue? Verse 20, last part.
  11. 11.What takes place at the end of the world? John 14:2, 3; Acts 1:11.
  12. 12.What work must be done to prepare for the second advent? Matt. 24:14; Rev. 14:6-14.
  13. 13.What people are to do this work? Rev. 14:12; 12:17; 19:10.
  14. 14.What kind of workmen should the remnant people study to be? 2 Tim. 2:15.
  15. 15.What are they commanded to teach and to preach? Deut. 6:6-9; 2 Tim. 4:1, 2.
  16. 16.What then must be their chief study, in school and out?
  17. 17.What is the Word able to do in the personal life? Ps. 119:11, 105, 140; 2 Tim. 3:15.
  18. 18.What is the Holy Scripture able to do for the gospel worker? 2 Tim. 3:16, 17.
  19. 19.What personal work must every man do before he can become a vessel meet for the Master's use? 2 Tim. 2:20, 21.
  20. 20.What part may the Christian school act in the advent movement, to hasten the finishing of the work?

Lessons We Can Learn

  1. 1.As Jesus' work was both to do and to teach, so are we to do what we teach, practice what we preach, that we may teach through doing, and preach through practicing. Living like this is more to be esteemed than necessary food. Job 23:12.
  2. 2.I can do all things in Him that strengthens me—the Father working hitherto and I working.
  3. 3.Jesus promised to send the Holy Spirit as His personal representative, to teach us all things and to guide us into all truth; but He ordained that we receive the Holy Spirit, and that He teach through us. On this condition, Jesus sends us into all the world to teach all nations and preach the gospel to every creature.
  4. 4.Jesus through Paul bids us study to become workmen that need not be ashamed, vessels unto honor, meet for the Master's use, thoroughly furnished unto every good work.
  5. 5.The Scriptures must be our chief study, in school and out; for they are able to make us wise unto our own salvation and unto the salvation of others.
  6. 6.The Christian school is one of the chief agencies to save our children from the world, and to furnish the advent movement with laborers for the finishing of the work.

Notes

"Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that He will send forth laborers into His harvest."
