Lesson 8 · August 21, 1920

The School of Christ

PDFShare

Questions

  1. 1.What was one capacity in which Jesus came to earth? John 3:2.
  2. 2.Who sent Him into the world? John 17:18, first part.
  3. 3.What may He therefore be properly called? Ans. A Teacher sent from God.
  4. 4.How is Jesus' work on earth frequently characterized? Matt. 4:23; 9:35; 11:1. The term "teach" alone is used of His work more than thirty times in the four Gospels, besides His being called by terms equivalent to "teacher" more than fifty times.
  5. 5.Where was His teaching largely done? Matt. 5:1; 13:1; Luke 5:3; 6:17; 7:36; 9:10; 10:38; 14:1; 20:1; John 10:23. Jesus taught much out of doors, amid scenes of nature, but also in the homes of the people, and wherever the people resorted.
  6. 6.Whom did He specially call as learners, or disciples? John 1:43; Matt. 4:18-22. Others were called, making twelve in all.
  7. 7.What kind of men were they? Matt. 4:18; 9:9. "Jesus chose unlearned fishermen because they had not been schooled in the traditions and erroneous customs of their time. They were men of native ability, and they were humble and teachable."—"The Desire of Ages," page 250.
  8. 8.In what ways did Jesus teach? Matt. 5:2 (by word of mouth); 9:35 (by observation); 13:3 (in parables); John 13:15 (by example); Matt. 16:13; John 21:15 and onward (by questioning); Matt. 16:23 (by rebuke); John 14:1-3 (by comfort and assurance).
  9. 9.How did He train the disciples in service? Matt. 14:19; 10:5, 6, 16; Luke 10:1.
  10. 10.What success did Jesus have in teaching His disciples? John 17:12.
  11. 11.How much did Jesus love His disciples? John 15:9.
  12. 12.How did He show the extent of His love? John 10:11, 15.
  13. 13.How did He commend His love to the disciples? John 15:13.
  14. 14.For what great work had Jesus been preparing His disciples? John 20:21.
  15. 15.What special endowment did He give them? Verse 22.
  16. 16.What is the record of the apostles and others after the ascension and Pentecost? Acts 8:4.

Lessons We Can Learn

  1. 1.Jesus came into the world as a teacher sent from God.
  2. 2.He chose men of humble occupation, native ability, and teachable spirit, to be His disciples, and kept them in training for about three and a half years, preparing them to carry on the work He began.
  3. 3.These disciples had not been schooled in the traditions and customs of their time, and were "men whom He could educate for His work."—"The Desire of Ages," page 250.
  4. 4.He taught them pure truth by precept, by parable, by observation, by example, and by experience in service under His guidance.
  5. 5.He loved them fervently in spite of their faults and errors, even to the point of laying down His life for them.
  6. 6.He appointed them His successors in the work of the gospel.
  7. 7.They received the Holy Spirit, and went everywhere preaching the Word.
