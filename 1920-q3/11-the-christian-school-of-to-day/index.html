Lesson 11 · September 11, 1920

The Christian School of To-Day

PDFShare

Daily Study Outline

SubjectAssignment
1.True basis of wisdom and knowledgeQues. 1-4
2.Responsibility for teaching the youngQues. 5,6
3.Training of teachers and workersQues. 7,8
4.Conditions in society and the schoolsQues. 9-11
5.Warnings against false teachingsQues. 12-16
6.Kinds of teaching needed by young men and womenQues. 17-19
7.Review the lesson.

Questions

  1. 1.Where do true wisdom and knowledge begin? Ps. 111:10; Prov. 1:7.
  2. 2.What is the fear of the Lord? Prov. 8:13.
  3. 3.What is the true basis of understanding? Prov. 9:10, last part; Job 28:28.
  4. 4.How do wisdom and understanding grow? Deut. 4:5, 6; Eph. 4:15; 1 Cor. 1:30.
  5. 5.How early in life should the Word of the Lord be taught? 2 Tim. 3:15; Ps. 71:17.
  6. 6.What is the responsibility of parents and teachers of the young in this respect? Deut. 4:9, 10.
  7. 7.What scripture expresses the great need to-day for training of teachers? 2 Tim. 2:2.
  8. 8.What scriptures suggest the importance of Bible study in training schools for gospel workers? Verse 15; 2 Tim. 3:16, 17.
  9. 9.What conditions in society in the last days emphasize the necessity of schools that teach pure truth? 2 Tim. 3:1-5, 7; 4:3, 4.
  10. 10.Against what kind of teaching are we warned? 1 Tim. 1:3-7; 2 Tim. 2:23; Titus 3:9.
  11. 11.Of what three types of subtle and insidious teaching must we beware, especially in the schools of to-day? Col. 2:8.
  12. 12.Through Paul's instruction to Timothy, against what attractive kind of teaching are young men of to-day warned? 1 Tim. 6:20.
  13. 13.Why is Paul so earnest about the matter? Verse 21.
  14. 14.To what should every young man and woman hold fast? Titus 1:9, first part.
  15. 15.Why is it important to do this? Verse 9, last part, and verse 10.
  16. 16.What is the only safe thing to study and teach? Titus 2:1.
  17. 17.What kind of teaching do young women need? Verses 4, 5.
  18. 18.What are young men exhorted to be and do? Verses 6-8.
  19. 19.What should be the great central theme of the teaching in the Christian school of to-day? Verses 11-14.

Lessons We Can Learn

  1. 1.Departing from evil is the beginning of wisdom and true knowledge.
  2. 2.Knowledge of the holy is the basis for understanding.
  3. 3.Wisdom and understanding grow when the truth is kept and taught in love. "Thy Word is truth."

Picture: School, Shanghai, China

  1. 4.The Scriptures should be taught diligently and daily by parents and by teachers of children.
  2. 5.One large function of the school is to educate "faithful men who shall be able to teach others also."
  3. 6.The Christian schools of to-day should so teach that they may furnish men of God for all kinds of good works.
  4. 7.Conditions in society and in the popular schools call loudly for a type of school that teaches pure truth—not fables, not traditions of men, not rudiments of the world, not philosophy and vain deceit, not science falsely so-called; for all these cause many to err from the faith.
  5. 8.Young men and women should be so taught that they hold fast to sound doctrine, that they may be sober, chaste, sincere, sound in speech, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, looking for the blessed hope and the glorious appearing of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
