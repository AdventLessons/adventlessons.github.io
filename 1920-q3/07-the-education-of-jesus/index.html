Lesson 7 · August 14, 1920

The Education of Jesus

PDFShare

Daily Study Outline

SubjectAssignment
1.Why and how Jesus came to earthQues. 1-4
2.Jesus' development as a childQues. 5-8
3.Educational environment of JesusQues. 9-12
4.Effect of Jesus' contact with othersQues. 13-16
5.Source of what Jesus learned and taughtQues. 17, 18
6.The knowledge Jesus' followers should seekQues. 19, 20
7.Review the lesson.

Questions

  1. 1.For what purpose did Jesus come into the world? Matt. 1:21; John 10:10; Isa. 61:1-3.
  2. 2.In what way did He come? John 1:14.
  3. 3.Why did Jesus come in the way He did? Rom. 8:3; Gal. 3:13; Heb. 2:9, 10.
  4. 4.In what spirit did Jesus take up the work He came to do? John 5:30, 31; 8:28, 54.
  5. 5.What is the first record we have of Jesus' development as a child? Luke 2:40.
  6. 6.At what age did He first say He was about His Father's business? Verses 42, 49.
  7. 7.What is said further of Jesus' development after this? Verse 52.
  8. 8.In what directions did He develop? Ans. In wisdom—mentally; in stature—physically; in favor with God—spiritually; in favor with man—socially.
  9. 9.In what kind of place was He born? Verse 7.
  10. 10.In what kind of place did He live? John 1:46.
  11. 11.What was His occupation? Mark 6:3.
  12. 12.How did He relate Himself to His earthly parents? Luke 2:51.
  13. 13.What impression did Jesus make in His first interview with learned men, when only twelve years old? Verses 46, 47.
  14. 14.How did He impress officers of the law who sought to arrest Him? John 7:46.
  15. 15.What effect did His communion with two of the disciples have upon them? Luke 24:32.
  16. 16.Did Jesus attend the schools of His day? John 7:15.
  17. 17.What was the source of what He taught? John 8:28; 12:49. "Of Myself" means "from Myself as a source," the same as when Jesus says, "The Son can do nothing of Himself."
  18. 18.What did John the beloved disciple, in after years, say he himself taught? 1 John 1:1, 3.
  19. 19.Wherein does the wisdom of the world fail? 1 Cor. 1:21.
  20. 20.After what knowledge should every follower of Jesus especially seek? Jer. 9:23, 24.

Lessons We Can Learn

  1. 1.Jesus had a special and important work to do.
  2. 2.As He was to work for humanity, He came in the form of humanity, identified Himself with humanity, and gave Himself up to saving humanity.
  3. 3.He was born in poverty, lived in a place of no repute, was subject to human parents.
  4. 4.As a child, He developed in the various ways other children do—physically, mentally, spiritually, socially.
  5. 5.When about His Father's business, He astonished the learned doctors of law with His understanding and answers at twelve years of age, amazed and unnerved officers of the law with what He said, caused the hearts of disciples to burn within them while He communed with them.
  6. 6.Jesus never attended any of the schools of the time, yet He was a master in letters, powerful in speech, a marvel in reaching the heart.
  7. 7.As the world by wisdom knows not God, we are not to glory in any wisdom, might, or learning of this world, but rather glory in understanding and knowing God.
