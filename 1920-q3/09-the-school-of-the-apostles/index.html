Lesson 9 · August 28, 1920

The School of the Apostles

PDFShare

Daily Study Outline

SubjectAssignment
1.Commissioning of the apostlesQues. 1-4
2.Witnessing for ChristQues. 5-7
3.Changes wrought in Peter under ChristQues. 8,9
4.The transformation of John and PhilipQues. 10-12
5.The education of PaulQues. 13-19
6.Discipline in the school of ChristQues. 20
7.Review the lesson.

Questions

  1. 1.What special appointment, after His resurrection, did Jesus make with the disciples? Matt. 26:32; 28:7, 16.
  2. 2.At this appointed meeting, what commission did Jesus give His disciples? Matt. 28:18-20; Mark 16:15.
  3. 3.What were the disciples called after this? Acts 1:2.
  4. 4.What further instruction did the apostles have from the great Teacher after the resurrection? Verse 3.
  5. 5.What command did Jesus give the apostles pertaining to the final act in their preparation as witnesses for Him? Verse 4.
  6. 6.After receiving the promise, where were they to be witnesses? Verse 8.
  7. 7.How did the work of the apostles in "the field" confirm the efficiency of their education in the school of Christ? Mark 16:20; Acts 2:41-43, 46, 47.
  8. 8.What wonderful change had been wrought in Peter? Matt. 4:18, 19; Acts 1:15; 2:14, 37-40.
  9. 9.Point out two steps in the fulfillment of Jesus' promise to Peter at his call. Matt. 4:19; Luke 5:4-10; Acts 2:41.
  10. 10.What marked change had John undergone? Mark 3:17. "Evil temper, revenge, the spirit of criticism, were all in the beloved disciple. He was proud, and ambitious to be the first in the kingdom of God."—"The Desire of Ages," page 295. See John 21:20-24; Rev. 1:1, 9, 10.
  11. 11.Note the change in Philip. John 6:5, 7; 14:8, 9. He was "slow of heart, and inclined to doubt."—"The Desire of Ages," page 296. See Acts 8:26-40; 21:8, 9.
  12. 12.Who was last to be called as an apostle? Acts 9:15; 22:21; 1 Cor. 1:1; 15:8.
  13. 13.What had been Paul's education? Acts 22:3; 5:34.
  14. 14.What was the effect of this education? Acts 26:4, 5.
  15. 15.After Paul's conversion, how did he prepare himself for entering upon the work of his calling? Gal. 1:15-18.
  16. 16.How did Paul esteem himself and his labor? 1 Cor. 15:9, 10.
  17. 17.Upon what did he depend for success as a minister? Eph. 3:7, 8.
  18. 18.What kept Paul from self-exaltation? 2 Cor. 12:7.
  19. 19.How did he relate himself to this infirmity? Verses 8, 9.
  20. 20.Under what discipline in the school of Christ may all workers be strong? Verse 10.

Lessons We Can Learn

  1. 1.The worker for God must have "many infallible proofs" that Jesus ever lives to make intercession for him, and will be with him always in all his labor.
  2. 2.He must receive "the power of the Holy Ghost coming upon him." Acts 1:8, margin.
  3. 3.He is then ready to be a witness of Jesus "unto the uttermost part of the earth."
  4. 4.His work in the field will confirm the efficiency of his preparation in the school of Christ.
  5. 5.The marvelous changes wrought in the lives of Peter, John, and Philip, are examples of what learning in the school of the apostles taught by Jesus, accomplished, and of what can be accomplished now if the principles of Christian education are faithfully carried out.
  6. 6.Though sincere in it all himself, Paul was wrongly educated, and consequently became a persecutor of the true people of God.
  7. 7.When Christ was revealed to Paul, he immediately began to preach Jesus, till his life was imperiled. Then he went into solitude "to be alone with God, to search his own heart, to deepen his repentance, and to prepare himself by prayer and study to engage in a work which appeared to him too great and too important for him to undertake."—"Life of Paul," page 33.
  8. 8.Paul esteemed himself the least of the apostles, and unfit to be an apostle, because of sin; but he became chief of the apostles, by the grace of God, and by rejoicing in the infirmity which kept him from self-exaltation, that the power of Christ might rest upon him.
  9. 9.Sitting at the feet of the greatest teachers of the day, even professed religious teachers, will not of itself fit young men and women for fruitful Christian service. There are some who may even array them against the true cause of God.
  10. 10.The sure way is to sit at the feet of the Master Teacher, learning in the school of Christ, as His disciples, receiving the Holy Spirit, as His apostles, then go forth everywhere preaching the Word.
