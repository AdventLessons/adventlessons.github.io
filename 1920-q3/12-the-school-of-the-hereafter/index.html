Lesson 12 · September 18, 1920

The School of the Hereafter

PDFShare

Daily Study Outline

SubjectAssignment
1.Marring the image of God in manQues. 1-3
2.Christ's part and ours in the restorationQues. 4-6
3.Education a part of the processQues. 7, 8
4.Keeping our eyes on the true aimsQues. 9-12
5.Reward of living and laboring for ChristQues. 13-17
6.Gathering knowledge in the ages to comeQues. 18-20
7.Review the lesson.

Questions

  1. 1.How was man created in the beginning in his relationship to God? Gen. 5:1; 9:6; 1 Cor. 11:7.
  2. 2.How did he lose this image of glory? Rom. 3:23; 5:12.
  3. 3.In what way did Christ come to the rescue of the sinner? Rom. 5:8.
  4. 4.What is our part in the restoration to life and glory? Rom. 6:4-8.
  5. 5.What is Christ's relation to the Father? Heb. 1:3, first part, A. R. V.
  6. 6.How is our transformation into the glorious image of God accomplished? 2 Cor. 3:18. The expression "from glory to glory" indicates a progressive change into the image of God. This suggests an educational process. As the glory of God shines forth in the study of His Word, His works, and His ways, the student, by continued beholding, is changed into the same image, reflecting the glory of his Maker, as did Moses, by intimate and prolonged communion with God.
  7. 7.How is the true educational process defined in the Scriptures? 2 Cor. 4:16.

Notes

Literally: "The inward man is being renewed day by day." "The object of true education is to restore the image of God in the soul."—Mrs. E. G. White.

  1. 8.What will this daily process in education work out for us? Verse 17.
  2. 9.On what must we keep our eyes? Why? Verse 18.
  3. 10.What objective aim in education for service should we keep in view? John 4:34, 35.
  4. 11.For what should we especially pray and educate our youth? Matt. 9:37, 38.
  5. 12.As we study to live and labor for Christ, what hope is held out before us? Titus 2:13.
  6. 13.What reward will Christ bring with Him? Matt. 16:27.
  7. 14.Can any man imagine the richness of the reward to the faithful? 1 Cor. 2:9.
  8. 15.What are some of the things that we know? 1 John 3:2; 2 Tim. 4:8; Rev. 21:4; 22:1-5; Isa. 35:1, 2, 5-10.
  9. 16.What will be the occupation of the people in Eden restored? Isa. 65:17, 21, 22; 66:22, 23.
  10. 17.What will perpetuate life and increase knowledge? Rev. 2:7; 22:2.
  11. 18.What scriptures show that the gathering of knowledge will continue throughout the ages to come? Eph. 1:17, 18; 2:6, 7.
  12. 19.What will be the measure of that accumulating knowledge? Eph. 3:17-19.
  13. 20.To whom will the redeemed give all the glory as the ages roll on? Verses 20, 21.

Lessons We Can Learn

  1. 1.Man was created in the image and likeness of God. He was clothed in the glory of God.
  2. 2.Man lost the glory of this image by sinning and coming short of the glory of God. He was left naked, ashamed, and lost.
  3. 3.Christ came to man's rescue by giving His own glorious life as a substitute, and lifted man up to sit with Him in heavenly places. Man's part is to die with Him, and rise with Him to walk in newness of life.
  4. 4.Christ is the raying-forth of His Father's glory. By beholding Him, we become changed into His likeness from glory to glory. We see the glory of God revealed as we study His Word, His works, and His ways. These should be the chief studies in our preparation school here on earth.
  5. 5.As we discover the love, wisdom, and power of God, and study and meditate upon these, their glory is reflected in our lives, and we long to go forth and reveal them to the children of men sitting in darkness. We look on the field, and offer ourselves as laborers to reap the ripening harvest, and thus to hasten the coming of the Lord of the harvest.
  6. 6.When He comes, He bestows the reward of the faithful—far beyond anything we can now comprehend.
  7. 7.The riches of God's grace and the glories of Eden restored will be the science and the song and the study of the redeemed through the ages as they roll ceaselessly on.
