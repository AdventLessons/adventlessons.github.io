Lesson 13 · April 3, 1886

Warnings of the Judgment

PDFShare
  1. 1.What announcement was made to ancient Israel when the atonement was approaching? Lev. 23:24. Read also Num. 29:1; and Lev. 25:9.
  2. 2.What prophecy speaks of an alarm to be sounded in the last days? Joel 2:1.
  3. 3.How is this message of warning foretold in Rev. 14:7, 8?
  4. 4.Tell briefly how this warning was given.—During a series of years immediately preceding the opening of the Judgment in 1844, hundreds of ministers, and some in every part of the world, went forth proclaiming that the day of God was at hand.
  5. 5.What does our Saviour say of this preaching? Matt. 24:14.
  6. 6.What questions is our Lord answering in this chapter? Verse 3.
  7. 7.What did he give as signs of his coming and of the end of the world? Verse 29.
  8. 8.How has this prediction been fulfilled?—In the year 1780 there was the most remarkable darkening of the sun and moon ever known; and in the year 1833 there was such a meteoric shower as caused many to cry out, "The Lord is coming."
  9. 9.Has the time of the Investigative Judgment been definitely foretold? Dan. 8:14, 27; 9:22-25.
  10. 10.Has the exact time of Christ's second coming been revealed? Matt. 24:36.
  11. 11.Since the signs he gave have already been seen, how sure may we be that his appearing is at hand? Verses 32, 33.
  12. 12.Will the people of the world be expecting his advent? Verses 36-39.
  13. 13.How will they receive it? Verse 30.
  14. 14.What will be done when our Lord appears? Verse 31; 1 Thess. 4:16, 17.
  15. 15.What must take place preparatory to that event?—The Investigative Judgment, or cleansing of the sanctuary. Dan. 8:14; 7:9, 10; Luke 20:35; 21:36.
  16. 16.What classes of people will God judge? Eccl. 3:17.
  17. 17.Upon which class will the work begin? 1 Pet. 4:17.
  18. 18.To whom must we all give account? 1 Pet. 4:5; Rom. 14:12.
  19. 19.What will be brought to light in that day? 1 Cor. 4:5.
  20. 20.What will God bring into judgment? Eccl. 12:14.
  21. 21.For what besides actual deeds will we have to give account? Matt. 12:36.
  22. 22.Who appears before God in behalf of penitent believers? Rom. 8:34; Heb. 7:25; 9:24; 1 John 2:1.
  23. 23.Whose sins alone, will be blotted out? Acts 3:19.
  24. 24.By what rule will our acts, thoughts, and motives be decided? Read Eccl. 12:13; Deut. 10:12, 13; Rom. 2:6-13.
