Lesson 8 · June 12, 1886

Review of Lessons 1-7

PDF
Share
  1. 1.State the circumstances under which the law was spoken from Mt. Sinai.
  2. 2.Upon what was it written?
  3. 3.What other name was given to that which was written on the tables of stone?
  4. 4.Where do you find the ten commandments recorded as the Lord spoke them?
  5. 5.Where were the commandments placed?
  6. 6.Repeat them.
  7. 7.How much of our duty do they cover?
  8. 8.Give the texts which prove that the ten commandments are the righteousness of God.
  9. 9.Then what did we conclude as to their nature?
  10. 10.What kind of character will those have who keep the commandments?
  11. 11.Prove that the law takes cognizance of what we think, as well as of what we do.
  12. 12.What is sin?
  13. 13.How much can a person do that is not right before the law will condemn him?
  14. 14.Prove that the law existed before it was spoken from Sinai.
  15. 15.Prove that it is binding on Gentiles as well as on Jews.
  16. 16.State as many arguments as you can, which show the extent of the law's jurisdiction.
  17. 17.Give the arguments by which it is proved that the commandments are the foundation of God's throne or government.
  18. 18.What does this prove as to the jurisdiction of the law?
  19. 19.Give as many proofs as you can for the perpetuity of the law.
  20. 20.What texts and arguments can you give to show that the law must exist unchanged to all eternity?
