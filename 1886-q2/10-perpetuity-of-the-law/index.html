Lesson 7 · June 5, 1886

Perpetuity of the Law

PDFShare

[Note to the Student.—Do not consider the lesson learned until you can give at least the substance of every text, with the correct reference for each. The references in black letters indicate those texts that should be committed to memory. A little diligent application each day will enable you to learn them, although this need not be considered a test of scholarship.]

  1. 1.Give as much proof as you can that the law existed before God spoke it from Sinai?
  2. 2.What relation do the ten commandments sustain to the throne of God?
  3. 3.From this, what must we conclude as to the extent of their jurisdiction?
  4. 4.How long is God's kingdom to endure? Dan. 4:3; 7:27.
  5. 5.Since God is to reign forever, and the ten commandments are the foundation of his throne or government, how long must they endure?
  6. 6.In what words does the psalmist corroborate this? Ps. 119:160.
  7. 7.To what would a change in the law be equivalent?—To a change in the government of God.
  8. 8.What strong language did Christ use concerning the law of God? Matt. 5:17, 18.
  9. 9.Can you refer to a noted prophecy that has not yet been fulfilled? Ps. 89:20-37; note especially verses 29, 36, 37.
  10. 10.How long will it take for this prophecy to be fulfilled?—As long as the days of heaven, or to all eternity.
  11. 11.Then how long will the ten commandments exist unchanged?
  12. 12.Quote a direct statement of the psalmist concerning the stability of the commandments. Ps. 111:7, 8.
  13. 13.What have we already found the ten commandments to be? Ps. 119:172.
  14. 14.Whose righteousness are they? Isa. 51:6, 7.
  15. 15.What does God say of his righteousness? Isa. 51:6, last part.
  16. 16.How long must the "righteousness of God" endure?—As long as God himself endures.
  17. 17.What does the psalmist say of the existence of God? Ps. 90:1, 2.
  18. 18.Then what must we still conclude as to the length of time the ten commandments will exist?
  19. 19.In making a direct address to the Jew, what does Paul say that he knows? Rom. 2:17, 18.
  20. 20.How does it happen that he knows God's will? Verse 18, last part.
  21. 21.Since "being instructed out of the law" causes one to know God's will, of what must the law be a statement?
  22. 22.What scripture further establishes the conclusion that the law of God is his will? Ps. 40:8.
  23. 23.Is God's will changeable? James 1:17.
  24. 24.In view of these different lines of argument, what must we conclude in regard to the law of God? Luke 16:17.

Notes

Christ said that "not one jot or tittle should pass from the law till all be fulfilled." Matt. 5:18. From the preceding verse it appears that he had reference to the prophets, meaning that nothing should pass from the law until all the sayings of the prophets were fulfilled. In Ps. 89:20-37 we have a prophecy concerning Christ. To fulfill this prophecy will require all "the days of heaven," that is, all eternity. Therefore Christ's words are equivalent to the statement that the law will exist unchanged to all eternity.
