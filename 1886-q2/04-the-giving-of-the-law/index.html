Lesson 1 · April 24, 1886

The Giving of the Law

PDFShare
  1. 1.To what place did the Israelites come about two months after they left Egypt? Ex. 19:1.
  2. 2.What did the Lord say he would do on the third day afterward? Ex. 19:11.
  3. 3.What preparation were the people required to make for this event? Ex. 19:10, 11.
  4. 4.What precaution was taken to keep the people from touching the mountain? Ex. 19:12, 23.
  5. 5.What was said would be the result if any one should touch it? Ex. 19:12, 13, 21.
  6. 6.What dreadful things did the people hear and see on the third day? Ex. 19:16.
  7. 7.What caused these things? Ex. 19:20.
  8. 8.How was the mountain affected by the presence of the Lord? Ex. 19:18.
  9. 9.Who attended the Lord on this occasion? Ps. 68:17.
  10. 10.For what purpose did the Lord thus come down on Mt. Sinai? Nehemiah 9:13; Deut. 33:2.
  11. 11.Where do we find that which the Lord spoke from Sinai? Ex. 20:1-17.
  12. 12.When the Lord had spoken these words, what did the people do? Ex. 20:18.
  13. 13.What request did they make? Ex. 20:19; Deut. 5:24-27; Heb. 12:19.
  14. 14.Did the Lord grant their request? Deut. 5:28.
  15. 15.In what manner did he give them subsequent instruction? Deut. 5:30, 31.
