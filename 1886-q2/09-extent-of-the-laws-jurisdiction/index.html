Lesson 6 · May 29, 1886

Extent of the Law's Jurisdiction

PDFShare

(Continued.)

[Note to the Student.—Do not consider the lesson learned until you can give at least the substance of every text, with the correct reference for each. The references in black letters indicate those texts that should be committed to memory. A little diligent application each day will enable you to learn them, although this need not be considered a test of scholarship.]

  1. 1.Did the law exist before it was spoken from Sinai? Give proof.
  2. 2.Can you prove that its claims are binding on Gentiles as well as on Jews?
  3. 3.Over what part of the earth has the law jurisdiction? Proof.
  4. 4.How many apartments were there in the tabernacle built by Moses? Ex. 26:33.
  5. 5.What article of furniture was in the most holy place? Ex. 26:34.
  6. 6.Why was it called the "ark of the testimony"? Ex. 25:16.
  7. 7.What was this "testimony"? Ex. 31:18; Deut. 10:4, 5.
  8. 8.Of what was this earthly tabernacle a pattern? Heb. 9:8, 9, 23, 24.
  9. 9.Was the furniture of the tabernacle, as well as the tabernacle itself, made according to this heavenly pattern? Ex. 25:9, 40.
  10. 10.Is there a real temple in heaven? Heb. 8:1, 2.
  11. 11.What article of furniture is specially noted as having been seen in it? Rev. 11:19.
  12. 12.Since the ark was called the "ark of the testimony" because it contained the tables of the law, what must be in the ark in the real temple in heaven? (See note.)
  13. 13.Then what relation did the tables which Moses placed in the ark bear to those in the temple in heaven? (See note.)
  14. 14.Where is the Lord's throne? Ps. 11:4; Hab. 2:20.
  15. 15.Between what beings is his seat? Ps. 80:1; 99:1.
  16. 16.In the earthly tabernacle, from between what figures did the Lord speak to the people? Ex. 25:22.
  17. 17.Then of what was the ark in the earthly sanctuary a representation?—Of the throne of God in the temple in heaven.
  18. 18.What was within the ark, and underneath the cherubim? Ex. 25:16, 22; Rev. 11:19.
  19. 19.Then what relation do the commandments sustain to the throne of God?—They are underneath it, forming its foundation.
  20. 20.Can you quote any texts of Scripture that directly support this conclusion? Ps. 89:14; 97:2. Compare Ps. 119:172.
  21. 21.How extensive is the authority of God's throne? Ps. 103:19.
  22. 22.Then how extensive must be the jurisdiction of the ten commandments?
  23. 23.Must they not, then, be the rule of conduct in heaven as well as on earth? Ps. 103:20.

Notes

In Ex. 31:18 we read that the Lord "gave unto Moses, when he had made an end of communing with him upon Mount Sinai, two tables of testimony, tables of stone, written with the finger of God." In Deut. 5:22 we learn that it was the ten commandments that were written on the two tables of stone; and from Deut. 10:4, 5 we learn that these tables were placed in the ark. Therefore we know that the ten commandments are the "testimony;" and when John says (Rev. 11:19) that in the temple of God in heaven he saw the "ark of his testimony," we must conclude that the ten commandments are in the ark. And, further, since the earthly tabernacle and its furniture were "patterns of things in the heavens" (Ex. 25:9, 40; Heb. 9:23, 24), we must also conclude that the ten commandments which were on the tables of stone in the tabernacle which Moses built, were but a copy of the ten commandments in the temple in heaven.
