Lesson 15 · April 17, 1886

The 2300 Days and the Judgment

PDFShare

(Review.)

  1. 1.What vision had Daniel in the third year of Belshazzar's reign? Read Dan. 8:1-14.
  2. 2.In connection with this vision, what part had Gabriel to act? Verses 15-27.
  3. 3.What hindered the completion of his work? Verse 27.
  4. 4.What part of the vision was left unexplained?
  5. 5.When did he return to complete this task? Dan. 9:1, 2, 20-22.
  6. 6.What assurance did the angel give Daniel, and what did he request him to do? Verse 23.
  7. 7.How did Gabriel then take up the unexplained portion of the former vision? Verse 24.
  8. 8.How did he give Daniel a starting point for the 2300 days? Verse 25.
  9. 9.Explain the triple nature of this commandment. Read Ezra 1; 4; 5; 6; 7.
  10. 10.How may we know that the Lord regarded the commandment as threefold in its nature? Ezra 6:14.
  11. 11.When was the decree issued that made this commandment complete? Ezra 7, margin.
  12. 12.How may we know that the date of the seventy weeks is also the date of the 2300 days? See definition of the word rendered determined.
  13. 13.Since the prophetic period of 2300 days began B. C. 457, when would it end?
  14. 14.What was to take place at that time? Dan. 8:14.
  15. 15.Show that the cleansing of the sanctuary must include an examination of the records, and a decision of every man's case. Dan. 7:9, 10; Rev. 20:12; Luke 20:35; 21:36.
  16. 16.Who presides at this awful tribunal? Dan. 7:9.
  17. 17.Who appears as an advocate for all faithful believers? 1 John 2:1; Heb. 9:24.
  18. 18.How, then, can it be said that "The Father judgeth no man, but hath committed all judgment unto the Son"?—This has reference to the Executive Judgment. See John 5:22, 26, 27.
  19. 19.When will this Executive Judgment take place? Verses 28, 29; Rev. 20:5; 1 Thess. 4:16; 2 Thess. 1:7-10; 2:8; Jude 14, 15.
  20. 20.Who will assist Christ in the work of judging wicked men and angels? 1 Cor. 6:2, 3.
  21. 21.When will this work of judgment be carried on? Rev. 20:4.
  22. 22.What will take place when the thousand years are finished? Verses 7, 8.
  23. 23.What will be the fate of Satan and his followers? Verses 9, 10.
  24. 24.Why was it to be expected that mankind would be warned of the approach of the judgment? Lev. 23:24; Joel 2:1; Rev. 14:6, 7.
  25. 25.How was this expectation fulfilled?
  26. 26.Is it the purpose of God that this warning voice should continue after the cleansing of the sanctuary begins? Rev. 14:8, 9, 10.
  27. 27.How long will this warning be proclaimed?—Till the work is finished in the most holy place of the heavenly sanctuary.
  28. 28.When asked, "What shall be the sign of thy coming, and of the end of the world," what signs did our Saviour give? Matt. 24:29-33.
  29. 29.How and when have these predictions been fulfilled?
